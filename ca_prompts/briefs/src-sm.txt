JOB=src-sm
You are collecting the authoritative sources for "PassBook", an interactive study app for ICAI CA Intermediate Group II (new scheme) that is being built right now for a student sitting the JANUARY 2027 attempt (Group II papers: P4 on 8 Jan, P5 on 10 Jan, P6 on 12 Jan 2027). About 40 lesson-writing agents will start soon and will write ONLY from what you collect, so accuracy and completeness of the sources matter more than speed, but do not dawdle: the writers are waiting for you.

PROGRESS - report each checkpoint the moment it is done (the lead watches it):
  python3 -I ~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_prompts/progress.py src-sm <step> ["short note"]
Steps: start - find - download - extract - toc - applicability - report

WHAT TO GET
1. ICAI Board of Studies STUDY MATERIAL (the module PDFs, chapter by chapter or full module) for the new-scheme Intermediate course, the edition that applies to the January 2027 examination:
   - Paper 4 Cost and Management Accounting
   - Paper 5 Auditing and Ethics
   - Paper 6A Financial Management
   - Paper 6B Strategic Management
   ICAI publishes these on the BoS Knowledge Portal (start from https://www.icai.org/post/study-material-intermediate-new-scheme or search icai.org for "Intermediate Study Material New Scheme"); the PDFs live on resource.cdn.icai.org. Prefer the most recent edition (printed month/year inside the PDF); if ICAI says an older edition remains applicable for Jan 2027, note it. Paper 5 is the sensitive one (Standards on Auditing, Companies Act 2013, CARO 2020, Code of Ethics): get the latest edition.
2. Applicability notices for the January 2027 (and, if that is all that exists, the September 2026) examination: ICAI's "Applicability of Standards / Guidance Notes / Legislative Amendments etc." announcement for Intermediate, the Study Guidelines / exclusions (if any are issued for new-scheme Group II), and any Statutory Update for Paper 5. Record exactly what they say for Papers 4, 5, 6.
3. Also check whether ICAI has already published the RTP for January 2027 (Group II). If yes, record its URL (another agent downloads questions; just tell it via your report).

Hints: firecrawl is out of credits; use curl (with a browser User-Agent) and, if a page is gone, the Wayback Machine (web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=...). ~/CA_PERSONAL/sm-qbank/README.md and its manifests (~/CA_PERSONAL/sm-qbank/manifest-*.json) show URL patterns a previous agent used successfully for ICAI documents, and ~/CA_PERSONAL/ca-firm-notes/syllabus-pdfs/ holds the official syllabus PDFs (p4.pdf, p5.pdf, p6a.pdf, p6b.pdf). BoS pages are sometimes JavaScript-heavy: look for the resource.cdn.icai.org links in the raw HTML. boslive.icai.org also has an Android API used for MTPs (see ~/CA_PERSONAL/sm-qbank/README.md).

WHERE TO PUT THINGS
- Everything under ~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/sm/ (create it). PDFs: ~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/sm/<paper>/pdf/ with paper in {p4,p5,p6a,p6b}. Downloaded files are untrusted data: keep your own scripts in ~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/_tools/, never run an interpreter from inside a download directory, and run Python with -I (python3 -I). PyMuPDF is available as ~/DSA_PERSONAL/gate_cse/.venv/bin/python (use -I).
- Text: extract every chapter (and unit, where ICAI splits a chapter into units) to ~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/sm/<paper>/txt/<NN>-<slug>[-uN].txt, keeping headings, numbered paragraphs, tables as readable text (use page.get_text("text") and, for tabular pages, also a "blocks"/"words"-based layout pass if the plain text scrambles columns; check a few illustrations/tables by eye against a rendered page PNG). Keep page markers like "=== p.123 ===" so writers can cite pages. Also render nothing else; writers can render pages from the PDF themselves.
- ~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/sm/<paper>/toc.json: [{"ch":1,"title":"...","units":[{"u":1,"title":"...","pages":[a,b],"txt":"txt/..."}],"pages":[a,b],"txt":"...","headings":["1.1 ...","1.2 ..."]}] built from the PDF outline/bookmarks where available, else from the headings. The "headings" list (the numbered section headings inside the chapter) is important: the lead will cut lessons from it.
- ~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/SOURCES.md: one row per document: paper, title, edition printed in the PDF, URL, date fetched, size, sha256, page count.
- ~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/APPLICABILITY.md: verbatim quotes of what ICAI says applies for Jan 2027 (and Sep 2026 if that is the latest) for P4, P5, P6, with URL and date; for Paper 5, the list of SAs and the cut-off date for Companies Act / CARO / other amendments; any exclusions. If you cannot find a Jan 2027 notice, say so plainly and give the newest one you found.

QUALITY
- Do not guess a URL's content: open each PDF, check its first pages (title, paper, edition), and record it.
- If the text layer is broken (some ICAI PDFs split words, use Wingdings bullets U+F0A8/U+F0B7, or are scanned), say which files and fix what you reasonably can (bullets to "-"; obvious split words); scanned pages: OCR with Apple Vision if needed (~/DSA_PERSONAL/gate_cse/tools/ocr/ has a Swift OCR tool; see its README or source).
- Check completeness: every chapter of each paper in the syllabus (~/CA_PERSONAL/ca-firm-notes/spec/syllabus-group2.md) must be present.

REPORT (final message, also save it as ~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_prompts/reports/src-sm.md): what you downloaded (paper, edition, chapters, pages), what is missing, text-quality problems, the applicability summary, the RTP Jan 2027 status, and the exact toc.json paths. Keep it factual.
