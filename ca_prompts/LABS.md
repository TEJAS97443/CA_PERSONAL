# PassBook labs — catalogue (31 labs, 10 lab jobs)

A lab is a step-through animated simulator (see `docs/LAB_GUIDE.md` in ~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_passbook): the student changes the inputs, presses play,
and watches the working build up frame by frame, with a caption explaining *why* each step happens; a challenge mode generates fresh
ICAI-style problems and checks the answer. Labs are the "animations" of PassBook: make them feel alive (many small frames, each
adding one line of working, one bar, one branch), but every frame must be correct.

Every lab: verified against (a) the illustrations / examples in the ICAI Study Material chapter (`~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/sm/<paper>/txt/`), (b) at
least two real ICAI questions with their Suggested Answers (`~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/q/`), (c) an invariant or brute-force check on random inputs
(e.g. variances add up to the total variance; reconciliation closes; NPV at IRR is 0). Conventions (rounding, FIFO vs weighted average,
which base) are controls or stated in `note`. Presets: at least 3, one taken from a real ICAI question (cite it in the label, e.g.
"RTP May 2025 Q3 (data)"), the others with the PassBook cast (Tapri Tea Co. Ltd; see the content guide §2a).

Column "embed in" lists the lessons that should carry the lab later (the lab owner adds the embeds after the lessons exist; see the brief).
