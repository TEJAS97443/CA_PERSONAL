## Things in the guide or tooling that got in the way
1. **Word limits disagree.** `build.py --check` warns only above 5,200 words; CONTENT_GUIDE §1 says 900–4,500.
2. **Table headers.** The renderer does not upper-case a header cell that contains "(", an apostrophe, a slash or digits, so headers like "Why (SM 3.39–3.40)" look different from their neighbours. I moved citations out of headers. The "Yes / No / N.A." headers in the questionnaire formats still look mixed-case.
3. **Flow labels.** A flow node label of three lines spills out of its box. I kept every label to two lines.
4. **Loops in flows.** A loop edge (back edge) in a flow is drawn from the wrong node and reads as a different loop. I removed the one I had.
5. **Seq diagrams on phones.** At 390 px a three-lane seq still needs sideways scrolling ("Scroll sideways or click to enlarge"), even with short labels.
6. **Full build blocked.** My own run of `build.py` failed on someone else's lesson (cma.svc.others.md, a KaTeX error: "Can't use function '$' in math mode"), so it wrote no data. Another agent's successful build at 05:38 included my files, so `tools/shot.sh` phone screenshots still worked.
