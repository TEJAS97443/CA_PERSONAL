## Things in the guide or tooling that got in the way
- **Wide formulas on phones.** A display formula wider than about 36 characters scrolls sideways at 390 px. `\qquad` only helps between parts; a single long fraction cannot wrap. I shortened labels (SDP, VOH, C × w), defined them in words, and rechecked with phone screenshots.
- **Mid-task rule change.** The lead said flows must be vertical and a two-route comparison should be a table. The multiproduct "units or values?" flow had two parallel branches, so I turned it into a table. All three remaining flows have `"w": 420`.
- **Lint and position colours.** The linter counts a flow's position colours (it includes sky blue, `e`), so a caption saying "Blue:" was flagged; I reworded the caption.
- **Dark previews overwrite light ones.** `preview_lesson.py --dark` writes to the same file names as the light preview, so the light slices are lost.
- **Not a blocker:** on phones the app shows Practice before the folded Under the hood section.
- **Word range:** after the rule change to 900–4,500, nothing was cut. I added back ICAI's full option list and the 3Es to the decisions answer frame (SM 14.32–14.33).
