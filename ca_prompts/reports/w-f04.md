## What got in the way (guide and tooling)
- **Flow width:** a flow `process` node draws far wider than its label, so a flow at `"w": 400` still lays out wider than 390 px and scrolls sideways on a phone. I shortened the labels; that helps only a little.
- **Plot tick labels:** x-axis tick labels on a bar plot are dropped at phone width when they are long (in the WACC lesson "equity" vanished). I shortened them to "RE", "equity", "pref.", "debt" and explained them in the caption.
- **Previews:** `preview_lesson.py --dark` writes to the same /tmp/preview/<id>-N.png names as the light run, and the folder is shared by every agent, so files get overwritten between runs.
- **Phone screenshots:** `shot.sh` captures answer folds closed, so tables inside worked answers cannot be checked at phone width. I relied on the 900 px previews and on keeping tables to 5 columns or fewer. I also turned one 6-column EPS table into a vertical one.
- **Rule change mid-task:** I followed the lead's change (words 900–4,500; flows `"w"` 400–450). No ICAI list or format was shortened to fit the word count.
