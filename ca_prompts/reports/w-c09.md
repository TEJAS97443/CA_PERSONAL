## Guide and tooling notes
- **Forks:** I could not split the work across forks because the concurrent-subagent limit (20) was already reached. I wrote all five lessons myself.
- **`flow` puts all source nodes in one row.** The SM 7.9 flowchart (three accounts feeding WIP, others feeding sideways) came out about 700 px wide, so I used a `graph` with manual coordinates. A flow option to place a feeder beside its target would help.
- **Edge labels on vertical `flow` edges sit on top of the arrow,** and a three-line `end` node overflowed. I worked around both with `io` nodes and shorter labels.
- **On phones, tables with three or more columns become cards.** That makes long journal and driver tables very long on phones.
- **In `::: cols` accounts the Dr and Cr totals do not line up** when the two sides have different numbers of rows. ICAI prints the totals on the same line.
- **Phone screenshots:** `tools/shot.sh` at the documented height of 4000 shows only the top of a lesson. I used a height of 16000 and sliced the image with PIL.
- **Table header case:** headers containing "(₹)" or a non-ASCII letter ("Café") are not uppercased like the other headers.
