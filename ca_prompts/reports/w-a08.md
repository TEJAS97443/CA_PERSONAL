## Things in the guide or tooling that got in the way
1. **Flow layout drops a skip-layer edge.** A decision whose "no" edge goes to a node two layers down (past its "yes" child) is drawn straight down behind the intermediate node, so the edge looks missing. I hit this in the SA 450 flow and worked around it with a "Plan stands" node so both branches sit on one layer. The linter does not catch it.
2. **Flow end nodes fit two lines.** A three-line label overflows the rounded box. I hit this in the SA 210 change-of-terms flow and shortened the label.
3. **Seq lane boxes are clipped on phones.** On the 390 px phone view, a lane box wider than its lane is cut off at the left edge of the scroll area: the left edge of the box is lost even after scrolling. I shortened the SA 260 lane texts. Desktop previews do not show this.
4. **Nest diagrams shrink to fit.** A nest scales its whole drawing down on phones, so long lines make tiny text. I shortened the SA 580 nest lines and set `w` to 360.
5. **The word ceiling is tight for these Standards.** 5,200 words is tight for a lesson that must reproduce ICAI's letters, or that covers two Standards (SA 260 + SA 265), and still carries 2–3 full worked examples and the Exam angle. Maybe a per-lesson override for such lessons.
6. **No tooling problems** beyond these. One note: `preview_lesson.py` now also writes a `-full.png`, which made cropping easy.
