## Originality checks (lead's note)
- I avoided ICAI's recurring fact patterns or gave them new facts and a different twist:
  - Inherited shares (SM TYU 3, RTP Jan 2025) and the 40% or 65% fee dependence (TYU 4, Sep 2024, MTP Jan 2025 ×2).
  - Book-keeping for an audit client (TYU 5, RTPs) and relying only on a representation letter for tax appeals (TYU 6, RTP Sep 2025).
  - NIA/ED raids (TYU 9, Sep 2025) and the fintech competence case (TYU 10).
  - Management pressing for the report before the EQCR is complete (Jan 2026 Q1(a)), and assembly late or retention for 4 years (May 2024, May 2025).
  - A sister or brother-in-law as the objectivity case (May 2024, RTP Jan 2026), and the predecessor auditor who does not reply.
- Two quiz items were too close and were replaced:
  - "An auditor who deals in shares is which threat?" read like September 2025 Q6(c)(iii). It is now a partner urging TV viewers to buy her client's shares.
  - A listed client with a low fee and no EQCR overlapped with my own worked example. It is now an unlisted company caught by the firm's own EQCR criteria, where the partner wants to date the report early.
- The skepticism example ("Bunty's two swings") was built to differ from SA 200's quiz, which used a CFO with six years of honesty and a single photocopy. Mine combines too much doubt (insisting all 400 rent agreements are notarised) with contradicting evidence (cold-store temperature logs) and over-generalising from two cafés.
