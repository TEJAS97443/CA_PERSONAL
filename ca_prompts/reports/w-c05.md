# w-c05 report: four Material Cost lessons (Paper 4)

All four files lint clean, have no `build.py --check` warnings, and were previewed in light and dark and screenshotted at 390 px (`tools/shot.sh`). Every number was computed with scripts in `$TMPDIR/wc05/calc/`. Word counts below are the build's count (everything except flashcards).

The updated guide rules (labelled links, flow `"w": 420`, flow labels of at most 2 lines, `::: cols` for two-sided accounts, the 900–4,500 word range) are applied to all four.

- **Study Material pages:** 2.21, 2.23–2.27 (re-order quantity, EOQ, ordering and carrying cost, formula, assumptions, Illustrations 3–4, maximum level "ROQ may be EOQ"), 2.30–2.31, 2.79–2.82 and answers 2.85–2.93 (Practical Problems 1, 2, 3, 5, 7), 2.83–2.84 and 2.97–2.100 (the perishable case scenario); FM Study Material 9.69.
- **ICAI documents read (new scheme, P4):** pyq-may2024-q/a (Q1(a)); pyq-sep2025-q/a (Q5(b)); pyq-sep2026-q (Q1(b), answers not yet published); pyq-jan2026-a (MCQs 6–10, all re-computed); pyq-sep2024-a (MCQ 12); pyq-jan2025-a (MCQ 15); rtp-may2025 (Q8); rtp-sep2025 (Q7); rtp-sep2026 (Q5); mtp-sep2024-s1-q/a (MCQs 1–3); mtp-sep2026-s1-q/a (MCQs 6–10); mtp-jan2026-s2-q/a (Q1(c)); mtp-jan2026-s1-q/a (Q6(b)).
- **Old scheme:** pyq-nov2022-a (P3 Q1(c)).
- **Facts to check:**
  - **Rounding up the number of orders.** I teach it as ICAI's practice, citing SM 2.90 ("cannot be in decimals"), RTP September 2026 and MTP September 2026 Series I. Illustration 4 never needed it.
  - **Safety stock in the discount comparison.** It is left out of carrying cost "as in ICAI's May 2024 suggested answer". Strictly, its carrying cost differs by a few rupees at the discounted price.
  - **The "flat bottom" figures** (±20% from EOQ costs +2.5% / +1.7%; half or double costs +25%) and **EOQ ∝ 1/√price** are maths, not SM text. The second is checked against SM Illustration 4.
- **Neighbours:** stock levels, the safety stock and stock-out are linked to cma.mat.levels, not repeated.

## What got in my way
- **`::: cols` titles are not drawn.** In `app/js/core/md.js` the `cols` case ignores its title, so the account name from `::: cols Abnormal Scrap Account` does not show. I added a lead-in sentence naming the account; if the renderer starts drawing titles, the name will show twice.
- **Flow nodes fit only two lines.** Three-line labels spill out of the box (seen in preview), so I kept every flow label to two lines.
- **Long fractions do not wrap at 390 px.** `\qquad` wraps sums, but long `\text{}` fractions still scroll sideways, so I rewrote them with symbols defined underneath.
- **`shot.sh` captures folded answers closed.** I could not check worked-answer tables at phone width.
- **`/tmp/preview` is shared by all agents**, so files were overwritten between runs.
- **The word limit changed mid-task** (3,600 to 4,500); see lesson 2.
