## What got in the way
- **seq diagrams default to 480 px wide** (`max(480, lanes × 150)`), so even a 3-lane seq scrolls sideways on a phone. An explicit `"w": 360` fixes it. Labels longer than about 16 characters on a lane box spill over (a box is 104 px), and a note spanning all lanes must stay under about 50 characters. The guide's phone notes cover flows and graphs but not seq; a line there would help.
- **Graph nodes with `"shape": "square"`** have a fixed size and do not grow with the label. `"r": 34` plus 2-line labels of about 8 characters made them fit.
- **nest boxes** are sized by their longest line and then scaled down to the phone width, so lines of 40+ characters made the text tiny. Wrapping lines at about 32 characters kept it readable. Leading spaces in nest `lines` are stripped, so continuation lines cannot be indented.
- **A 3-column markdown table of parallel lists** (long, medium and short-term sources) turns into stacked cards on phones, which mixes up the columns. I replaced it with a nest.
- **A 4-column table inside a worked-example answer** scrolls on a phone because the fold adds padding. I transposed it to 3 short columns.
- **`US$` is read as maths** by the linter; it has to be written `US\$`.
- `build.py --check` briefly failed on another writer's lesson (`cma.intro.classify`, a bad link `fm.capb.npv`). It cleared on a later run.
