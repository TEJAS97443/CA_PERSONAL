## aud.report.sa700: 11 fixes

1. **Incomplete ICAI list (qualitative aspects, SM 8.5–8.6, para 1.3.1).** The lesson gave only points 3 and 4 of ICAI's four points. I added point 1 (management makes a number of judgements about amounts and disclosures) and point 2 (SA 260 (Revised) discusses the qualitative aspects of accounting practices). I also restructured the section as ICAI's numbered list, with the two lack-of-neutrality indicators nested under point 3. ICAI's old MTP Nov 2019 answer to this question gives all four points.
2. **Specific evaluation (e) was trimmed (SM 8.6).** It now uses ICAI's full wording: "...provide adequate disclosures to enable the intended users to understand the effect of material transactions and events on the information conveyed in the financial statements".
3. **Lakshmi said "subject to" is "the one phrase" a clean opinion never uses.** That is wrong: SM 8.11 names two phrases ("with the foregoing explanation" and "subject to"). Changed to "a phrase".
4. **Opinion phrases (SM 8.11).** Added "unless law or regulation requires otherwise". The Sep 2024 Q4(c) Suggested Answer includes it.
5. **Date of the report (SM 8.22).** Added the missing SM sentence: the date tells users that the auditor has considered the effect of events and transactions of which the auditor became aware and that occurred up to that date.
6. **UDIN moved from Under the hood (Deep only) into How it works (the Exam view).** RTP Jan 2025 Q17 asks for its objective and significance, so it belongs in the Exam view. I also added SM 8.100's "helps secure documents issued by practising CAs".
7. **Reasonable assurance in In plain words.** Changed "high, but not absolute" to SM 8.17's "high, but not a guarantee".
8. **Nest-diagram caption.** It called only the blue box "the part SA 700 requires", but signature, place and date are SA 700 elements too. The caption now names the three boxes accurately and says the diagram is "based on" (not "as in") ICAI's illustration.
9. **Recap.** Added "with the foregoing explanation" next to "subject to".
10. **Missing neighbour links.** Added SA 570 (`aud.complete.sa570`) and SA 701 in the elements table.
11. **Possible confusion between the format and the case.** ICAI's own Opinion wording says "according to the explanations given to us", yet the lesson teaches that "subject to the explanations" is wrong. I added one line saying ICAI's phrase is not a "subject to" and the opinion stays unconditional.

Other changes:

- To make room under the 4,500 cap I tightened prose, but cut no ICAI list items. The Under the hood minimum elements (a)–(m) are still all present and in ICAI order, with some items reworded more compactly.
- Example 2's analysis now reads "Neither indicator is itself a misstatement".
- The nested list renders correctly; I checked it in the preview.

## aud.report.sa701: 9 fixes

1. **Cast canon contradicted.** The lesson said "Tapri Tea Co. Ltd is now listed, and its first AGM as a listed company". The guide's canon is listed on NSE and BSE since 2024, so a 2025-26 audit cannot be its first AGM as a listed company. Fixed.
2. **Dialogue format.** `(whispering)` is now in italics after the colon, as the guide requires.
3. **Overclaim in Under the hood.** "So one matter never appears twice" is now "a matter determined to be a key audit matter does not also get an Emphasis of Matter or Other Matter paragraph". That is exactly what SM 8.49 and 8.51 support.
4. **Quiz Q1 explanation ignored the Emphasis of Matter distractor (option D).** It now says an EOM paragraph is not a substitute for SA 570 reporting either (SM 8.50).
5. **Format text altered inside ICAI's brackets.** I restored the exact SM 8.27 wording "[Description of each key audit matter in accordance with SA 701.]" and put the subheading rule (SM 8.53) on a separate line.
6. **Worked example 1.** It said matters 4 and 5 did not need significant attention but gave no reason. It now gives one: an ₹18,000 petty cash deficiency, and a routine scope and timing communication.
7. **Worked example 2(b).** Added the KAM-section wording from SM 8.54: "Except for the matter described in the Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern section". The question now says "taking each on its own", so the separate "Except for" sentences in (a) and (b) do not contradict each other.
8. **Missing SA 570 link** in the "not a substitute" list.
9. **The `seq` diagram overflowed at phone width.** It defaulted to 480 px and clipped the Users lane. It now has `"w": 400` and fits at 390 px.

"The one exception" is now "The exception" (wording only).

## Left uncertain (not changed)

- **SA 700 example 1 and quiz Q2: is the board's approval "those with the recognised authority"?** I kept the writer's reading that the board's approval is when those with the recognised authority take responsibility. SM 8.22 does not name the board, but SM 8.16–8.17 and 8.28 make the Board of Directors responsible for preparing the financial statements. It is a reasonable reading, but it is an inference.
- **SA 701: what each KAM description must contain, and the exclusion of a qualified matter from the KAM section.** The SM covers neither, beyond the bracketed "Except for..." wording on SM 8.54. The lesson stays within that wording.
- **SA 701: where the KAM section sits.** The lesson says it "comes directly after the Basis for Opinion" in ICAI's illustration (SM 8.27). That is true of that illustration only. In the element list (SM 8.9), Going Concern comes between Basis for Opinion and Key Audit Matters.
- **SA 700 word count.** It sits at 4,497 of 4,500. Anyone who adds content will have to trim elsewhere.
