## Facts I am less than certain about (with the page checked)

1. **Strategic vs operational planning.** ICAI's RTP answers (May 2025 and September 2026, Q16) add a row: operational planning "is concerned with the short-term success". The Study Material's figure has no such row; I rendered SM 5.10 to check. The lesson gives the Study Material's 6 + 5 items and mentions ICAI's extra row in the Exam angle.
2. **Transactional leadership setting.** The main text says "in mature industries" (SM 5.56). The chapter's own answer says "in growing or mature industries" (SM 5.78). The lesson teaches the main text and points out the difference neutrally in Under the hood.
3. **Narayana Murthy's name.** I followed the Study Material's spelling, "N. R. Narayan Murthy" (SM 5.52).
4. **SBU benefits and "imperative".** The six benefits come from the chapter's own answers (SM 5.74–5.75), not the main text. So does the line that an SBU structure becomes imperative as divisions grow in number, size and diversity (SM 5.69). The lesson labels them as coming from the chapter's answers.
5. **How ICAI keys structure MCQs.** Three MCQs describe "independent product centres … responsible for profitability" and all three are keyed SBU, not divisional or multidivisional: Sep 2024 MCQ 14 (D), May 2025 MCQ 16 (C), RTP May 2025 MCQ 2 (d). RTP May 2026 MCQ 4 keys a semi-autonomous segment as SBU (c). The lesson teaches this as ICAI's keying.
6. **An MCQ that mixes two lists.** MTP May 2026 Series I MCQ 1(B)(iii), key (c), words Lewin's refreeze as "institutionalizing new norms", borrowing a word from the steps to initiate strategic change. The lesson says so neutrally.
7. **Pearce/Robinson timeline.** In the strategic control table, surveillance and premise control run from Time 1, while special alert and implementation control run from Time 2. That is my reading of the figure on SM 5.59, which I rendered.
8. **My own interpretation in a model answer.** In the Tapri OS Lewin example (sm.impl.digital, worked example 1), I place the app-linked bonuses under compliance "applied while refreezing". Under the hood also says internalised behaviour is easiest to refreeze. The Study Material does not say either of these. A reviewer may want to simplify that part.
9. **Hourglass drawing.** The Study Material draws it as two curves ("wide at the top, narrow at the middle, widest at the bottom", SM 5.46). I drew it as a graph: 3 top nodes, 1 middle node, 5 bottom nodes, with dashed IT links.

## What got in the way (guide and tooling)

- **Word limit.** The guide says 900–5,200 words. While I was working, the build's limit changed to 900–6,000; it now prints "(900, 6000)". I kept to 5,200. sm.impl.structure needed several rounds of cutting to fit, and is complete but tight.
- **Wide figures on phones.** A figure scrolls sideways on a phone when its drawn width is more than about 432 px: fit.js keeps at least 80% of the drawn width, and the column is about 345 px. The guide says only "w about 450 or less", which is not tight enough.
  - Two side-by-side multi-line process nodes in a flow, or long seq labels, push the width over the limit. A long node label also pushes the back-edge line into the boxes.
  - I fixed every case by shortening labels.
  - Suggestion: state the ≤ 430 px rule in §9 of the guide, and measure a multi-line node by its longest line, not the whole string.
- **Missing diagram types.** `layers` cannot vary layer widths, so a funnel or hourglass shape is impossible. A tree node's `note` is drawn across the edges below it. I removed those notes.
- **Card warning.** `build.py --check` warned "1 card removed" after I trimmed a card from a lesson that had never been built. It is harmless.
- **Subagent limit.** Only one research subagent could start because the concurrent-subagent limit (20) was reached. I did the question research for four of the lessons myself.
- **No old-scheme Paper 6B in sources.** `~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/q/old/p6` is old Paper 6, Auditing. There is no old-scheme Strategic Management folder, so old-scheme examples were not available.
