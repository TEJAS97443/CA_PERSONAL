## Facts I am less than certain about (please check)
1. **SM 1.25 has a word missing.** The second way to address the agency problem is printed "Employee is also designed to address the issue…". I rendered the PDF page: it really is printed like that, and every ICAI answer copies it. The lesson quotes it exactly and adds a neutral `note`: read in context it describes employee rewards tied to the share price, with an employee stock option scheme as the usual example. That reading is mine.
2. **What the four agency-cost types mean.** The Study Material names them (monitoring, bonding, opportunity, structuring) but never explains them; no ICAI source I found does either. The lesson gives the usual finance-text meanings in Under the hood only, clearly labelled as not from the Study Material. Model answers name the types without explaining them.
3. **Value creation** ("sustainable returns exceeding the cost of capital") comes from the Suggested Answer to MTP Series II, September 2025, Q4(a), and is cited as such. The Study Material only puts "Wealth maximisation / value creation" under one heading (1.14).
4. **"Emerging issues for the CFO"** (nine items) comes from RTP January 2025 Q11(a). It is not in the Study Material edition that applies for January 2027, and the lesson says so.
5. **"Financial management environment"** is syllabus item (d) (initial pages, p.302), but the Study Material has no section with that title. Under that name, `fm.intro.agency` teaches §11 (related disciplines, financial management against accounting), the stakeholder supervision in §8 (1.16) and the liberalisation context (1.18).
6. **Inventory policy.** The Study Material places it under the investment decision (1.8). The classification example (role, part e) answers "investment" and adds that day-to-day running is working capital management.
7. **Hedging** is classed as a financing decision, following 1.9 (role example, part b).
8. **September 2026 paper:** the OCR lost the question number, so the finance-executive question is cited without "Q4(c)" (its Suggested Answers are not yet published).
9. **Exam-angle counts** (22, 14 and 14) are my tally of the documents listed above.
10. **Originality:** the kulhad case shares a theme with Study Material theory question 7 (regulation against wealth maximisation), but the facts, the question and the twist are new. I replaced two draft quiz questions that were near-copies of Study Material MCQs 3 and 12.

## What got in my way (guide and tooling)
- **Table headers:** short ones render in capitals and long ones don't, so one table can mix both, e.g. "What a CFO used to do" (cosmetic).
- **`nest`:** with side arrows on both sides, the diagram grows wider than its `w` and scrolls on a phone. Putting both arrows on the left fixed it.
- **`graph`:** two opposite edges with the default curve put their labels on top of each other; it needed a curve of about 0.4.
- **`layers`:** a long `note` runs out of the box and into a `side` arrow.
- **`flow`:** at w 400 with long node labels it scrolled at 390 px; w 360 with shorter labels fixed it.
- **`shot.sh`:** with folds open these lessons are about 28,000 px tall at 390 px, so the guide's suggested height of 26000 cuts them off; I used 30000.
- **Stray scrolling:** a long display formula (`\text{Present value of benefits}…`) and a 6-column table were the only things that scrolled on a phone; both are fixed.
