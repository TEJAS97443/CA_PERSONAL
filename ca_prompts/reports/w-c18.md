## Problems with the guide and tooling
- **Wide tables on phones:** a cash budget has one column per month, so the 4- and 5-column budgets in the folds scroll sideways at 390 px even after I shortened the labels. The probe reports scrolling inside the table, not overflow. I accepted this, because ICAI's layout is one column per month.
- **Plot point labels** (`points[].label`) sat on top of nearby lines, and I could not place them clearly. In two charts I dropped the labels and described the dots in the caption instead.
- **Flow node text:** a node label of three lines spills out of its box. I kept every node label to two lines.
- **Phone screenshots** with `tools/shot.sh` need a full `build.py` first, which writes the shared `data/` folder; I ran it several times while other agents were working.
- **Guide change mid-task:** CONTENT_GUIDE.md gained the "original means original" rule while I was working; I rechecked all three quizzes against it and changed four questions.
- **Progress log:** I reported the cma.bud.cash checkpoint once with wrong counts ("4230 words, 5 diagrams") and then again with the correct ones (4206 words, 4 diagrams). Later table edits brought it to the 4,226 words given here.
