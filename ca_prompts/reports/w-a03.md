## Facts I am less than certain about (please check)

The SM gives the formula and says assessments "may be expressed in quantitative terms, such as in percentages" (SM 3.10). ICAI never asks for this calculation. The lesson says the figures are illustrative.
2. **model**: SM 3.4 prints "risk of material statement". I wrote "risk of material misstatement", as SA 200 has it.
3. **model, Under the hood**: "CEO/CFO bonus pressure is a financial statement level risk" rests on the MTP January 2026 Series I answer key (MCQ 11 = (a)). It is not SM text.
4. **sa320, worked example**: the normalised PBT uses the average of the three past years. That method is the example's own judgment: the SM says only "normalized profit before tax from continuing operations figure based on past results" (SM 3.21).

7. **sa315, worked example "Four items"**: calling the gift-hamper returns provision "likely significant" and routine payroll "not significant" is my application of the six factors (SM 3.41–3.42). The two "always significant" cases are SM text.
8. **sa315**: the SM teaches the pre-revision SA 315 (five internal-control components; "significant risk" factors). I followed the SM, as APPLICABILITY.md requires. The revised SA 315 is not used.
9. **sa330, Under the hood**: the SM gives no list of overall responses. The examples (IT specialists, experienced staff, interim testing) come from the MTP September 2026 Series II case, and the lesson says so.
10. **sa330, kiosk-rent example**: the acceptable difference of ₹4,00,000 is given data. The investigation steps follow SA 520 as described at SM 4.114.
11. **Exam-angle counts** ("appeared in 19 / 10 / 8 / 10 of 32 documents") count the documents in which I found and read a question on the topic, using grep over the 32 new-scheme P5 files. ## What got in the way
- **Word-range change mid-task.** The limit was 3,600 words when I wrote lessons 1–2 and I cut ICAI lists and wording to fit. After the lead's change to 4,500 I put back the full ICAI wording (inherent-risk categories, control-risk examples, combined assessment, SA 320 "not a bright line", the revision example, and others).
- **Flow renderer bug.** When a decision's "no" branch rejoins a node lower in the same vertical chain, the edge is drawn hidden behind the chain and its label disappears. This happened in the first versions of the SA 320 and SA 315 flows and in a merge in the SA 330 flow. I worked around it by making those flows linear, or giving each branch its own end node.
- **Colour lint vs the default start colour.** A flow's start node defaults to sky blue (e), so any caption that says "blue" triggers the colour lint. Workaround: the caption says "Start at the sky-blue box".
- **`preview_lesson.py` flakiness.** It failed once with `FileNotFoundError ... -full.png` (headless Chrome under load); a retry worked.
- **Tree width on phones.** The trees with four leaves (model, sa330) still need sideways scroll at 390 px. They are readable, but there is no option to stack leaves.
- **Table headers.** A markdown table header containing "(FS as a whole)" rendered without the uppercase header style. I simplified that header.
