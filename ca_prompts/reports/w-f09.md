## Originality
I checked each worked example and quiz item against the ICAI questions above. One quiz item was too close: a Baumol question with monthly disbursements turned into annual, the same twist as RTP January 2025. I replaced it with a total-cost-at-optimum question.

The bank-assessment example uses the SM case scenario's stipulation (75% of net current assets, 10% minimum balance). To keep it original I added two new twists: the company's own cash counting towards the minimum balance, and a test of whether the usable funds cover the peak need.

The four-source comparison overlaps the old RTP Dec 2021 in two of its sources: a bank loan with a minimum balance, and trade credit. The other two sources (bills discounting and CP), the gross-up amounts and the CP lots of ₹5 lakh are new.

## Guide and tooling friction
- Two `$$` lines back to back with no blank line between them merge into one formula and fail the lint. The guide does not mention this.
- Tree `note` text written on several lines overlapped the node boxes and connectors. I moved the lists into multi-line labels.
- Plot `vectors` labels sit on the arrow tip and overlapped the data line. I removed the labels and described the arrows in the caption.
- Flow edge labels are drawn across the arrow line (readable, but crossed).
- At 390 px, 3- to 5-column tables of 9 to 11-digit rupee amounts inside example and answer containers still scroll sideways. The probe counts these as scrolls-inside, never as page overflow. I narrowed the tables (fewer columns, short labels, workings moved to notes), but amounts this size cannot fit fully.
- To stay within the image budget I viewed contact sheets and crops (about 25 images), not every slice.
