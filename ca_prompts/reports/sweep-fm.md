# sweep-fm: originality sweep, Paper 6A Financial Management (2026-10-20)

Scope: the 17 FM lessons in the (revised) brief: fm.capbud.cashflows, irr, npv, payback, rationing; fm.coc.debt, equity, wacc; fm.cs.ebiteps, planning, theories; fm.div.basics, models; fm.lev.leverage; fm.wc.basics, estimate, receivables. Every lesson's reviewer job was done (r-f04 to r-f08), so none was skipped. No lock was held by anyone else when I took it; all locks are released.

Method: for every worked example (prompt and Required) and every `Q:` quiz question (137 items) I ranked all windows of the ICAI papers, RTPs and MTPs (~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/q/new/p6, old/p8) and the SM chapter texts (illustrations, examples, Test Your Knowledge) by TF-IDF similarity, read the closest hits and also grepped the topic's keywords, then judged scenario, data sequence and twist. Flashcards were not in scope (the regenerated detector skips them too); none that I saw turned an ICAI MCQ into a card. The first, flashcard-heavy similar-fm.json list was superseded by the lead's change of method.

Result: **23 near-copies rewritten in 12 lessons; 114 items judged FINE.** Five lessons needed no change (cs.planning, div.basics, wc.basics, wc.estimate, wc.receivables). Several r-f07 rewrites were themselves still near-copies (IRR vans, Walter sister companies, MM example), because they kept the ICAI data rows and requirements and changed only the numbers or added a part. Every new number comes from my own Python in $TMPDIR/sweepfm/. The dependent working notes, plots, mistake boxes, answer-frame ladders, Under-the-hood notes and Exam-angle wording were updated with them. Lint is clean for all 17 files, and `build.py --check` reports nothing for any FM file. It does currently stop on 5 errors, all in another job's bank/items/new/p4 files (tag `cma.mc.cvp`). I used 2 preview images (the new K4/K8 bar chart and the MCC step chart). Re-running `similar.py fm` gives 3 flags. Two are in lessons outside my list (fm.intro.role, fm.sources.other). The third is the fm.lev.leverage DCL quiz, judged FINE below: its match is generic wording only.
