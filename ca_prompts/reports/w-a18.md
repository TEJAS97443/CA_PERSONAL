## Guide and tooling notes
- **Flow diagrams.** A decision whose "no" branch skips over the "yes" branch's node draws the skipped edge behind that node, so the edge and its label vanish. This happened twice. I rewrote both flows as linear chains; the section 77 flow keeps a decision with two separate end nodes, and that works. A layout fix or a note in the gallery would help.
- **Node labels.** Flow end nodes (stadium shape) overflow at 3 or more lines. Graph nodes are small circles, so multi-line labels overflow too. I replaced the graph with a flow.
- **nest diagrams** default to a monospace font for `lines`; it needs `"mono": false` on every box.
- **Word cap.** Lessons with four full ICAI lists hit the 6,000 cap. Lessons 1 and 2 sit at 5,950 and 5,971 after trimming prose; the ICAI lists were not cut.
- **Images.** I stayed within the image budget by building contact sheets and side-by-side crops of the preview slices.
- **Coordinator's instruction.** Charitable institutions are not duplicated; lesson 1 links [[aud.entities.npo]].
