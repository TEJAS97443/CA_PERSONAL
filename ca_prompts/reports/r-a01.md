## aud.nature.basics: 5 fixes

1. **"Who appoints the auditor" table (wrong).** One row said that when a law such as a tax law requires an audit, a government authority appoints the auditor. That is not what the SM says. Income tax law can require an audit without any authority appointing the auditor. SM 1.19 says only that an auditor may be appointed by a government authority in accordance with some law or regulation, for example under tax laws. I rewrote the row to say exactly that. I also added one line: who requires an audit and who appoints the auditor are different questions (the law requires a company's audit, but its members appoint the auditor, SM 1.18).
2. **Quiz Q1 copied an ICAI question.** It was a near-copy of MTP Sep 2026 Series II Q4(c): a charitable trust, the trustee's real brother as auditor, and "independence only matters for companies". I replaced it with an original case MCQ built on the SM's other example, a CA who holds shares in the company (SM 1.5).
3. **Seq diagram caption (unsupported claim).** It said management "neither appoints the auditor nor receives the report". The SM does not say this. Under company law the Board appoints the first auditor, although SM ch. 1 does not cover that. The caption now says only what SM 1.18 to 1.19 support.
4. **Exam angle (incomplete).** I added MTP May 2025 Series I MCQ 15, a 2-mark MCQ on "which is not a likely benefit of auditing". The key is (a), checked against the answer file.
5. **Neighbour links (missing).** I added links to [[aud.nature.scope]] and [[aud.nature.assurance]].

Checked and correct: the definition, its three points and the six "ensures" points (SM 1.5 to 1.6); the origin dates (SM 1.4); the seven benefits in ICAI's order (SM 1.17 to 1.18); mandatory or voluntary audits (SM 1.18); who receives the report (SM 1.19); the SA 200 objectives (SM 1.8); and SM 1.40. ## aud.nature.sa200: 8 fixes

1. **Worked example 2(a) taught something wrong.** The answer said "There is no formula" for how many invoices to test. SM 4.56 (SA 530) says the sample size can be determined by a statistically-based formula or through professional judgement. I rewrote the provision, the analysis and the conclusion. A formula can size a sample, but its inputs follow from the assessed risks, and whether the evidence is sufficient and appropriate is a matter of professional judgement (SM 4.12 to 4.13). No formula makes that decision. I also changed the In plain words line from "No formula tells…" to "No formula decides whether the evidence is enough…".
2. **Quiz Q3 paraphrased an ICAI question.** It was a close paraphrase of MTP Jan 2026 Series II Q1(a) (a junior asks whether risk assessment can be measured precisely). I replaced it with an original case MCQ on materiality: Bunty fixes 5% of profit before tax "because SA 320 lays down 5%" and treats everything below it as immaterial. Sources: SM 3.18 (a misstatement below materiality can still be material because of its circumstances), SM 3.19 (materiality is a judgement) and SM 3.21 (5% of PBT is only an example; higher or lower percentages may be appropriate).
3. **Under the hood (wrong attribution).** The departure duty from SM 1.32 sat under the heading "More that SA 200 does". SM 1.32 belongs to the overview of the engagement standards (15.7) and does not mention SA 200. I renamed the heading and labelled the point as not tied to SA 200.
4. **Bunty's "about thirteen years".** 31 lakh orders × 30 s = 25,833 h. That is 12.9 years only at 8-hour days and 250 days a year; round the clock it is about 2.9 years. The line now says "thirteen years of office hours".
5. "SA 200 is the Standard every other SA builds on" is not in the SM. I softened it to "sets the overall objectives that every audit in accordance with the SAs pursues".
6. **Exam angle, old-scheme Jan 2021 Q1(b).** It now quotes the actual statement: "expected to reduce audit risk to zero and can therefore obtain absolute assurance".
7. **Recap.** I spelled out "TCWG", because the Quick view must stand alone.
8. **Example 2 provisions.** I added the SA 530 sample-size provision and the exact SM wording on how much evidence is needed ("the higher the assessed risks, the more evidence is likely to be required…").

Checked and correct: objectives and the four points (SM 1.8 to 1.9); absolute versus reasonable assurance (1.9, 11.17); purpose (1.10, 1.40); applicable financial reporting framework (1.10); audit risk (3.4); "reasonable assurance is obtained when…" (3.23, 4.12); the premise (8.15 to 8.16); professional judgement (3.9) and the six-stage table (2.11, 3.18 to 3.19, 3.32, 4.12, 8.27); the SA 200 factors (4.13); documentation (6.10); skepticism (11.12 to 11.13); and 4.35 and 8.24. I verified all 13 exam citations, including question numbers and marks.

## aud.nature.limits: 6 fixes

1. **Exam angle, wrong question number.** "MTP September 2026 Series I Q2(c)" (going concern) is actually **Q5(c), 4 marks**; I checked the numbering in both the question file and the answer file. The w-a01 report repeats the same error.
2. **Plot caption and text (false claim).** They said a random sample is certain to contain a fake bill "only when every bill is picked". With 12 fakes in 4,800 bills, any sample of 4,789 or more must contain one. I recomputed the hypergeometric values myself: 60 → 14.03%, 480 → 71.80%, 960 → 93.15%, 1,200 → 96.85%, 2,400 → 99.98%. Those match the plotted points, and so do 160 hours = 20 days of 8 hours.
3. **Future events (gap).** I added SM 7.15: as described in SA 200, the potential effects of inherent limitations are greater for future events or conditions that may cause an entity to cease as a going concern. ICAI's MTP Sep 2026 Series I Q5(c) answer quotes this sentence. I linked [[aud.complete.sa570]].
4. **Worked example 2(b), "what it does not excuse".** It now uses SA 530's wording: a sample size sufficient to reduce sampling risk to an acceptably low level (SM 4.56).
5. **Worked example 2(c).** It repeated RTP May 2026 Q9 almost word for word ("unusually uniform… lack standard commercial markings"). I gave it its own facts.
6. **Quiz Q3 explanation.** "A lack of independence bars the audit altogether" was loose. It now gives the reason from SM 1.5, and the cost option is worded "not in itself a valid basis", as SM 4.71 puts it.

Checked and correct: the five factors in ICAI's order with their explanations (SM 1.14 to 1.17) and the SA 200 box on SM 1.16. Three ICAI Suggested Answers give the box's wording, and they agree with the lesson: PYQ May 2024 Q2(c), MTP May 2025 Series II Q5(c) and MTP Jan 2026 Series II Q5(a). Also correct: 3.30 to 3.31 (the internal control list), 3.70, 4.71, 7.15, 8.18, 11.13 and 11.19. The other 8 exam citations are correct.
