4. **Lead note (3), Sep 2024 MCQ 6 (₹5,100): reconciled.** The lesson cites MCQ 10's ₹2,04,000, so I added a neutral note. The key is reproduced exactly when the 80 units of normal wastage are absorbed by the good units: (5,000 × ₹5,150 + 60,000 × (₹4,800 − 2% + 8% duty) + ₹62,000 insurance) = ₹33,10,92,000, over 65,000 − 80 = 64,920 units, gives ₹5,100.00. The suggested answer publishes only the key, so this is my reconstruction, though exact to the rupee. The MCQ 9 key (29 days) also checks.
