# osweep-c3: originality sweep, Paper 4 Cost and Management Accounting (19 lessons, 2026-10-20)

**Scope.** 19 lessons (process costing, cost sheets, standard costing, service costing, cost accounting systems, unit and batch costing): **148 items checked**, i.e. 52 worked examples and 96 quiz questions. Each item was compared by hand with the ICAI items on the same sub-topic: the SM chapter's illustrations and Test Your Knowledge (`~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/sm/p4/txt/06, 07, 08, 10, 12, 13`), new-scheme past papers, RTPs and MTPs (`q/new/p4`) and old-scheme ones (`q/old/p3`).

**Result.** **58 near-copies rewritten, 90 judged FINE.**
- 42 of the 52 worked examples were near-copies. Almost every one had been built one-to-one on an SM illustration or TYK problem, or on an RTP/MTP/past-paper item that ICAI reuses: the same data table, the same twist and the same Required list, with only names and numbers changed. Several lessons even named their source ("ICAI solves its own version the same way", "the pattern is ICAI's").
- 16 of the 96 quiz questions were near-copies. In each, the stem and question repeated a single SM TYK or MTP/PYQ MCQ. They were concentrated in service costing (7) and standard costing (5).

**How rewrites were done.** Each rewrite keeps the concept and ICAI's formats and wording, but uses the PassBook cast, a new scenario and a different twist, at the same or higher difficulty. Each was checked so that it does not copy a second ICAI item either. Every number was recomputed in Python (scripts in `/tmp/osweep-c3/calc/`): variances add to the total variance, ledgers balance and reconciliations close. Everything that quoted the old figures was updated too: answers, working notes, diagrams and captions, Common mistakes, flashcards, Answer frame lines, Under the hood paragraphs and In plain words stories. Three stories that gave away an example's answer (rates or profits) were neutralised. Correct-option positions were re-varied where an edit had bunched them.

**Checks.**
- `node tools/lint_content.js` is clean for all 19 files, and `build.py --check` shows no errors or warnings for them. All word counts are within 900–6,000.
- Locks were taken and dropped for every lesson, and progress was logged for each.
- 4 preview images were used: the batch EBQ plot, the quotation survey plot, the process-litres diagram and the dialysis break-even chart.
- Original files are backed up in `/tmp/osweep-c3/bak/`.
- A private run of the shingle detector (`similar.py` logic, output kept in /tmp) flags no example or quiz block in these 19 lessons.

Sections below are per lesson: each NEAR-COPY item (source, what matched, what changed, new answer), then the FINE count with the closest ICAI items checked.
