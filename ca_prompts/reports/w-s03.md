## What got in the way
- **Diagram types:**
  - The `plot` type has no circle or bubble mark. For the strategic group map I approximated circles with 40-point `regions` polygons generated by a script. A circle region, or bubble points with a radius, would make strategic group maps and the best-cost figure easy.
  - A `flow` ignores a `w` narrower than its own layout: the four-criteria flow still scrolls sideways at 390 px.
  - Table-diagram headers must be short, or the table overflows.
- **Word limits:** `build.py` accepts up to 5,200 words but CONTENT_GUIDE §1 says 4,500. I kept every lesson under 4,500.
- **Canon clash in others' files:** "Tapri Express" is a delivery app in one existing lesson and railway-station kiosks in the spot-strategy lab. I used neither, and kept TapriNow as the app.
- **Wording close to ICAI MCQs:** three of my first-draft quiz items followed ICAI MCQs too closely (the Altex best-cost MCQ, an R&D/culture core-competence MCQ, an EV subsidies PESTLE-versus-SWOT MCQ). I replaced them with original items before finishing.
