## Things in the guide or tooling that got in my way
- **Adjacent display formulas.** Two `$$…$$` lines one after the other in a `::: formula` block failed with "Can't use function '$' in math mode" in fm.wc.receivables. A blank line between them fixed it. The same pattern passed in fm.wc.basics, so the trigger is unclear; worth a look in the math linter.
- **No timeline diagram type for CA.** The operating cycle timeline is faked with a `plot` of horizontal arrow segments and text y-ticks. It renders well, but a native CA timeline would be cleaner.
- **The probe.** `probe=1` reports "scrolls-inside" 96–113 on every page, including the exemplar-style ones, so the number tells me nothing about my content.
- **PNG budget.** With the ~30-PNG cap I viewed previews as side-by-side pairs of 2,600 px slices. I did not view every slice. Text-only slices (quiz, recap) were checked only through the lint, build and phone overflow probe.
