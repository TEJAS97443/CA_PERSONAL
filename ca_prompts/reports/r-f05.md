## Lead note: the MM-with-tax cost-of-equity formula (SM 5.18). The discrepancy is real, and the note stays.
- **Rendered SM page 5.18:** the boxed formula is Keg = Keu + (Keu − Kd) × Debt ÷ (Debt + Equity). There is no (1 − t), and the denominator is Debt + Equity.
- **Rendered SM page 5.27** (the worked note to Illustration 10) uses Keu + (Keu − Kd) × Debt(1 − t) ÷ Equity, which gives 20.62%.
- **The two ICAI answers** use the 5.27 version: the May 2025 Suggested Answer Q2(b) (19.06%) and RTP September 2024 Q6 (24.28%).
- **Numerical check:** on SM Illustration 10, the 5.18 version gives 20.75% against the true 20.62%. On the lesson's Example 3 it gives 21.68% against 21.60%.
- **Wording:** the lesson's "Two printings of one formula" note in Under the hood is neutral and does not accuse ICAI, so I kept it.
