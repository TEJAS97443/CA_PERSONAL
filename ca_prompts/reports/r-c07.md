## cma.oh.classify (5 fixes)
1. **Four steps or five?** The heading said "Five stages", but the steps listed below it are SM's four (4.8–4.10). Now both are explained: SM's procedure has four steps (estimation and collection, assignment, absorption, under/over-absorption, SM 4.8–4.10). Its chart has five stages (estimation, allocation, apportionment, re-apportionment, absorption, SM 4.11).
2. **"The absorbed amount never equals the actual".** This was stronger than SM, which says "there is bound to be some difference" and that the difference is adjusted "keeping in view … the reasons" (SM 4.10). The step now uses SM's wording.
3. **Exam angle: invented marks.** It gave "MCQs, 1–2 marks" and "Theory, 4–5 marks", but the TYK MCQs and RTP Nov 2023 Q14(e) carry no marks. The ranges are removed. MTP May 2024 S-I Q6(c) (4 marks) stays.
4. **"₹7,54,000, the whole heap".** This clashed with the opening story's ₹15.78 lakh heap. It now reads "the whole ledger".
5. **Neighbour links.** Step 2 now links primary distribution and secondary distribution.

All other facts check out against SM 4.2–4.16, 4.60–4.65 and 1.26/1.33: the four classifications in ICAI's order, the tables and examples, the advantages, the standing orders, the definitions, the packing treatments and the TYK answers.

## cma.oh.primary (11 fixes)
1. **Six fryers, 6,000 machine hours.** "Six fryers that run day and night" cannot log the survey's 6,000 machine hours in a month (the maximum is 6 × 720 = 4,320). It now says ten fryers. The numbers are unchanged, so cma.oh.secondary and cma.oh.absorb still agree.
2. **Rent diagram against case MCQ.** The "How it works" tree had Stores sharing the kulhad unit's rent (2,000 : 1,000 : 1,000). The case MCQ, on the same unit with the same areas, had Stores in an owned shed paying nothing. The MCQ is reworded as a later year in which Stores has moved to a bought shed.
3. **Perquisites.** "Either is acceptable" was not in SM (4.14 simply lists perquisites under both bases). It now reads "pick one and state which you use".
4. **SM 4.17 note.** The lesson said ICAI's note on selecting a base "applies here too". It is softened: ICAI prints the note under the service-department table, and the four tests are offered as a sound check on any basis.
5. **"The benefit cannot be measured".** The lesson framed the other bases as used where benefit cannot be measured. SM 4.15 says only that benefit received is "generally the principal criterion" and that the other bases "may be used". The heading and answer-frame point 1 now follow SM.
6. **Where ICAI prints the ratio.** The lesson said "under the basis". SM 4.24 and the Nov 2023 answer print it beside the item. It now reads "beside the item or its basis".
7. **"Either layout earns the marks".** This mark claim had no source. It now says both layouts are ICAI's own, and to show every basis and its ratio.
8. **"ICAI's bases all measure something physical … because those can be verified".** This was wrong: technical estimates, wages and value of materials are not physical. It is replaced with an accurate statement.
9. **Nov 2023 "accepted both".** The suggested answer *showed* both power bases; "accepted" claimed more than that. It now says "showed".
10. **Cast.** Bunty "visiting for a cost audit" did not fit: he is an article assistant with the statutory auditors. It is now "for Iyer & Associates' audit".
11. **Layout.** The primary-total formula wrapped badly on desktop and overflowed at 390 px. It is shortened.

**Improvement:** the trap about service departments' "direct wages" now cites MTP Jan 2025 S-I Q2(a). There the service departments' direct wages of ₹12,00,000 and ₹1,56,000 were allocated as "Actual", and indirect wages and sundries were spread on direct wages. ## cma.oh.secondary (13 fixes)
1. **Quiz Q3 explanation misstated SM.** It said the largest-cost view "helps only to break a tie". SM 4.20 gives it as some authors' alternative view. The explanation now gives SM's rule and says the largest-cost view is only an authors' view. The "tie-breaker" is now labelled as PassBook's suggestion.

3. **Two wordings of the step-method order.** SM's body (4.19–4.20) counts service and production departments; its summary (4.64) counts other service departments. The lesson now quotes both and notes that they give the same order whenever every service department serves all the production departments.
4. **Direct method "fair enough when small".** SM does not say this. It now shows the drift instead (Example 1 measures it).

6. **Statement heading.** SM's own heading, "Secondary Overhead Distribution Statement" (SM 4.19), is added next to the MTP heading "Secondary Distribution Summary".
7. **Recap bases.** "Maintenance hours" is not in SM's table. The recap now gives the SM 4.17 bases. Its "(tie: largest cost)" now reads "(some authors: largest cost first)".
8. **Wrong marks.** Old-scheme May 2023 Q6(d) is 5 marks (Q6 is "4 × 5"), not 4. It now reads "Bases, 4–5 marks", with each question's marks.

12. **Answer frame.** "Earns credit in a 4-mark answer" had no source. It is softened to an optional illustration.
13. **Layout.** The simultaneous-equation formula broke into four lines, one of them a lone "⇒". It is split into two displays with a line between them.
