## What got in the way (guide and tooling)
- **Long labels overflow.** Flow process boxes and end pills overflow at three or more label lines (fixed by shortening to two). On phones, a long event box on a seq diagram's first lane is clipped at the left edge (fixed by shortening).
- **Reading time.** The app shows its own reading time (26 / 29 / 30 min on the phone screenshot), not the front-matter `minutes:` I wrote (28 / 30 / 30).
- **Word limit changed mid-job.** CONTENT_GUIDE.md was edited while I worked: the word range went from 900–5,200 to 900–6,000. The branch lesson relies on the new limit.
- **Line breaks.** The SA 706 table in Under the hood and the branch "Formats" table become stacked cards on phones, which reads well. The SA 710 effect table keeps its grid, with headers wrapping onto three lines.
- **Image budget.** I used contact sheets (several slices downscaled into one PNG) to stay within about 20 image views for the whole job.
