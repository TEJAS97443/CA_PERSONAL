## Tooling and guide
- The concurrent-subagent limit (20) blocked forking, so everything ran sequentially.
- `tools/shot.sh` at the default 4000 px height shows only the top of a lesson. Heights of 30,000–34,000 work, and I cropped the result into slices.
- 5-column tables with long headers overflow even at the 900 px preview width. I shortened headers and transposed department tables.
- Flows with four or more decisions lay out as a wide staircase (the R&D chart), so I used a `nest` instead.
- A flow's default node colours count as sky blue for the colour lint, so a caption saying "blue" warned until I set `mute` on the neutral nodes.
- `build.py --check` now reports the word target as 900–5200, while CONTENT_GUIDE §1 still says 900–4,500. All four lessons are under 4,500.
