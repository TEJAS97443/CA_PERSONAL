## Tooling (check.py changes; other agents were editing the file at the same time, and I kept their changes)
1. **OCR scans.** A scanned question booklet prints marks as bare numbers in the margin, so the "(N Marks)" comparison could never pass. If the qfile's text is OCR and it has no marks lines, check.py now prints an info line instead of an error.
2. **"(2 + 3 = 5 Marks)" lines.** These were not parsed at all. Now each such line can be read either as its parts (one item per requirement) or as its total (one item, or an OR of that total). check.py picks whichever reading leaves the fewest differences (it tries every combination for up to 12 such lines). Files with no such lines behave as before.
3. **Shared pages in Paper 6.** A page that both section files cite, such as a common answer key, also prints the other section's marks lines. Marks of the sibling file's items whose question ends on that page are now left out.
