## What got in the way
1. **Guide changes in mid-task.** The guide changed three times while I worked:
   - the word range went from 900–3,600 to 900–4,500, and the build counts tables;
   - the guide first said Paper 4 has no MCQ section, then that it does;
   - flow widths and phone rules were added.
   Fitting the first range meant cutting `cma.sheet.mfg` (one worked example became a quiz question). When the 4,500 rule arrived, I restored ICAI's full format wording and ICAI's example lists.
2. **Tables on phones.** The phone card rule in `md.js` (3 or more columns and an average cell length of 24 or more characters) is not in the guide. Several cost-statement tables silently became cards until I wrote a checker. Worth documenting.
3. **Shared preview folder.** `/tmp/preview` is shared and was cleaned by another agent between my render and my reading. I copied my slices to $TMPDIR.
4. **Narrow flows.** A flow with two-way decisions overflows 390 px unless node labels are about 2–3 words; `"w"` alone does not prevent it.
5. **Brief versus guide.** The brief says to cite the Study Material's printed pages, and I did. The theory exemplar `aud.report.sa705.md` existed by the time I checked, and I read its answer frame.
