## What got in the way (guide and tooling)

- **Word cap.** Under the old 3,600 cap I had compressed some SA 700 lists. When the lead raised the cap to 4,500, I restored all of them.
  - SA 700 now sits at 4,487 words, just under the cap.
  - To make room I dropped only non-list material: a third worked example (four correct/incorrect statements), the small print from ICAI's illustrative report, one quiz question and one common mistake.
  - A Standard spanning 30 SM pages (8.4–8.33) barely fits even at 4,500.
- **SA 701 needed no changes.** I had cut none of its lists.
- **Flow: converging edges.** Several edges into one shared end node draw overlapping, ambiguous routes and labels. I worked around it with a separate end node per branch.
- **Flow: three-line end labels.** A three-line label on an end node (pill) overflows the shape vertically. Two lines are fine.
- **Flow: width.** Each decision pushes the chain sideways, so a chain of three or more decisions widens the flow. With long labels it overflowed the 390 px column; shortening the labels fixed it.
- **Tree notes.** A multi-line `note` (with `\n`) under a tree node overlaps the node's box. I used one-line notes.
- **Seq boxes.** On the first lane, a long box text sticks out to the left of the lane header (cosmetic). I shortened the texts.
- **Dialogue lines.** A line written `> **Bunty** (whispering): ...` merges into the previous speaker's line. Only the `**Name:**` form starts a new line.
