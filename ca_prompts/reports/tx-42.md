## Tooling problems
- **"Any four of five" inside a question:** this keeps old P3 papers from being sat; several siblings hit the same thing. It needs grammar support, for example `q6: any 4 of a-e`.
- **o-p3-pyq-nov2023 is inconsistent with its siblings:** it sets a choice rule and so counts Q6 at 25 marks.
- **Running heads:** check.py does not strip running heads that carry page numbers (old P8), so those numbers have to be accepted by hand.
- **Text layer (`pdftotext -layout`):** it inserts spaces inside words and before punctuation ("Co mpany", "Octob er", "Unfortunately ,"). `seg.py --words` (the PDF's word geometry) is the reliable reference for repairing these.
- **Planner estimate:** it counted spurious "MCQs without marks" for these descriptive-only papers. It estimated 19, 13 and 12 items, against the real 18, 12 and 17.
- **Queue:** `bankq.py` and `progress.py` worked without problems.
