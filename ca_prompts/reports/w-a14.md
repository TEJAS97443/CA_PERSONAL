# w-a14 report: aud.entities.govt, aud.entities.local, aud.entities.npo

All three files lint clean (`node tools/lint_content.js` exit 0) and give no errors or warnings in `build.py --check`. I looked at the light and dark previews (diagram slices) and at 390 px phone screenshots of all three (`probe=1`: innerWidth = scrollWidth = 390, no overflow). Every number was produced by scripts in $TMPDIR.

## aud.entities.govt "Government audit and the C&AG"
- **Words:** 5,942 (cap 6,000). The first draft had 7,165 words; I cut prose to fit, but kept ICAI's lists whole. 12 flashcards, 5 quiz questions.
- **Diagrams:** flow (parliamentary control before and after spending), layers (the five expenditure-audit standards), seq (C&AG, statutory auditor and company under s.143(5)–(6)).
- **Study Material pages:** 9.4–9.22 (all of section 1), the case study 9.80–9.82, the summary 9.83, TYK 9.84–9.89, and the TYU 1 answer 9.92.
- **ICAI documents read:** new/p5: mtp-may2024-s1 (Q6(b)), mtp-sep2024-s2 (MCQ 15), rtp-sep2024 (MCQ 8), rtp-may2025 (Q17), mtp-may2025-s1 (MCQs 10–12), pyq-may2025-a (MCQs 1–3), pyq-sep2025 (Q6(b)), mtp-jan2026-s1 (MCQs 6–9, Q3(c)), mtp-jan2026-s2 (Q3(c)), pyq-jan2026 (Q6(c)), pyq-sep2026-q (MCQ 15). old/p6: pyq-nov2020 (Q5(b), Q5(d)), pyq-dec2021 (Q6(c)), mtp-dec2021-s2 (Q6(c)(ii)), rtp-nov2020, rtp-nov2019, rtp-may2023 (Q28(a)), mtp-may2022-s2 (MCQ 6).
- **Facts I am less than certain about:**
  1. **Directions to the auditor.** SM 9.19 puts appointment and directions under s.143(5), but SM 9.20 says "the directions under section 143(6) broadly covers the system of book-keeping…". The lesson gives no sub-section number for the directions.
  2. **Test audit.** SM 9.19–9.20 says the C&AG's role is in s.143(5), (6) and (7), and lists test audit third, but never prints "143(7)". The lesson does not label the test audit with a sub-section either.
  3. **Section 136.** SM 9.20 prints "under sub-section of section 136", with the sub-section number missing. I wrote "under section 136".
  4. **Receipts audit item (ii).** SM 9.16 says "proper allocation of cases"; duty (v) on SM 9.10 says "of revenue". I kept each as ICAI prints it.
  5. **Propriety principle (c)(iv) on allowances.** I nested it under (c), as SM 9.15 prints it, even though it reads as a separate principle.
  6. **"Independent statutory" or "constitutional" authority.** SM 9.7 calls the C&AG an "independent statutory authority"; SM 1 calls it "constitutional". Plain words says "an independent authority whose safeguards are written into the Constitution".
  7. **The "allowed, approved, provided" gloss.** The key box and the layers diagram describe the first three standards this way, and propriety and performance as catching spending that is "regular and still wasteful". This rests on SM 9.14 ("may satisfy all the requirements of regularity audit … but may still be highly wasteful"). It is not an ICAI classification.
  8. **PYQ Sep 2026 MCQ 15.** The answer key is not yet published, so the Exam angle says so. By the SM the answer is option (A).
  9. **Example 3 dates.** 12 Jul to 28 Aug 2026 is 47 days, inside the 60-day window (computed in code).
- **Spoiler fixes I made:** plain words uses a locked museum as its propriety example. Worked example 1 (iii) therefore uses a different case (a building first used years after completion, the SM's "avoidable expenditure"). I also rewrote a dialogue line that hinted at the AGM answer.

- **Words:** 5,956 (cap 6,000). The first draft had 6,241 words. 12 flashcards, 5 quiz questions.
- **Diagrams:** layers (SM 9.3's "major points" frame), layers (the three legal forms and their laws), flow (the revolving fund cycle), seq (the donation money trail, inside an answer).
- **Study Material pages:** 9.3 (rendered the PDF page to read the figure), 9.27–9.32 (NGOs), 9.40–9.42 (charitable institution), 9.75–9.80 (trusts and societies), the summary 9.83, TYK 9.84–9.92 (including the SM's own answer to theory Q10 on 9.91–9.92).
- **ICAI documents read:** new/p5: pyq-may2024 (Q6(a)), pyq-jan2026 (Q5(b)), mtp-may2025-s2 (Q2(b)), rtp-jan2025 (Q18), mtp-sep2025-s1 (Q5(b)), mtp-sep2026-s1 (Q3(a)), rtp-may2026 (MCQ 8, Q18), rtp-sep2026 (Q18), and as settings only mtp-may2024-s1 (MCQ 1), mtp-sep2024-s1 (case scenario 3) and mtp-sep2026-s2 (Q4(c), plus MCQ 10, an educational society, which belongs to the institutions lesson). old/p6: pyq-nov2023 (C/I, Q6(a) OR), pyq-may2023 (Q6(d)), pyq-nov2019 (Q6(b)), pyq-dec2021 (Q6(d)), pyq-jan2021 (Q5(a)), mtp-jul2021-s1 (Q(c)), rtp-dec2021 (Q28(b)).
- **Scope choice:** the brief assigns no lesson to SM §6 "Audit of charitable institution" (9.40–9.42), so I included it here. w-a18's institutions lesson covers educational institutions, hotels, clubs and hospitals, and may also use the SM 9.3 frame; a reviewer should check for overlap.
- **Facts I am less than certain about:**
  1. **Accounting for the van grant and blankets (Example 1).** The SM prescribes no accounting for a specific-fixed-asset grant or a contribution in kind, so the lesson only says the auditor evaluates the accounting policy. The corpus and revolving-fund treatment does follow SM 9.28–9.29.
  2. **Foreign money in an ordinary account (Example 2).** SM 9.32 requires deposit in the FC account and SM 9.83 asks for "special attention", but the SM gives no consequence. "Report the non-compliance" is my conclusion.
  3. **Name of the 1882 Act.** SM 9.28 says "India Trust Act, 1882"; SM 9.29 says "Indian Trusts Act 1882". I used the latter.
  4. **FCRA filing date.** The FCRA report to the Ministry of Home Affairs within 60 days, i.e. by 30 May, is stated as SM 9.30 does, with a version-sensitivity `::: warning`.
  5. **Example 2 marking.** Several remittance points (account payee cheques, 80G check, confirmation letters) come from ICAI's own answer to TYK theory Q10 (SM 9.91–9.92), not from the main text.

## Guide and tooling problems
- **The 6,000-word cap.** Two of the three lessons had to lose 300 to 1,200 words of prose to fit, and govt and npo now sit about 50 words under it. A reviewer has almost no room to add anything without cutting.
- **Tree notes overlap boxes.** A `note` under a one-line node label overlaps that node's box when a sibling has a two-line label. This hit the NPO legal-forms tree, which I replaced with `layers`. Notes seem to be placed for the tallest box in the level.
- **Seq notes do not wrap.** A long spanning `note` ran off both sides of the drawing; I had to shorten it.
- **Flow back edges are misleading.** A back edge from a process node with an end node beside it seemed to start from the neighbouring node (the first revolving-fund flow). I redesigned the flow so the loop leaves from a node that has no sibling.
- **Dark previews are slow.** `preview_lesson.py --dark` for three lessons in a row took more than 120 s, so I ran it in the background.
