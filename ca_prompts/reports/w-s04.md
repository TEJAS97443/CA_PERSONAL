## Things in the guide or tooling that got in the way
1. **Word cap changed mid-job** (3,600 → 4,500). I had compressed ICAI lists in growth to fit, then restored them.
2. **Graph nodes:**
   - `shape: "square"` draws a fixed small box, and longer labels overflow it.
   - Only multi-line labels (`\n`) make the node grow.
   - The default circle with a two-line label worked.
3. **Flow width:** a flow with four end nodes in one row (Ansoff "which box?") is too wide for phones, so I removed it. Long decision labels also overflow at 390 px, so I shortened them.
4. **`nest` lines do not wrap.** Long lines ran off the right edge on phones; I split them into short lines and child boxes.
5. **Table cell colours:**
   - Every `tc-*` cell is bold, so the 20-cell ADL table reads heavy.
   - There is no orange cell colour, so the SM's orange cells became amber.
   - A 5-column table, such as ADL, scrolls sideways at 390 px. That is acceptable, but a stacked phone form would be nicer.
6. **`plot` has no reversed axis.** The BCG convention (high share on the left) needed the −log₁₀ trick with custom tick labels.
7. **Frontmatter titles:** a title in quotes keeps the quotes (`title: "…"` shows them). Leave titles unquoted.
