## aud.report.sa710 (6 fixes)

1. **Worked example 3(ii), the case where the prior statements are not amended.** The lesson said "the Study Material gives no separate rule … evaluate under SA 705 the effect … on the comparative information". The Study Material does give a rule for the current year: SA 510 at SM 4.91. Last year's overvalued closing inventory is this year's opening inventory. If that misstatement materially affects 2025-26 and is not properly accounted for or disclosed, the opinion is qualified or adverse. Rewritten on that basis, with the predecessor Other Matter paragraph kept.
2. **Master table, comparative-statements cell for "prior opinion modified, matter unresolved".** The writer's own inference ("each period's opinion reflects it") was softened to "No separate rule in the Study Material (see Under the hood)".
3. **Worked example 2(b), Malwa Dairy cut-off error.** "Confined to revenue, receivables and profit" left out inventories: goods still held at 31 March 2025 were recorded as sold. Added. I also added that the same sales belong in 2025-26 revenue, so the current year's figures must be checked too and any misstatement there evaluated under SA 705.
4. **Worked example 2(c) repeated SA 706's example 2(d)** (the same unaudited partnership, the same question). It now has a twist of its own: Bunty wants to skip the opening balances because the Other Matter paragraph "warns users". Answer: the paragraph does not relieve the auditor of that evidence (SM 8.60; SA 510).
5. **Under the hood, the SA 560 line.** "SA 560's requirements for facts discovered after the report" went beyond the Study Material, and now reads "the relevant requirements of SA 560" (SM 8.59).
6. **Under the hood, the SA 580 line.** "Both years" did not hold when a predecessor audited the prior year. It now reads "every period on which the auditor expresses an opinion".
