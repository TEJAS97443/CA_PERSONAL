## aud.nature.scope (7 fixes)
1. **Near-copy quiz Q1, replaced.** It had the same twist as MTP Sep 2025 S1 MCQ 15: machinery, authenticating a document and one other exclusion, with the disclosure option correct. The new case MCQ is about doubtful invoices. Bunty uses "not experts in authentication" as an excuse; the correct answer is to investigate further and modify or add procedures (SM 11.13).
2. **Near-copy quiz Q4, replaced.** It had the same twist as the SM's TYK theory Q4 ("CA specifically asked to check whether fraud exists → investigation, not a reasonable assurance engagement", SM 1.39/1.41). The new question is "which statement about audit vs investigation is NOT correct". The wrong statement is "an audit's objective does not extend to fraud", a common error (SM 1.8, 1.12).
3. **Quiz Q3 distractor changed.** "Suitability and life of a building" was the same as May 2026 MCQ 8 (III). It is now about machinery condition (fryers).
4. **Worked example 1 rewritten.** It used RTP Sep 2025 Q9's template: three matters, each mapped to one inclusion, with management saying "outside scope". The facts are kept, but management now gives a different argument for each matter. One argument misuses the authentication exclusion for missing lorry receipts. This makes the example harder and original; the answer and the answer ladder were updated to match.
5. **New material in How it works.** The rule "not an expert in documents ≠ accept anything" (SM 11.13) was only in Under the hood, which the Exam view hides, although the new quiz and example need it. I moved it into How it works §3 and turned the Under the hood item into its flip side as an inherent limitation (SM 1.15; Case Study 1 Q5, SM 1.21–1.22).
6. **Recap bullet 2.** It was a semicolon list, so the Key ideas box would have shown only "coverage". It is now written with commas.
7. **Exam angle wording.** "Suggested answers open with the purpose of the audit" was not true of RTP Sep 2025. It now says the answers list the inclusions, sometimes after a line on purpose.

Added a fifth mistake: "Not an expert, so not my problem".

No factual errors were found in the definitions, the ICAI-order lists (inclusions, exclusions, the eleven qualities), the mnemonic, or the Exam angle citations and marks.

## aud.nature.assurance (9 fixes)
1. **Near-copy quiz Q1, replaced.** It was SM Case Study 2 Q1–Q3: half-yearly statements from a trial balance and drafts, a compilation report, SQC 1. The new case MCQ is about subject matter versus criteria in a green-loan use-of-proceeds report (SM 1.23).
2. **Near-copy quiz Q2, replaced.** It was MTP Sep 2026 S2 MCQ 15 almost word for word ("NOT true … only limited evidence"). The new question asks which Standard fits the statutory auditor's review of the June quarter (SRE 2410, SM 1.29). The "limited assurance, so no sufficient appropriate evidence" trap is kept as a distractor.
3. **Near-copy quiz Q3, replaced.** It was RTP Jan 2026 MCQ 4 (team member, tax authorities and bankers as the distractors, intended users correct). The new question tests sufficient (quantity) versus appropriate (quality) evidence (SM 1.23).
4. **Quiz Q6 rewritten.** It had the same twist as TYU 6. The new case asks whether skipping a required procedure for lack of time, and disclosing that, is acceptable (SM 1.32).
5. **Story count.** The opening said "four requests and one purchase order", but the dialogue has three. It now says three envelopes.
6. **Worked example 1 entities.** Iyer & Associates, Tapri's statutory auditors, were compiling the accounts of "Tapri Cold Chain Pvt Ltd". That reads as a group subsidiary, which would raise a non-audit-service problem the lesson does not discuss. The firm also did agreed-upon procedures for franchisees against Tapri, a conflict of interest. Both are replaced with unrelated entities (Mehra Masala Traders, Kulhad Crafts Ltd).
7. **Flow "Choosing the family of Standards".** Its second decision asked "Historical financial information?", but the SM defines the SAEs as covering assurance "other than audits and review of historical financial information" (SM 1.28). The decision now reads "Audit or review of HFI?". The flow was also narrowed to w 380 with shorter labels, so it no longer scrolls on a phone.
8. **Traps block.** It said ICAI "keeps offering" the SQC 1 trap. In ICAI's MCQs the SQC trap is actually "SRSs and SA 220"; "SQC 1 not applicable" comes from SM Case Study 2. The block now says this, with ICAI's actual distractors.
9. **Recap bullet 2 and model answer (ii).** Recap bullet 2 was rewritten without semicolons (the Key ideas cut problem). In model answer (ii), "consistency with actuals" was attached to disclosure; it now belongs to the assumptions.

## Left uncertain (not changed)
- **Agreed-upon procedures.** Calling them "no assurance / not an assurance engagement" is inferred from the SM's classification (SM 1.28, 1.30–1.31); the SM never states it in so many words. It is consistent with SRS 4400 itself.
- **Internal audit evidence.** The refund-controls answer says the internal audit team's conclusion "does not replace the practitioner's evidence". That is a reasonable inference from element 4; the SM has no SA 610-style rule for assurance engagements.
- **Wording of quiz Q6.** The correct option says a departure is allowed "where a required procedure would be ineffective". That reads SM 1.32 as excluding time pressure, which is an inference.
