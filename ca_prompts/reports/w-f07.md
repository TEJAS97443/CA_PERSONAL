## What got in the way

- **Phone screenshots show folds closed.** `tools/shot.sh` captures worked-example answers folded shut, so their tables and formulas cannot be checked at 390 px; `preview_lesson.py` opens the folds but cannot go below about 500 px. As a precaution I added `\qquad` breaks to the long displays inside answers.
- **Quoted titles keep their quotes.** A `title:` wrapped in YAML quotes renders with the quote marks visible, because the parser does not strip them. Unquoted titles containing a colon work fine.
- **Flow nodes need explicit colours.** A flow node with no colour is drawn in a positional colour, which can include sky blue `e`. The linter then flags a caption that says "blue", so I gave every node an explicit colour.
- **Plot points work without labels.** This was the clean way to mark crossover points where labels collided with curves; the crossover rate then goes on an x tick instead.
- **`timeout` is missing on macOS.** Minor: plain commands worked.
