# w-c07 report: overheads, classification, primary and secondary distribution

Three lessons written, all passing `lint_content.js` and `build.py --check` with no warnings. Each was previewed in light and dark mode and screenshotted at 390 px phone width.

All three use the same factory, Tapri's samosa factory (Frying and Packing are the production departments, Stores and Maintenance the service departments), so the numbers carry over. The ₹15.78 lakh heap in lesson 1 is the budget distributed in lesson 2. Lesson 2's service department totals, Stores ₹1,20,000 and Maintenance ₹3,00,000, are where lesson 3 starts. Every number was computed with scripts in $TMPDIR, and all totals and checks reconcile. The reciprocal results are the same under repeated distribution, trial and error and simultaneous equations: Frying ₹10,15,500 and Packing ₹5,62,500.

## Facts I am less than certain about (each with the page checked)
1. **"Primary distribution" is never defined in the Study Material.** It uses "Primary Distribution Summary" (4.18) and "overheads as per primary distribution" (4.25–4.26), and says that after apportionment "all the overhead costs would have been either allocated to or apportioned over the various departments" (4.9). The lessons define primary distribution as allocation plus apportionment over all departments, which is how ICAI's answers use the term. Secondary distribution is defined at 4.16.
2. **Step-method order.** The body text (4.19–4.20) says to begin with the department that serves the largest number of other service and production departments. The summary (4.64) says "maximum number of other service departments". I teach the body text. Both of my examples give the same order under either reading.
3. **The "largest cost first" rule.** SM 4.20 gives it as "some authors are of the view". I present it as a tie-breaker, which is my framing, not ICAI's words.
4. **"Economy, practicability, equitability and reliability" (4.17)** is printed as a note to the re-apportionment bases. Lesson 2 says the same note applies to primary bases, and flags that it is ICAI's note to the service-department table.
5. **Primary packing.** It is classed as production cost, following SM 4.3 and 4.60, TYK MCQ 4 and MTP Jan 2025 S-I. ICAI's cost sheet adds it after works cost, at the cost-of-production stage, not inside factory overheads. The lesson says "production cost" to stay safe.
6. **Notional rent excluded and rent shared only by the departments that use the building.** This comes from ICAI's RTP Sep 2025 Q9 answer, not the Study Material, and the lesson says so.
7. **ICAI's RTP Sep 2026 case-scenario answer may be internally inconsistent.** Store requisitions are 4,000 : 2,000, but the step-method row uses 5 : 2. I did not cite this anywhere; I mention it here for the question-bank team.

## Guide or tooling issues
- **The 5-column limit and distribution statements.** ICAI's statements have one column per department plus Item, Basis and Total, so they are wider than 5 columns. I split them as ICAI's own answers sometimes do: a working-notes table (Item | Basis | Ratio | Total) and a summary (Item | four departments). On phones, 5-column numeric tables still scroll sideways (the app shows a "scroll sideways" hint). That is acceptable, but a 3-production plus 2-service layout cannot fit ICAI's format under the rule.
- **Display formulas made of words** (for example "Share = Overhead × department's base ÷ total base") overflowed at 390 px until I cut them to two or three words a side. The guide's `\qquad` advice does not help inside a fraction.
- **Flow node labels on three lines overflow their boxes** in the flow renderer; keep them to two lines.
- **The dark preview filenames changed mid-task** to a `-dark` suffix. Earlier dark runs overwrote the light PNGs.
- **No diagram type was missing.**
