## cma.sheet.mfg: Cost sheet for a manufacturer (7 fixes)

**Errors fixed**
1. **Quiz Q1 answer was wrong.** The data: cost of production ₹12,00,000 for 20,000 units, opening finished goods 2,000 units at ₹1,10,000, 21,000 units sold.
   - The key said ₹11,60,000.
   - Correct: ₹12,00,000 + ₹1,10,000 − ₹60,000 = **₹12,50,000** (check: ₹1,10,000 + 19,000 × ₹60).
   - The key and the explanation are corrected.
2. **One Exam angle reference was wrong.** It listed "MTP Series I September 2026 Q5(b)" as a treatment-of-items question. That question is a backward cost sheet: cost of goods sold given, direct wages "being 180% of factory overheads", 5 marks. It is now listed under a new "Working backwards" bullet, with January 2026 Q2(a) (9 marks) and SM Problem 1 (6.23). MTP Series I September 2024 Q1(a) (cost of production from a list of bills) is added.
3. **An ambiguous item in Example 2.** "Special die, life two years' production" was taken as a direct expense. A die can be read as tooling, which SM 6.5(h) puts in factory overheads ("amortised cost of jigs, fixtures, tooling"). Replaced by a patent, which is a direct expense under SM 6.4(e) ("amortised cost of moulds, patterns, patents"), with a working note. All figures are unchanged.
4. **FIFO valuation of closing stock had no Study Material source.** It was cited only to ICAI's suggested answers. The Study Material's own answer to Problem 2 does it (₹94.73 × 44,000, SM 6.32); that citation is added.
5. **May 2025 Q2(a) note added.** ICAI's suggested answer says that a student who reads "office overheads" as general overheads puts them in cost of sales. The lesson now says ICAI accepted both readings.
6. **May 2026 Q2(a) wording corrected.** The question itself asked for "net works cost". It was not an answer-only convention.
7. **Trap quote made exact.** SM 6.23 reads "Direct labour cost ₹17,500 being 175% of works overheads", so overheads = ₹10,000.
