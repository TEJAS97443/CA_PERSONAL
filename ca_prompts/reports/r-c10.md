## Layout: ICAI two-sided accounts in `::: cols` (lead's request), and where it does not fit

What I measured in the app:
- Inside an `example > answer` fold, each half of a `cols` block gets **267 px** at every desktop width from 760 px up. The reading column is fixed, so this does not change with screen size. On a phone, the stacked half gets 275 px.
- Table headers do not wrap on desktop. With "Dr. Particulars | Units | Amount (₹)" headers and lakh amounts, a 3-column process account needs 268 to 282 px. So nested cols tables scrolled sideways, and the fit code even turned some credit sides into "cards".
- At the Formats level (no nesting) a half gets 304 px, and the xxx formats need 275 px, so they fit.

What I did:
- **Formats**: every T-account is in `::: cols` (Process I, Abnormal Loss, Abnormal Gain and Normal Loss accounts in cma.proc.accounts). Before, the process account was split into two tables, and the three loss and gain accounts were single tables with "Dr./Cr." rows.
- **Small accounts inside worked examples** (loss, gain, finished stock, income statement): converted to `::: cols` with the short headers "Dr. / Cr." and ICAI's "(₹)". They now measure 198 to 243 px against 267 px, so they fit at every width.
- **Process accounts inside worked examples** (3 in accounts, 3 in EU): left stacked at full width, labelled "(debit side)" and "(credit side)". Even with short headers the widest ones need 268 to 277 px, so side by side they would scroll. The Formats intro sentence now says so.
- **Inter-process three-column accounts** (Total, Cost, Profit): left stacked. Four columns of lakh amounts need about 370 px, against 267 side by side, and 299 even stacked on a phone. So on phones the Profit column scrolls sideways whichever layout is used. This was already true before my review.
- `::: cols Title` does not render the title (md.js ignores it), so each account keeps a bold title line above its block.

For the lead: widening nested cols halves (less padding) or a dedicated T-account type would let the process accounts go side by side. On phones, 4- and 5-column statements still scroll (EU statements of equivalent production and cost; inter-process accounts). The exemplar cma.mc.bep has one such table.
