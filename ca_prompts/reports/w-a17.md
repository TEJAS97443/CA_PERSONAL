- **Facts I am less than certain about:**
  - The SM never states outright that agreed-upon procedures give "no assurance". The lesson calls them "a related service, not an assurance engagement". That follows from the SM's split: SAEs cover assurance other than audit and review, SRSs cover related services, and the SM's chart of assurance engagements has no AUP branch. It is also consistent with ICAI's MCQ stems ("reporting factual discrepancies").
  - Worked example (d) uses SAE 3420 (pro forma information in a prospectus). The SM names the Standard but not its level of assurance, so the answer says only "assurance on the compilation".

  - SM 1.32 says the report "should draw attention to" departures from a required procedure. The lesson reproduces this as the SM states it and does not reconcile it with SA-specific routes such as SA 501 alternatives.

## Tooling and guide notes
- Graph node labels are not wrapped to the circle, so long two-line labels need an explicit `r`. I used 39–40 so that "Financial management" and "Statistics & mathematics" fit.
- Edge labels on a vertical graph edge sit on top of the line ("evidence, criteria" in the three-party graph). It is still legible, but there is no option to offset the label to one side.
- In the flow layout, the "no" label at a branch junction can touch the arrow (scope lesson). It is legible, and the SA 705 exemplar does the same.
- About 24 images viewed in total. Contact sheets (reduced montages of the preview slices) worked well for checking layout without spending the image budget; full-resolution crops were used only for the diagrams.
- I edited no shared code.
