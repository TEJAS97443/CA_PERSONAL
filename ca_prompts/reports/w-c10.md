## Things in the guide or tooling that got in the way
- **Layouts with too many columns.** ICAI's process account is a 6-column T-account, the inter-process account has 8 columns, and the statement of equivalent production has 10. None fits the 5-column/phone rule, so I split each T-account into "debit side" and "credit side" tables and told the reader to write them side by side in the exam. A table type for T-accounts (side by side on desktop, stacked on phone) would make these formats exact.
- **The word cap counts tables.** The build's 3,600-word cap counts tables, and split T-accounts double their row labels. cma.proc.accounts had to be trimmed three times and now sits at 3,569.
- **Nest boxes stretch.** Nest children stretch to the height of their tallest sibling, so short boxes show empty space (June's "Normal loss" box).
- **Guide change mid-task.** §9 now rules out gantt and memory, so I drew the area chart and the stacked bars with plot "regions". This works well, including at phone width, where plots are redrawn.
- **Out-of-date guide line, since fixed.** The line in §5 that "Paper 4 has no MCQ section" was out of date. The lead corrected it; all three quizzes include case-style MCQs.
