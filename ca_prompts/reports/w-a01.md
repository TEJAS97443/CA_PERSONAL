## What got in the way (guide and tooling)
- **Word limit changed mid-job.** CONTENT_GUIDE and sections.json moved from 900–4,500 to 900–5,200 while I worked; my lessons fit either limit.
- **Flow back edges.** A back edge (`["d", "p", "no: more evidence"]`) was routed over the right edges of the nodes in between, and its label was clipped to ": more evidence" or hidden. I replaced it with a terminal "Back to step 2" node. A flow option to route loops outside the widest node would help.
- **seq and flow overflow on phones.** `seq` defaults to `w` 480, and a flow at `w` 420 with two side-by-side end nodes still overflows at 390 px ("Scroll sideways"). I set `"w": 380` (seq) and `"w": 360` (flow) and shortened labels until they fitted.
- **layers right-hand labels.** At phone width, `right` labels wrap under long layer names. I dropped them from the origin timeline.
- **Concurrent builds.** Once, my `build.py` ran while another agent was building and the data file was not rewritten, so `shot.sh` showed stale content. Re-running fixed it.
- **Phone screenshots of hidden sections.** `shot.sh` captures the default (Exam) depth, so Under the hood (my plot) never appears in phone shots. I checked the plot in preview_lesson only.
