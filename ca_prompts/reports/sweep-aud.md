# sweep-aud: originality sweep, Paper 5 Auditing and Ethics (15 lessons)

Method: for every worked example and quiz question I searched ICAI new-scheme P5 and old-scheme P6 question/answer files and the SM chapter text (illustrations, Test Your Understanding, Test Your Knowledge, case studies), read the closest items and judged NEAR-COPY vs FINE. Every rewrite uses the PassBook cast, is at the same or a higher difficulty, keeps ICAI wording in the answers, cites SM pages checked against `~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/sm/p5/txt`, and has its numbers recomputed. Each lesson was locked, linted (clean) and build-checked (no errors or warnings for these files), then unlocked. Preview images used: 1 (the two new tables, at 500 px). The detector was re-run at the end: 4 flags remain, 3 of them in these lessons (SA 450 Q3, SA 510 Q4, SA 550 Q4). All three are select-all questions on Study Material lists, judged FINE.

Totals: 41 items rewritten in 13 lessons (aud.complete.sa450 and aud.evid.sa530 needed none), plus 1 in aud.doc.sa230, done under the first, detector-based version of this brief.

## Doubts for a reviewer
- **SA 560 Ex1, event 3.** I treat a debtor's insolvency that was caused by a cyclone after the year-end, when the debtor paid on time up to April, as a condition that arose after the date of the financial statements. This applies the SM principle (SM 7.4–7.5). The SM's own example treats a debtor's insolvency as a condition that existed at the year-end, and the answer explains the difference.
- **SA 580 Ex2(b).** The valuation consequence of losing the long-term classification is stated only qualitatively ("the valuation basis that rests on it"). No AS 13 rule is quoted.
- **SA 210 Ex2.** A compilation is treated as a "related service" under SM 11.22.
- **SA 505 Ex2 and SA 210 Ex1(b).** The final type of opinion is stated conditionally: qualified if the effect is material but not pervasive; withdraw or disclaim if it is pervasive.
