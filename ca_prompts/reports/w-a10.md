## What got in the way (guide and tooling)

- **The 5,200-word cap.** It is tight for sub-topics that span several Study Material sections. PPE covers sections 3.7 and 3.8 (about 15 SM pages); current assets covers 3.4, 3.5, 3.6 and 3.10 (about 25 SM pages). PPE started at about 6,000 words. I kept every Study Material list item in ICAI's order but paraphrased many compactly. A reviewer may want to check that nothing examinable was lost.
- **Tables on phones.**
  - A markdown table turns into stacked cards when any column averages 24 or more characters. The blank five-column CWIP format became empty cards because its first column averaged 25 characters, so I used a `table` diagram instead.
  - The seven-column Schedule III receivables ageing format cannot fit 390 px, so it scrolls sideways with a hint. I shortened its headers ("< 6 m", "6 m–1 yr") so it fits on desktop and put the full row names in the caption.
- **Key ideas box.** The box at the top of the lesson takes the first three Recap bullets and cuts a long one (over about 200 characters) at its first "; " (`trimIdea` in `app/js/views/lesson.js`). One bullet was cut down to a stub, so I rewrote the Recaps. The guide does not mention this.
- **Diagram colours.**
  - Flows whose decisions hang off the main line drift right with each decision, so the intangible-asset flow scrolls on phones.
  - The linter flags a caption that says "blue" when default start nodes draw sky blue. I recoloured those end nodes to violet.
- **Another agent's error.** `build.py --check` failed for a while on another agent's file (aud.report.sa706.md, unbalanced fences). It later cleared; nothing of mine was involved.
