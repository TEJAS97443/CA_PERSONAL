## What got in the way (guide and tooling)
- **Table headers** that contain "…" or parentheses are not upper-cased like the other headers ("Concerned with …", "Industry sector (CIMA)"). It is cosmetic; I reworded the headers instead.
- **Graph nodes** default to circles of radius 34 or less, which clip two-line labels such as "Management\naccounting". `"shape": "square", "r": 46` fixed it. It would help if the gallery said so.
- **Image budget:** `tools/preview_lesson.py` also works on a temporary .md outside the repo, so a single diagram can be rendered and checked as one small image. This saved several images. It wrote `/tmp/preview/diag-1.png` (mine; nothing else was touched). Making a montage of dark slices with PIL also helped.
- **build.py:** I ran it once without `--check`, as the guide asks before `shot.sh`, so data/ was rewritten.
- **Ownership gap:** SM section 9, "Cost accounting with the use of IT", and section 12, Limitations, had no named owner. I put the limitations in cma.intro.install and left IT and digital costing to w-c02's cma.intro.methods, with links only.
