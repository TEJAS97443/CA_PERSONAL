## Facts I am less than certain about (page checked)
1. **SA 610, tracing café sales as direct assistance** (example 2, task v). The Study Material only lists the procedures direct assistance must *not* be used for (SM 4.42–4.43). I treated tracing as a limited-judgement task. Support for that: SM 4.40 lists tracing among usable internal audit work, and in MTP Sep 2025 S1 MCQ 2 ICAI treated tracing (Q) as assignable (answer (b) = S, the going concern evaluation, is the one that is not).
2. **IFC, two wordings of s 143(3)(i).** SM 3.63 says "adequate internal financial controls system in place and … operating effectiveness"; SM 8.70 says "adequate internal financial controls with reference to financial statements". I quote 8.70 for the auditor's duty and 3.63 in the responsibilities table. The exemption is printed as two limbs on 3.63 and three on 8.70; they mean the same thing, and I used the three-limb version (8.70).
3. **IFC, Under the hood material not in the current Study Material.** (a) The Act's explanation to s 134(5)(e), quoted from old-scheme ICAI answers (Jan 2021 Q3(b), RTP May 2022 Q14). (b) The old ICAI answer that IFC reporting does not apply to interim financial statements (Nov 2019 Q1(a), MTP Jul 2021 S2 Q4(a)). Both are labelled as coming from older ICAI answers. A reviewer may prefer to cut them.
4. **IFC, small-company warning.** Per CONTENT_GUIDE §7, there is a `::: warning` on the G.S.R 880(E) limits (₹10 crore / ₹100 crore). The Study Material gives no numbers for "small company".
5. **IFC, nest diagram.** It shows ICFR as one of five boxes inside IFC. That is a simplification of SM 4.43 ("wider" and "narrower"); the caption says so.
6. **SA 501, example 2(c).** I applied SA 705 to conclude "qualified (except for possible effects)" for a ₹2.60 crore scope limitation that is 9% of total assets, against materiality of ₹80 lakh. This matches the SA 705 exemplar's logic.
7. **SA 501, Under the hood SA 706 point.** This comes from ICAI's Jan 2025 Q4(c) suggested answer, which cites SA 706 for an uncertainty about the outcome of exceptional litigation, plus the s 143(3) Rule 11 disclosure.
8. **SA 505, an ICAI answer that disagrees with the Study Material.** ICAI's MTP May 2026 S1 Q4(c) suggested answer lists the evaluation categories as "positive confirmation request / negative confirmation request / non-response / exception". SM 4.86 lists "agreement / response deemed unreliable / non-response / exception". The lesson follows SM 4.86.
9. **SA 505, layers diagram.** It says a non-response leads to "follow up; other evidence". Follow-up is in SM 4.82. The SM gives alternative-procedure examples only in the refusal context (SM 4.84). ICAI's MTP answer says alternative procedures may be required for non-responses.
10. **Exam-angle counts.** The counts ("examined in N documents") are counts of the documents I read, not an exhaustive census.

## What got in the way (guide / tooling)
- **Flow process boxes do not grow** for 3-line labels: the text overflows the box (decisions do grow). I kept all process labels to 2 lines.
- **Seq diagrams on phones**: lane boxes with long text on the leftmost lane are clipped at the left edge at 390 px. I shortened them; a minimum left margin or a wrap rule would help.
- **Table header casing**: headers containing an apostrophe, parentheses or ₹ render in mixed case instead of the uppercase style of the other headers (e.g. "Balance per Tapri (₹ crore)", "Reporting under 143(3)(i)"). Cosmetic.
- **Full build blocked once**: `build.py` refused to write data because of another agent's file (sm.int.drivers.md, invalid diagram JSON). It worked on a retry later, so the phone screenshots were taken after that.
- **Length**: SA 610 and SA 501 are around 4,800 words, above the guide's "about 3,000–4,200" for a whole Standard. Both are within the 5,200 limit, and I kept ICAI's lists complete (guide: completeness wins).
