## Tooling
1. **Lone number lines dropped as running heads.** In check.py, `heads()` treats lone number lines with the shapes `#,#,#` and `#.#` as running heads. These are table cells or fraction denominators that the text layer puts on a line of their own. They recur on 40% or more of the pages of an answer-heavy file, so check.py drops them. The result is false "in the transcription ... more than in the PDF" reports. Here that meant 46 accepts in o-p8-mtp-jul2021-s1. Suggested fix: never treat a line made only of digits, commas and points as a head, apart from the page number already handled.
2. **check.py changed during the job.** It was edited at about 15:57 to add page-number logic. After that it reported that two of my accepts (1, 2021) were no longer needed, and I removed them.
3. **Pages missing from the app after `--check`.** `build.py --check` writes nothing, so `tools/shot.sh` shows "Question not found" for a new document until a full `build.py` runs. I ran a full build once, for the third document.
4. **Text, numbers and ₹ inside KaTeX.** The rupee sign (e.g. `\text{Rs.}21{,}…`) must be written inside `\text{}` with the number (e.g. `\text{Rs.21,33,333.33}`). Otherwise check.py's number regex reads the PDF's "Rs.21,33,333.33" differently from the transcription and reports a mismatch.
