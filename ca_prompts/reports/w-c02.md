## Tooling
1. **Flow nodes don't grow.** In `flow`, only decision diamonds get taller for multi-line labels. Start, process and end nodes are a fixed 38 px high (diagram.js, `nh`), so a `\n` label in them spills out of the box. Neither the guide nor diagram-examples.json mentions this. I rewrote those labels as single lines.
2. **The probe doesn't say which elements scroll.** It reports a `scrolls-inside` count but not which tables scroll. I wrote a small script (in `$TMPDIR/wc02/chk/twx.sh`, not in the repo). It loads the app at 390 px inside an iframe, opens every fold, and dumps the DOM with Chrome's `--allow-file-access-from-files` flag, listing every `.tw.tw-x` table with its width. It found tables I could not see were scrolling. Something like it would be worth adding to `tools/`.
3. **Tables inside answers have very little width.** A table inside a worked example's answer fold has only about 300 px on a 390 px phone. So 4-column tables, even with short cells, scroll sideways; one was 3 px too wide because of the word "Depreciation,". I cut those tables down to 3 columns.
4. I used about 25 images in total. The cheap way to work was montages of 2–3 preview slices at 0.6 scale, and phone-screenshot crops placed side by side.
