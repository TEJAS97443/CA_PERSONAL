## Facts I am less than certain about

1. **SA 510, reading the predecessor's report.** The requirement to "read the most recent financial statements … and the predecessor auditor's report" is not in the main SA 510 text (4.88). It appears only in the TYK answer on SM 4.131 and in ICAI's suggested answers. I cite it as SM 4.131.
2. **SA 510, how far the auditor may rely on the predecessor.** SM 4.89 says the current auditor can "ordinarily … place reliance" on the predecessor's closing balances. ICAI's answers (MTP May 2026 S2 Q3(a), MTP Sep 2025 S2 Q1(c)) say reliance "cannot be placed solely". The lesson reconciles the two: reliance is reached through procedure (c)(i), never by doing no procedures.
3. **SA 510, Pinky's turmeric example.** I treat the misstated opening stock as a prior period item that leaves net profit unchanged, and say the opinion is qualified if it is not disclosed. This rests on SA 510(a) ("adjustments disclosed as prior period items", SM 4.88) and the reporting rule on SM 4.91. Accounting for prior period items (AS 5) is not in the P5 Study Material.
4. **SA 560, adjust or disclose.** The Study Material classifies the two types of event (7.4–7.5) but states the treatment only in TYU answer 1 (SM 7.57: existed at the year-end, so adjust). That type (b) events need disclosure comes from ICAI answers (RTP May 2024 Q16, MTP Jan 2026 S1 Q6(c)). The lesson cites them this way.
5. **SA 560, wording of the new report after issue.** ICAI's January 2026 Q5(a) answer says the new report after issue includes "Modified opinion as applicable/ an Emphasis of Matter paragraph or Other Matter(s) paragraph". I followed SM 7.10: an Emphasis of Matter or Other Matter paragraph.
6. **SA 570, January 2025 MCQ 15.** The stem says management finds the going concern assumption "will not be suitable", yet ICAI's key is (A), the "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" section. On SM 7.22 an inappropriate basis gives an adverse opinion. The trap note reports the key neutrally and gives the Study Material rule. A reviewer may prefer to drop the citation.
7. **SA 570, the name.** Chapter 7 calls it "SA 570"; chapter 8 calls it "SA 570 (Revised)". I use "SA 570", and "(Revised)" only where SA 700 is quoted.
8. **SA 550, family links on the related-party map.** The Study Material does not define "close family member". The map draws the cousin and brother-in-law links as dashed, and the lesson says the applicable framework decides whether those firms are related parties. A quiz question treats the managing director's husband's company as related through Bunty's wording only.
9. **SA 550, CARO clause (xiii)** (sections 177 and 188, SM 8.80) comes with the version-sensitivity warning.
10. **Invented illustrative figures:** the liquidation values for Mehra Mithai (shown "as estimated by a valuer") and its cash forecast are my own numbers, not data from ICAI.
11. **No question numbers available.** The old-scheme November 2023 SA 550 question has no question number in the OCR text, so it is cited as "the November 2023 paper (3 marks)".

## What got in the way

- **Flow node height.** Process nodes are a fixed height. A label of three lines spills over the box border, and the linter does not warn. I kept every flow label to two lines or fewer.
- **Flow width on phones.** In a flow, `"w"` barely changes the real width: two branch nodes side by side are sized by their labels. Making flows fit 390 px meant cutting labels to two or three words, then rechecking with `shot.sh` (which first needs a full `build.py` run).
- **Hidden edge.** When a decision's "no" edge went to a node directly below the "yes" path, the edge was drawn on top of the yes path and could not be seen (first draft of the SA 550 flow). I made that flow linear instead.
- **Table header styling.** Headers with parentheses or more than about 25 characters are not set in capitals ("Quantity (kg)", "Opinion (SA 705)"), so one table can mix two styles. I shortened some headers; the rest is renderer behaviour.
- **Phone screenshots.** `preview_lesson.py` only renders at 900 px, and `shot.sh` captures from the top of the page. Checking diagrams lower in a lesson needed a tall capture (16,000–30,000 px) that I then cropped.
