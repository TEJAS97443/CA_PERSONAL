## Tooling problems
1. **check.py, fixed (small change, shared tool).** MTP Sep 2026 S2 6A prints "(2 Marks)" against each case MCQ and then the case total "(5 x 2 = 10 Marks)" again. check.py counted five extra 2s and reported "PDF only {2: 5}", and an `accept:` line cannot clear a marks mismatch.
   - The fix: a `(n x m = …)` line is treated as a repeated total only when the PDF has n more marks of m than the items *and* a case holds exactly n MCQ items of m marks. It adds `many_pdf` plus one block after the "carry N marks each" rule.
   - Verification: the unit tests pass (`tests/py/test_bank.py`, 19 OK), and check.py output is identical before and after on all 30 checked files.
   - Someone else was editing check.py at the same time; my change was still in place afterwards.
2. **Bank figures are never shipped (not fixed: core code).** `build.py`'s `figures_mod.write_webp` copies only `pyq/figs/*`, never `bank/figs/<doc>/`. The app loads `data/figs/<doc>/<file>`, so every bank figure shows as a broken image with its alt text. Affects the figure above and `n-p4-pyq-jan2026`.
3. **Figure placement (not fixed: core code).** `qcard.js` draws every non-option figure in the question area, even one with `where: answer`. The Q4(b) answer diagram's alt text therefore appears before the answer is opened.
4. **Main build blocked by another job's file, and how I worked around it.** During batch 1, `build.py --check` failed only on someone else's in-progress `n-p4-pyq-jan2025.md` (`marks: none` without a note), so it wrote no data. To open my items in the app I built a throwaway copy in `/tmp` (since deleted); I wrote no shared data. The main `--check` was green for batches 2 and 3.
5. **Minor UI.** Long labels such as "1(b), part 1 of 2" wrap and get cut off in the side navigator.
6. **OR choice logic.** The choice logic pairs an OR item with one item only (`alt:`). An OR that replaces two sub-parts (P5 Jan 2025 Q6(c)) still counts (c)(ii) in the maximum when the OR is the one answered.
