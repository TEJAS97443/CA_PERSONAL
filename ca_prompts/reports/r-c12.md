It reproduces SM 12.7–12.8 only: the composite unit, both bases, the cinema seats and the definition of the equivalent unit, with no hotel example. Corrected, and point 6 of the frame now gives the full SM definition of the equivalent service unit.
5. "Deluxe suite is let at 2.5 times" was missing its base. Now "2.5 times the Standard suite", with "same amount of overheads" (SM 12.8–12.9).
6. **Mistake "Per bed for a hospital's composite unit"**: it could teach that "per bed" is wrong. SM 12.4 lists "per bed" as a valid hospital cost unit, and ICAI's January 2025 answer uses it. 8. **MTP Series I May 2026 Q1(c) trap**: the lesson said "22.667 × 750 is exactly 17,000". Reworded: ICAI writes 17,000 "(approx.)", and 68/3 × 750 is exactly 17,000.
9. Van example: the cost data did not say which period it covered. Now labelled "(for the month)". The ambiguous "₹5,000, ₹2,400 and ₹1,500" for four items is now split by semicolons.
10. The trick box's "which basis is bigger" now ties the sign of Σ(dᵢ − d̄)(wᵢ − w̄) to each case. The identity is correct in general; I checked it on Kesar's truck (−80 = 760 − 840) and on the Pune truck (−110).
