## Facts I am less than certain about (with the page checked)

1. **SM's two statements on NRV** (SM 11.7 vs 11.8/11.11): sales value at split-off "is the preferred technique" when split-off prices exist, yet the NRV method "is used when the realisable value of joint products at split-off is not known". The 11.7 paragraph sits oddly under the NRV heading. I teach both in ICAI's words and reconcile them in Under the hood; a reviewer may want to check the reading.
2. **NRV pro-forma's "Less: Profit margin" line** (SM 11.7; RTP May 2024 Q13(d) answer lists "estimated profit margins" first). Most ICAI numericals give no margin; I say the line drops out when no margin is given.
3. **Constant gross-margin NRV method** appears only in the SM's Case Scenario 1 answer (SM 11.38–11.39), not in the chapter text; I included it in Formats and Example 3 because ICAI examines it in case MCQs.
4. **SM 11.4 says management needs the share of joint cost for the further-processing decision**, while every ICAI solution decides on incremental figures (the apportioned cost appears on both sides and cancels: SM 11.32–11.33, 11.36–11.37). I stated the SM sentence and explained it rather than contradicting it.
5. **ICAI's advice sentence** is printed "to sale product Y at the point of separation" (SM 11.36; Jan 2026 suggested answer). I quote it with "sell".
6. **Fixed costs of a further-processing department:** ICAI's May 2026 Q3(a) answer counts all of Department II's conversion cost (fixed overhead included) as incremental, without stating why. My rule (count a fixed cost only if it arises because of the processing) is derived from the relevance principle at SM 14.33, not stated in chapter 11.
7. **"Oil cakes" as a by-product** comes from MCQ 7's answer (b) "Edible oils and oil cakes" (SM 11.21, key 11.29). The option names both items; I read oil cake as the by-product.
8. **Re-use basis** (SM 11.16) repeats the same rule for "the same process" and "an earlier process only"; I wrote it as "valued at the materials introduced into the process it re-enters" and flagged the oddity in Under the hood.
9. **SM 2.66 scrap note** is paraphrased, not quoted (the SM text reads "Scraps are termed as by-products and has small recoverable value").
10. Exam-angle counts are counts of the documents I read (listed above); September 2026 PYQ has no suggested answers yet, so its by-product items (Q6(b)(i)–(iii)) are cited from the question paper only, and its marks come from OCR of a scanned paper (Q6(b)(ii) 1 mark, (iii) 2 marks).

## What got in the way

- The linter does not catch a diagram nested in an `answer` with the same colon count; only `build.py --check` does. Running both caught it.
- 5-column statements (Particulars + 3 products + Total) scroll sideways at 390 px; the app handles it (sticky first column, "Scroll sideways" hint), so I kept ICAI's layout instead of splitting statements.
