## Lead's note: "property of the auditor" vs "property of the firm"
I rendered both PDF pages (ch06.pdf p.11 and ch11.pdf p.30), and both wordings are genuine:
- SM 6.11 (§1.12) says "SQC 1 provides that, unless otherwise specified by law or regulation, audit documentation is the property of the auditor".
- SM 11.30 says "engagement documentation is the property of the firm".

ICAI's own January 2026 suggested answer to Q3(b) cites SQC 1 and uses "property of the auditor".

The lesson used to tell students "In an answer on SQC 1, write 'the firm'". That instruction was not supported, because ICAI itself writes "the auditor". The note now:
- says both wordings are ICAI's;
- explains that where a firm is appointed, the auditor is the firm;
- says either wording may be used, as long as the "unless otherwise specified by law or regulation" condition and the client-extracts rule come with it.
