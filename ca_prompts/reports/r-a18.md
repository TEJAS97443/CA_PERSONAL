## Lead note: the LLP audit exemption (either vs both)

I rendered and read the pages myself.

- **SM 9.37 (rendered):** "any LLP, whose turnover does not exceed, in any financial year, forty lakh rupees, **or** whose contribution does not exceed twenty five lakh rupees, is not required to get its accounts audited." The Study Material repeats this wording in its Test Your Knowledge answers 6 and 12 (SM 9.89–9.90).
- **MTP January 2025 Series II:** I rendered question page 3 and answer page 1. MCQ 5 is keyed **(b)**: "always required to conduct an audit if either the turnover exceeds ₹40 lakhs or the capital contribution exceeds ₹25 lakhs". On that reading an LLP is exempt only when both figures are within the limits.
- **The literal Study Material reading** is option (d) ("contribution exceeds ₹25 lakhs **and** turnover exceeds ₹40 lakhs"), which ICAI did not key.
- **Conclusion: the clash is real, so the neutral warning stays.** I tightened it:
  - it cites SM 9.37 and 9.89–9.90;
  - it notes that the MTP's reading coincides with the small-LLP definition (both limits, joined by "and");
  - it says no other ICAI question read decides between the two readings.
- **Supporting evidence:** January 2025 PYQ MCQ 13 has its key **(B)/(C)** (rendered answer page 9). Option (B) gives a small-LLP definition with only the turnover limit; option (C) says Form 11 is due "within 30 days".
- **Recap and flashcard:** both now carry the MTP reading next to the Study Material wording. I also fixed the Recap bullet so its "Key ideas" cut at the first semicolon no longer breaks the sentence.

## Left uncertain

- **LLP readings.** Which reading of Rule 24 ICAI will mark in a written answer is unknown; the lesson tells the student to quote the rule and state the reading applied.
- **Partnership accounting standards.** SM 9.33–9.34's "Alternatively, only disclosure…" is ambiguous about which case it covers. The lesson repeats it without interpreting it.
- **Small-LLP limits and the Multi-State Act provisions** are taught as the Study Material states them. I did not check later amendments, by design. MTP May 2026's section 77 answer omits the 51% condition, which the lesson keeps from SM 9.72.
- **Van lease.** Treating the van lease as a finance lease rests on the SM's attribute list; ICAI's own questions so far have only used cases where all the cues agree.
