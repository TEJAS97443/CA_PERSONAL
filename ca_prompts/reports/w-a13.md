## Anything that got in the way
- **Word limit.** CONTENT_GUIDE §1 says 900–5,200 words, but `tools/buildlib/lessons.py` (via config) accepts 900–6,000. Both lessons sit just under 5,200 because ICAI's lists are long (section 143(3), Rule 11, the 21 CARO clauses, and the five exemptions). I kept the lists complete and trimmed prose elsewhere.
- **Flow merge node.** In a `flow`, a node that several branches merge into (three report destinations into one "also CARO" end) drew a line through the middle box, so it read as CG → audit committee. I removed the merge node and moved the point into the caption. A real merge or join layout would help.
- **Six-column formats.** The CARO formats for clauses (i)(c) and (ix)(a) have six columns, more than the guide's limit of five. I gave them as Column / What it gives lists.
- **Minor.** `timeout` does not exist on macOS (harmless). The image budget was fine: about 18 PNGs viewed, using contact sheets to find the diagram slices.
