## Facts I am less than certain about (please check)

1. **Scope of cma.std.recon.** The January 2027 Study Material teaches no sales variances, no operating statement and no journal entries for variances. The syllabus line is "Calculation and Reconciliation of Material Cost, Labour cost, Variable Overhead, Fixed Overhead" (SM initial pages, module 1). So I built the lesson from (a) standard cost of actual output ± cost variances = actual cost (SM 13.13), (b) the flexible-budget comparison of SM 15.92, (c) ICAI's MTP income statement (standard gross margin → actual gross margin), (d) disposition (SM 13.5, MCQ 8, 4.42–4.46, 7.5). The two revenue lines are labelled "effect of volume" and "effect of selling price", not as named sales variances, and the lesson says so. **Format 2 (statement reconciling budgeted and actual profit) is my own layout** of the SM 15.92 variance column, not a format ICAI prints.
2. **SM 4.43 flowchart against SM 4.44–4.46 text.** The flowchart sends "wrong estimation and abnormal reasons" to the Costing P&L. The text (4.44) and Illustration 8 (4.45–4.46) send a large difference from an unavoidable wrong estimate to a supplementary rate, and abnormal causes and inefficiency to the Costing P&L. I followed the text. The owner of cma.oh.underover should know.
3. **Disposal rules for non-overhead variances.** The detailed rules in SM 4.44–4.46 are written for overheads. For variances in general the SM gives only 13.5 (transfer to the Costing P&L as per the plan adopted) and MCQ 8 (controllable variances to the Costing P&L). The lesson says exactly this. The Costing P&L Account with adverse variances debited and favourable ones credited (recon Example 2) rests on SM 7.5 and 13.5; ICAI prints no such account for variances.
4. **ICAI's two versions of the Astra MTP question disagree.** MTP September 2026 Series I computes absorbed factory overhead on actual hours (₹2,50,000; variance ₹30,000 (A)). MTP January 2026 Series II uses standard hours for actual output (₹2,56,000; ₹24,000 (A)). The lesson follows SM 13.42 (SH × SR) and mentions the discrepancy as a trap.
5. **VOH and idle time.** "Idle hours create no VOH variance" is inferred from the SM's footnote "# Actual Hours (Worked)" on the VOH formulas (SM 13.36). The SM does not state it in words.
6. **VOH reasons and responsibility.** The SM lists none for VOH variances. The answer frame's one-line comment ("expenditure reflects the rate paid for power, fuel, consumables ...") is general wording, not SM text.
7. **Illustration 12's "overhead expenses variance"** (SM 13.46–13.47). My reading: budgeted fixed − (total actual − standard variable for actual output), "in effect one figure for the spending on both". Worth a check.
8. **SM oddities, not used in the lessons.** The Practical Problem 12 answer (13.79) labels absorbed overhead "standard output for actual time" though the figure is actual output. Illustration 13 (13.49) prints a stray intermediate "₹3,80,000 − ₹3,90,000" in the efficiency line; the result ₹10,000 (A) is right.
9. **Exam-angle counts** (VOH 14 documents, FOH 16, recon 7 + 3 theory) are counts of the documents listed above, not of every file. The old-scheme January 2021 question number for Premier Industries was not recorded (the OCR shows only "(b)").

All worked-example, diagram, quiz and flashcard numbers came from scripts in $TMPDIR (voh.py, foh.py, recon.py), with every reconciliation checked to close.

## What got in the way (guide / tooling)

- **Nesting errors pass the linter.** A `::: cols` account inside a 3-colon `::: answer` rendered as raw text, and an example/answer/diagram nested in the wrong order showed up only in `build.py --check` or in the preview. Both are fixed; a lint check for this would help.
- **Phone width.** Tables with 4 numeric columns of lakh-formatted amounts ("30,40,000", "95,000 (F)") scroll sideways at 390 px. I split them into 3-column tables. The 5-column Format 1 template still overflows by about 20 px even with short headers. Display formulas longer than about 30 characters overflow even with `\qquad` splits, so I wrote them in symbols with legends.
- **No waterfall chart type.** I built one from `plot` `regions`. On phones the x-tick labels thin out (Vol., Mat. are dropped), and the two smallest bars carry no label; the caption lists every value.
- `preview_lesson.py` started writing dark slices as `<id>-dark-N.png` part-way through the session (earlier `--dark` runs overwrote the light slices).
- Mid-task rule changes from the lead were applied: the 900–4,500 word range, `::: cols` for Dr/Cr accounts (recon Example 2) and an explicit `"w"` on flows (400). The FOH nesting warning the lead saw came from an intermediate draft and is gone.
