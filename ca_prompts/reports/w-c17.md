## What got in the way
1. `build.py` refuses to write data/ while any other lesson has a KaTeX error ("1 errors — data not written"); at times cma.svc.transport and cma.svc.others had one. That blocked `tools/shot.sh` until their authors fixed it.
2. `tools/shot.sh` shows answer folds closed, so the working cannot be checked at phone width. My workaround was the preview HTML inside a 390-px iframe in headless Chrome. That copy is static, so md.js's phone logic (row cards, sticky first column) is not applied there.
3. On phones the app groups consecutive formula blocks into a numbered list, which makes each formula narrower. Formulas that fit at 900 px overflowed at 390 px, so I rewrote them with symbols plus a legend.
4. A `graph` with `"layout": "layers"` placed a node out of rank order. I used a `flow` with branches instead, which merges well.
5. The proseTable rule turns a table into row cards when **one** column averages 24 or more characters. Working-note tables with explanations in the first column were hit by this. I moved those explanations below the tables.
