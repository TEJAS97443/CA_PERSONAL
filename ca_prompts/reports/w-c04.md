## What got in the way
- **Rule changes mid-job.** The guide's size rule changed twice (prose 1,200–2,800, then a build count of 900–3,600, then 900–4,500). I had cut ICAI lists in procure to fit 3,600, then restored them: the store keeper's seven duties, the ten requirements, bin card and stock control card pros and cons, the five objectives, and the true/false example.
- **Shared /tmp/preview.** Other agents' runs deleted my slices between generating and reading them, so I copied them to $TMPDIR straight after each run.
- **Phone width.** preview_lesson.py at 420 px does not emulate a phone (text overflows), so the phone checks used tools/shot.sh.
- **Phone overflow in maths.** Display fractions overflow on phones and `\qquad` cannot split a fraction, so formula wording had to be shortened (Rate = Total cost in stock ÷ Total units in stock, OE = C × x/(100 − x)).
- **Diagram widths.** seq lane boxes are a fixed 104 px, so 4 lanes need w of about 430–440. Any diagram wider than 440 px gets a min-width and scrolls sideways.
- **Key ideas box.** It shows the first three Recap bullets cut at about 150 characters, which cut one bullet mid-parenthesis. I reordered the Recaps so the first three bullets are short.
