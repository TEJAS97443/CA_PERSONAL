## Facts I am less than certain about (please check)
1. **MM cost of equity with tax.** SM 5.18 prints Keg = Keu + (Keu − Kd) × Debt ÷ (Debt + Equity), with no (1 − t). SM 5.27, the May 2025 Suggested Answer and RTP Sep 2024 Q6 all use Keu + (Keu − Kd) × Debt(1 − t) ÷ Equity. Only that version matches Ke computed directly from the income statement. The lesson teaches the (1 − t) version and explains the misprint in Under the hood.
2. **DCL with preference shares.** The Jan 2026 Suggested Answer (Q2(a)) labels as "sales at which EBT is zero" a figure it computed with DCL = C ÷ (EBT − Dp ÷ (1 − t)) = 3.84; that figure is really the sales at which EPS is zero. It gives C ÷ EBT = 3.2 as an alternative. The leverage lesson (Example 3 and the Exam traps) teaches both and says which is which. A reviewer may want to confirm this reading.
3. **DOL from percentage changes.** May 2024 Q1(b) shows DOL as 2.22/2.40 (contribution ÷ EBIT at the reduced sales) "or" 1.875 (% change method). I wrote that the % method gives the DOL at the starting level and that the answer showed both.
4. **The illustrative trade-off chart** uses an invented distress-cost function (0.0002 × D²). The caption labels it "Illustrative". The Study Material's figure has no numbers.
5. **Business vs financial risk table** (leverage, Answer frame). The rows follow ICAI's answer to MTP Series I Jan 2026 Q2(b): unavoidable risk, variability of EBIT, demand/prices/proportion of fixed cost, financial leverage multiplier. The "additional variability of EPS" cell is my wording, not ICAI's.
6. **Capital structure vs financial structure** (planning, Under the hood). This comes only from the answers to SM MCQs 3 and 5 (SM 5.49, 5.59); the chapter text gives no definition.
7. **Optimum capital structure definition.** I used SM 5.9 ("value of the firm is highest and the cost of capital is the lowest") and SM 5.32. The MTP May 2024 S1 answer words it slightly differently.
8. **Marks** quoted in the Exam angle sections are those printed in the question papers. RTPs carry no marks. May 2024 Q1(b) marks were read from an OCR margin as 5 (not 100% certain), and May 2024 Q2(b) as 4.

## What got in the way
- **Word cap.** The build's 4,500-word cap forced trims in theories (6 theories, 3 full worked examples, ICAI's full assumption lists) and in planning (ICAI's full lists of 5 causes, 4 consequences, 4 remedies, 3 consequences, 5 effects, 3 remedies, 7 principles, plus 3 case scenarios). I kept every ICAI list complete and cut prose instead. Theories had to lose an Under-the-hood subsection on personal leverage.
- **Plot series have no x-domain.** `plot` cannot draw `fn` over a range, so a curve with a vertical asymptote (DOL against sales) has to be drawn as two hand-sampled `pts` series. A `from`/`to` domain on a series would help.
- **Point labels on line charts.** Labels at line intersections almost always overlap the lines. I dropped the labels and used dots plus a dashed vertical line, naming them in the caption.
- **Another lesson broke the build.** `build.py` (full build, needed before `tools/shot.sh`) failed once on another agent's lesson (content/subjects/sm/sm.int.drivers.md, a diagram containing "@@SGM@@"). I retried a minute later and it built.
- **Section order differs on the phone.** On the phone the app shows Practice before Exam angle and collapses Under the hood. That is the app's layout, not the file order. I mention it only because the guide's section order and the rendered order differ.
- **Subagents.** I could not delegate the question-bank survey to subagents (the concurrent-subagent limit was reached), so I read the question documents directly with grep/context scripts.
