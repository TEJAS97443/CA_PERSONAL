## Lead note: January 2025 MCQ 15 (SA 570)

I rendered the PDF pages of `pyq-jan2025-a.pdf`:
- **The question (p.8):** management "acknowledged that going concern assumption will not be suitable" and says "the fact of uncertainty related to going concern would suitably be disclosed".
- **The key (p.9):** 15 = (A), the separate "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" section.
- **SM 7.21–7.22 (rendered, ch07.pdf pp. 21–22):** if the statements use the going concern basis and its use is inappropriate, "the auditor shall express an adverse opinion".

The stem, read literally, conflicts with SM 7.22. However, none of the four options is an adverse opinion. Options B, C and D all put the matter under Key Audit Matters, so (A) is the only option that follows SA 570.

I kept the citation and rewrote the trap neutrally under the heading "Its own section, never Key Audit Matters". It states the facts of the MCQ and ICAI's key, and draws the lesson the key does support: a material uncertainty goes in its own section, never in Key Audit Matters (SM 8.50, 8.54). It then sets out SM 7.22's two rules separately. It no longer suggests that the key is wrong.
