## Tooling notes
- `build.py --help` ignores `--help` and runs a full build that writes app data. That build is also needed before `tools/shot.sh` can find a newly added item: `build.py --check` alone leaves the app showing "Question not found".
- check.py's running-head detection drops "Question N" lines when a document has six questions on 14 pages, which forces question numbers into `accept:`.
- check.py has no way to accept a marks mismatch, so a marks conflict between the Suggested Answers and the scan can only be settled one way (Q5(d) above).
