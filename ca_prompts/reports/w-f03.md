# w-f03 report: FM Ratio Analysis (4 lessons)

All four files lint clean (`node tools/lint_content.js`): no errors, no warnings. `build.py --check` shows no errors or warnings for them. Each was previewed in light and dark mode, and checked at a phone width of 390 px (`tools/shot.sh` with `probe=1&fold=open`, plus a scratch check for formulas that scroll inside their box, see Tooling). The final state has no page overflow and no formula or table that scrolls sideways.

Between them the four lessons cover the whole of Study Material chapter 3:
- Liquidity lesson: definition, ratio analysis, sources of data and classification.
- Activity lesson: the activity ratios.
- Profitability lesson: profitability, market ratios and DuPont.
- Prepare lesson: users, application in decision making, limitations, horizontal and vertical analysis, the disclosure list, and rebuilding statements from ratios.

## Tooling and guide notes
- **Phone overflow check.** `probe=1` at 390 px reports only elements wider than the screen. A display formula that scrolls inside its own, narrower box (the numbered formula list on phones) does not appear in its counts: probe said "1 scrolls-inside" while the screenshot showed many "Scroll sideways to see the whole formula" hints. I found them with a scratch script: headless Chrome loads the lesson in a 390-px iframe (`--allow-file-access-from-files`), opens the folds and counts `.fit-x` elements. A tool like this would help every writer. One run gave a false positive, probably timing, and a re-run cleared it.
- **What fit the phone column.** At 390 px a display-formula part fits only up to about 30–35 characters. Many ICAI formulas needed a `\qquad` before "=" or named symbols to fit.
- **Diagrams:**
  - Tree `note`s wider than their node overlap the neighbouring notes, and there is no automatic spacing for them.
  - On graph square nodes, `note`s collide with edge labels; I used two-line labels instead.
  - A flow with merging edges lays out in a confusing order; I restructured it as a branching flow with no merges.
- **Word limits.** The guide says 900–5,200 words; content/sections.json allows 900–6,000. All four lessons are within both.
- **Shared build.** `build.py --check` briefly failed on another lesson's KaTeX error (fm.wc.receivables). Its owner has since fixed it.
