## What got in the way (guide and tooling)
- **Guide changes mid-job.** The size limit rose from 3,600 to 4,500 words ("do not cut ICAI lists"). I had already trimmed ICAI lists in payroll to fit 3,600, and restored them afterwards. Gantt diagrams were deprecated after I had used one (replaced with seq). Flows now need an explicit `w`.
- **Flow width.** `w` does not narrow a flow. With decision diamonds, the idle and direct flows are still slightly wider than a 390-px column, and the app shows its "Scroll sideways" hint.
- **Wide formulas.** Display formulas overflow at 390 px unless very short. I shortened them or added `\qquad` break points.
- **Colour lint.** Flow start nodes are drawn sky blue (e) by default. So a caption that says "blue" gets the blue/sky-blue warning even when only the end boxes are blue.
- **Phone screenshots.** `shot.sh` at 390 px only captures from the top, so I took 16,000-px-tall shots and cut them into slices.
- **Preview file names.** During the job, `preview_lesson.py` started naming dark slices `-dark-N.png`.
- **Others' errors.** `build.py --check` briefly failed on someone else's lesson (fm.coc.wacc). That was not mine.
- **Wrong diagram counts on the dashboard.** My progress notes said 5 diagrams for payroll and 3 for direct; the real counts are 4 and 2.
