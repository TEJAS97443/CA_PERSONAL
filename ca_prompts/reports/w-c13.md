## Facts I am less than certain about (with the page checked)

1. **Price-variance basis.** SM 13.15 says AQ for the price variance is the quantity purchased. But SM Practical Problem 6 (Novell, answer
   SM 13.74) computes MPV on the 19,000 units consumed although 20,000 were purchased, and Problem 13 (SM 13.80–13.81) uses consumption
   with purchases only in a note (whose formula line even says "Standard cost of actual material used – Actual cost of actual material used"
   while using purchase figures). MTP Jan 2026 Series II Q5(a) asks explicitly for the consumption basis; RTP Sep 2025 CS1 uses purchases.
   The lesson teaches: purchases unless the question says otherwise, state the basis, reconcile.
2. **Labour efficiency on hours worked.** SM 13.26's middle formula carries the "AH*" (hours paid) marker, but the bracket form uses "AH#"
   (worked) and the words say "Actual Hours worked"; Illustrations 6–7 and the Sep 2024 Q4(a) suggested answer use hours worked. I used
   hours worked. RTP Sep 2024 Case Scenario 2's answer takes efficiency on the 30,000 hours paid while also finding 2,000 idle hours
   (₹25,000 A instead of ₹15,000 A); the lesson flags it as a trap.
3. **MTP Jan 2026 Series I Q3(a)** ("usage variance adjusted for abnormal loss"): ICAI's answer takes 400 kg out of AQ for usage but not
   for MCV, so MPV + MUV ≠ MCV by ₹32,000. I only mention the instruction, not a method.
4. **Normal idle time in standards.** SM 3.13 says the allowance for normal idle time is built into standard hours or rate; MTP Sep 2024
   Series II (Hilfy) multiplies SH by 0.95 for a 5% expected idle time. Not taught beyond the SM sentence.
5. **"Expected standard".** SM MCQ 4 (13.55, key 13.67) answers "expected standard" for cost control; SM 13.52 says attainable good
   performance is most effective. I state both without claiming they are defined as the same thing.
6. **Short-cut mix formula** (total AQ × difference of average standard prices) comes from ICAI's RTP Jan 2026 Q16 answer, not the SM text;
   yield by total input × standard cost per unit of input is the SM's own (13.71); the output-side yield is my check (Illustration 4 WN 3
   computes the ₹4 standard cost per kg of output).
7. Inferences, not SM sentences: idle time variance "nil or adverse"; yield variance responsibility = production department (as part of
   usage, SM 13.16); "the personnel manager cannot negotiate below the law" in the setting case answer.
8. The "I Never Buy Chai" mnemonic is mine.
