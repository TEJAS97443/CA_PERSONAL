## What got in the way
- **Concurrent subagent limit (20):** I could not fork the lessons out to run in parallel, so all four were written in sequence.
- **Word-cap change mid-job:** the cap moved from 3,600 to 4,500 while I worked. I had cut SA 500 to fit the old cap, then restored the full SM lists (accounting records, types of evidence examples, the management's expert matters, selecting items) and a third example.
- **SA 530 is at the cap:** with every ICAI list kept, the lesson sits at 4,488 words.
- **Word counter:** my own counter differed from the build's by about 20 words until I copied the build's exact body extraction.
- **Diagram rendering, `nest`:** straight arrows between side-by-side boxes, and side arrows, overlapped the boxes. I replaced that diagram with a `seq`.
- **Diagram rendering, `flow`:** back edges (loops) were routed confusingly, so I redrew both affected flows without loops.
- **Diagram rendering, wide trees:** a tree with 5 leaves exceeds 3 nodes side by side. I replaced it with a stacked `nest`.
- **Diagram rendering, plots:** a plot legend overlapped a bar label; fixed with `"legend": "outside"`.
- **Phone width:** flows with a two-way branch at `w` 420 still need sideways scrolling at 390 px (the app shows a "Scroll sideways" hint), as the SA 705 exemplar does.
- **Container nesting:** the linter does not flag an answer that uses more colons than its enclosing example (`:::: example` holding `::::: answer`). I caught and fixed this by hand in SA 530 and SA 520.
- **Dark previews:** `preview_lesson.py --dark` now writes `<id>-dark-N.png`; earlier runs overwrote the light files.
