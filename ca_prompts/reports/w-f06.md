## fm.capbud.cashflows — Capital budgeting and estimating relevant cash flows
- **Study Material pages used:** 7.2–7.17 (sections 1–6, Examples 1–3, Illustration 1); 7.69 (summary); 7.74 (theory questions); 7.95–7.98 (Test Your Knowledge solutions 9 and 10). The formats on PDF pages 10–12 were rendered to PNG and read from the image.
- **ICAI documents read:** pyq-may2024-q/-a (Q3(a)); pyq-sep2025-q/-a (Q1(c)); pyq-jan2025-q/-a (Q3(a)); pyq-sep2026-q (Case Scenario I MCQs 1–5; Q3(a)); mtp-may2024-s1-q/-a (Q2(a)); rtp-jan2025-qa (Q9); mtp-jan2025-s2-q/-a (Q3); mtp-sep2026-s1-q/-a (MCQs 1–5); mtp-sep2026-s2-q (MCQ 6, Q(c)); mtp-sep2024-s1-q/-a (Q3(a)).
- **Examples:** expansion with sunk, allocated, opportunity and financing items (gives an NPV); replacement at ICAI difficulty (tax on loss, incremental depreciation, working capital); block-of-assets kiln (both cases, both sale values).
- **Facts I am less than certain about:**
  - SM 7.12 prints the replacement interim format's last line as "Add/(less): Net decrease (increase) in depreciation charges". I reproduced it word for word and added a line saying the extra depreciation is added back. Please check that wording.
  - Only asset in the block: SM 7.13 says no depreciation in the year of sale. Two ICAI answers did charge it: RTP Jan 2025 Q9 and MTP S1 Sep 2024 Q3(a). They took the loss on the closing WDV. The lesson follows the Study Material and flags the difference.
  - Rent given up: the lesson deducts it before tax. The SM's solution to TYK 10 (SM 7.97) deducts the full rent from PAT. Both treatments are stated in the lesson.
  - In Example 2, the tax saving on the loss from selling the old machine is assumed to arrive in year 0. This follows the Sep 2025 Q1(c) answer, which put tax on the sale at year 0. The Study Material gives no timing.
  - The Sep 2026 paper has no suggested answers yet. The claim that its options "match cash flows before loan interest, ₹220 and ₹184 lakh" is my own calculation.

- **Facts I am less than certain about:**
  - Profitability index for mutually exclusive projects: SM 7.31 says to choose the higher PI. SM 7.52 says the highest PI only "when NPV is same". The SM's illustrations follow NPV (7.59). The lesson teaches NPV first and states all three.
  - MTP S1 Sep 2025 labelled negative equivalent annual NPVs as "EAC" and chose the "lower" one (−7,810 against −14,077). The lesson describes this as the smaller negative equivalent annual figure.
  - RTP Sep 2026 Q7 is cited neutrally: its answer chose Machine I by majority ranking. On NPV and on equivalent annual NPV, Machine II comes first. The equivalent annual NPVs (₹24,108 and ₹28,721) are my own, using standard ICAI table factors (3.605 and 4.111) that the RTP does not print.
  - The NPV-profile curve uses exact discounting, while the text uses 3-decimal table factors (NPV ₹62,300 in the text against ₹62,102 exact). The label "₹0.62 lakh" holds for both.

## What got in the way (guide and tooling)
- **Word limit changed mid-task** (3,600 to 4,500). I had trimmed lesson 1 to fit, then restored ICAI's full lists and the kiln example after the change.
- **Flow labels:** yes/no labels sit on the short vertical stub, so they overlap the line when the next decision is directly below. The SA705 exemplar shows the same thing.
- **Phone view:** the app shows a "Scroll sideways" hint at 390 px even when a flow fits.
- **Plot point labels:** there is no offset option, so a label on y = 0 runs across the gridline.
- **Formulas on phones:** long `\text` fractions scroll at 390 px. I switched to symbols with their meanings written below.
- **Shared /tmp/preview:** other processes deleted my preview slices once; I re-rendered them.
- **Build break from another lesson:** fm.coc.wacc.md (unbalanced fences) briefly made `build.py` fail, so data/ was not written for a phone check. I re-checked after it was fixed.
