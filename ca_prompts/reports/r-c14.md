## Lead's two checks

1. **Scope claim (no sales variances in the January 2027 SM): confirmed.**
   - Ch.13 of the July 2024 edition (reprint Aug 2025) mentions sales variances only in two places. SM 13.12 gives the sign convention ("The situation will be reversed for sales variance") and the revenue/cost classification. SM 13.5 notes that standards are also set for sales quantity and value ("budgeted sales").
   - The chapter has no sales-variance formula, illustration or exercise. Its "Important Formulas" summary (13.53–13.54) covers material, labour, VOH and FOH only.
   - The syllabus line reads "Calculation and Reconciliation of Material Cost, Labour cost, Variable Overhead, Fixed Overhead".
   - A grep of all 110 new-scheme P4 files and 56 old-scheme P3 files finds no sales price, volume, mix or quantity variance, sales margin variance or operating statement. "Operating statement" in SM 15.66 is only a report title for works managers.
   - The lesson's wording ("does not compute named sales variances for your attempt"; revenue lines labelled "effect of volume" and "effect of selling price") is therefore right.
2. **Material price variance basis: recon is now consistent with cma.std.material and the lab.**
   - The rule (SM 13.15) is the quantity purchased, with the quantity used "also correct". Follow the question; if it is silent, use purchases, say so, and give the consumption figure in a note.
   - In a reconciliation, the consumption basis reaches the actual cost of material used. The purchase basis reaches purchases less closing stock at standard price, which is the ALZO RTP's setting.
   - The lab's reconciliation mode uses quantity used, which matches the consumption row.
