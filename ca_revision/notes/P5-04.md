## Structure

Four topic chapters, grouped by logic rather than strict syllabus order (the syllabus puts SA 610 and IFC third and fourth):
1. SA 500 and assertions (4.1, 4.2): what evidence is, and what it must prove.
2. SA 530 and SA 520 (4.5, 4.10): the two most-asked "how" topics (49 and 42 documents).
3. SA 501, 505, 510, 550 (4.6–4.9): special items, outside evidence, opening balances, related parties.
4. SA 610, IFC, audit trail (4.3, 4.4, 4.11): other people's work and controls (15, 13 and 3 documents).
Weighting follows the brief's table: SA 530, SA 500, SA 520 and assertions get the most lines; audit trail only gets its core
idea (3 documents).
