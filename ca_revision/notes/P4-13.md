Every variance is given as a formula in words, "standard minus actual" throughout, with the rule said once (positive = F,
negative = A, F or A after every figure), plus the special readings: idle time is nil or adverse; fixed overhead under-absorption
is adverse; volume is adverse when output falls short of the budget. Checks: MUV = MMV + MYV; MCV = MPV + MUV (purchased equals
consumed); LCV = LRV + ITV + LEV (LRV + LEV with no idle time); LEV = LMV + LYV; VOH cost = expenditure + efficiency; FOH cost =
expenditure + volume; volume = efficiency + capacity + calendar; the bridge closes on the actual profit.
