## In plain words

### Last year's column

Every page of Tapri Tea Co. Ltd's 2025-26 financial statements has two columns of numbers: 31 March 2026 and, beside it, 31 March 2025. Bunty is ticking only the first.

> **Bunty:** Ma'am, the second column was audited last year. Not my problem.
> **Lakshmi:** If that column is wrong, Bunty, every comparison a reader makes this year is wrong.
> **Kesar:** But your opinion is on this year, no? Last year is finished.
> **Lakshmi:** Usually, Kesar-ji. Unless last year's problem never went away.

Last year's amounts shown in this year's statements are **comparative information**. SA 710 says two things about them.

First, the auditor **checks** them: do they agree with last year's statements, and were the same accounting policies used (or were changes properly accounted for and disclosed)?

Second, the auditor **reports** on them in one of two ways, depending on how they are presented. Usually they are **corresponding figures**: part of this year's statements, meant to be read only alongside this year's numbers. Then the opinion is about **this year only**, and mentions last year's figures only in a few situations: last year's report was modified and the problem is still there; a material misstatement in last year's figures has surfaced and has not been dealt with; last year was never audited; or the auditor chooses to refer to a predecessor auditor's report. Sometimes law or the engagement terms require **comparative financial statements** instead: a full second set of statements, and then the opinion covers **each year** shown.

::: analogy The cricket commentary
"Rohit made 82 today; he made 45 in the last match." The commentary is about today's match; the 45 is context, there to be read against the 82. That is a corresponding figure. A season review that rates both matches in full, one by one, is comparative financial statements. And if the 45 itself was misreported, today's commentary must say so: a wrong comparison misleads today's listener.
:::

::: key
Corresponding figures: the opinion refers to the current period only, and mentions the corresponding figures only in the exceptions SA 710 lists. Comparative financial statements: the opinion refers to each period presented. Either way, the auditor audits the comparison.
:::

## How it works

SA 710, "Comparative Information – Corresponding Figures and Comparative Financial Statements", deals with the auditor's responsibilities regarding comparative information in an audit of financial statements. When the financial statements of the prior period were audited by a predecessor auditor or were not audited, the requirements of [[aud.evid.sa510|SA 510]] on opening balances also apply (SM 8.57).

::: definition Comparative information
The amounts and disclosures included in the financial statements in respect of one or more prior periods in accordance with the applicable financial reporting framework (SM 8.58).
:::

### 1. Two approaches, one difference

The nature of the comparative information presented depends on the requirements of the applicable financial reporting framework. There are two broad approaches to the auditor's reporting responsibilities: **corresponding figures** and **comparative financial statements**. The approach is often specified by law or regulation, but may also be specified in the terms of engagement (SM 8.57–8.58).

The essential audit reporting differences (SM 8.58):

- (a) for corresponding figures, the auditor's opinion on the financial statements refers to the **current period only**; whereas
- (b) for comparative financial statements, the auditor's opinion refers to **each period** for which financial statements are presented.

### 2. The objectives

As per SA 710, the objectives of the auditor are (SM 8.58):

- (a) to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence about whether the comparative information included in the financial statements has been presented, in all material respects, in accordance with the requirements for comparative information in the applicable financial reporting framework; and
- (b) to report in accordance with the auditor's reporting responsibilities.

### 3. The audit procedures

These apply under both approaches (SM 8.58–8.59):

::: steps
1. **Required and classified?** The auditor shall determine whether the financial statements include the comparative information required by the applicable financial reporting framework and whether such information is appropriately classified.
2. **Agreement and consistency.** For this purpose, the auditor shall evaluate whether (a) the comparative information agrees with the amounts and other disclosures presented in the prior period; and (b) the accounting policies reflected in the comparative information are consistent with those applied in the current period or, if there have been changes in accounting policies, whether those changes have been properly accounted for and adequately presented and disclosed.
3. **A possible material misstatement.** If the auditor becomes aware of a possible material misstatement in the comparative information while performing the current period audit, the auditor shall perform such additional audit procedures as are necessary to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence to determine whether a material misstatement exists. If the auditor had audited the prior period's financial statements, the auditor shall also follow the relevant requirements of [[aud.complete.sa560|SA 560]].
4. **Written representations.** As required by [[aud.complete.sa580|SA 580]], the auditor shall request written representations for **all periods referred to in the auditor's opinion**, and shall also obtain a specific written representation regarding any **prior period item** that is separately disclosed in the current year's statement of profit and loss.
:::

### 4. Reporting: corresponding figures

::: definition Corresponding figures
Comparative information where amounts and other disclosures for the prior period are included as an integral part of the current period financial statements, and are intended to be read only in relation to the amounts and other disclosures relating to the current period (referred to as "current period figures"). The level of detail presented in the corresponding amounts and disclosures is dictated primarily by its relevance to the current period figures (SM 8.59).
:::

When corresponding figures are presented, ==the auditor's opinion shall not refer to the corresponding figures== except in the following circumstances (SM 8.60–8.61):

**1. Prior report modified, matter unresolved.** If the auditor's report on the prior period, as previously issued, included a qualified opinion, a disclaimer of opinion, or an adverse opinion and the matter which gave rise to the modification is unresolved, the auditor shall **modify the auditor's opinion on the current period's financial statements**. In the Basis for Modification paragraph, the auditor shall either:

- (a) refer to both the current period's figures and the corresponding figures in the description of the matter giving rise to the modification when the effects or possible effects of the matter on the current period's figures are material; or
- (b) in other cases, explain that the audit opinion has been modified because of the effects or possible effects of the unresolved matter on the comparability of the current period's figures and the corresponding figures.

**2. Prior period misstated, previously unmodified.** If the auditor obtains audit evidence that a material misstatement exists in the prior period financial statements on which an unmodified opinion has been previously issued, the auditor shall verify whether the misstatement has been dealt with as required under the applicable financial reporting framework and, if that is not the case, the auditor shall express a **qualified opinion or an adverse opinion** in the auditor's report on the current period financial statements, modified with respect to the corresponding figures included therein.

**3a. Prior period not audited.** If the prior period financial statements were not audited, the auditor shall state in an **Other Matter paragraph** that the corresponding figures are unaudited. Such a statement does not, however, relieve the auditor of the requirement to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence that the opening balances do not contain misstatements that materially affect the current period's financial statements.

**3b. Prior period audited by a predecessor auditor.** If the financial statements of the prior period were audited by a predecessor auditor and the auditor is permitted by law or regulation to refer to the predecessor auditor's report on the corresponding figures and decides to do so, the auditor shall state in an **Other Matter paragraph**: (a) that the financial statements of the prior period were audited by the predecessor auditor; (b) the type of opinion expressed by the predecessor auditor and, if the opinion was modified, the reasons therefor; and (c) the date of that report.

### 5. Reporting: comparative financial statements

::: definition Comparative financial statements
Comparative information where amounts and other disclosures for the prior period are included for comparison with the financial statements of the current period but, if audited, are referred to in the auditor's opinion. The level of information included in those comparative financial statements is comparable with that of the financial statements of the current period (SM 8.61).
:::

- **Opinion on each period.** When comparative financial statements are presented, the auditor's opinion shall refer to each period for which financial statements are presented and on which an audit opinion is expressed (SM 8.61).
- **A different opinion on the prior period.** When reporting on prior period financial statements in connection with the current period's audit, if the auditor's opinion on them differs from the opinion the auditor previously expressed, the auditor shall disclose the substantive reasons for the different opinion in an **Other Matter paragraph** in accordance with [[aud.report.sa706|SA 706]] (SM 8.61).
- **Predecessor auditor.** If the prior period was audited by a predecessor auditor, in addition to expressing an opinion on the current period's financial statements, the auditor shall state in an Other Matter paragraph: (a) that the prior period was audited by a predecessor auditor; (b) the type of opinion expressed by the predecessor and, if modified, the reasons therefor; and (c) the date of that report, unless the predecessor auditor's report on the prior period's financial statements is revised with the financial statements (SM 8.61–8.62).
- **A misstatement the predecessor missed.** If the auditor concludes that a material misstatement exists that affects the prior period financial statements on which the predecessor had reported without modification, the auditor shall communicate the misstatement with the appropriate level of management and those charged with governance and request that the predecessor auditor be informed. If the prior period financial statements are amended, and the predecessor agrees to issue a new auditor's report on the amended financial statements of the prior period, the auditor shall **report only on the current period** (SM 8.62).
- **Prior period not audited.** The auditor shall state in an Other Matter paragraph that the comparative financial statements are unaudited; this does not relieve the auditor of the requirement to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence that the opening balances do not contain misstatements that materially affect the current period's financial statements (SM 8.62).

## Formats and formulas

### When the report mentions last year: the master table

*Reproduce this table from memory: the situation, the reporting and the paragraph carry the marks.*

| Situation | Corresponding figures | Comparative financial statements |
|---|---|---|
| Normal case | Opinion silent on them | Opinion on each period |
| Prior opinion modified, matter unresolved | Modify current opinion; refer to both, or to comparability | No separate rule in the Study Material (see Under the hood) |
| Prior misstatement, prior opinion unmodified | Not dealt with: qualified or adverse, on corresponding figures | (Predecessor's report) communicate; predecessor informed; amended and reissued: report on current period only |
| Prior period not audited | Other Matter: corresponding figures unaudited | Other Matter: comparative statements unaudited |
| Predecessor audited prior period | Other Matter, if law permits and the auditor decides to refer | Other Matter, unless the predecessor's report is revised |
| Auditor's new opinion on prior period differs | Not applicable | Other Matter: substantive reasons |

Both Other Matter paragraphs about a predecessor contain the same three things, in this order: **(a)** the prior period was audited by a predecessor auditor; **(b)** the type of opinion expressed and, if modified, the reasons; **(c)** the date of that report.

### The Basis for Modification: two ways to write it

| Effect on current figures | Basis for Modification paragraph |
|---|---|
| Material | Refers to both the current and the corresponding figures |
| Other cases | Explains the modification is due to the effect on comparability |

## Worked examples

:::: example The trucks that never wear out
Narmada Cold Chain Ltd, a refrigerated-transport supplier to Tapri, has charged no depreciation on its reefer trucks since 1 April 2024, which is a departure from the applicable accounting standards. Iyer & Associates qualified their opinion on the 2024-25 financial statements for this reason. The policy continues in 2025-26. The company presents corresponding figures.

| Depreciation not charged | Amount (₹ crore) |
|---|---|
| 2024-25 | 1.65 |
| 2025-26 | 1.80 |

Overall materiality for 2025-26 is ₹1.20 crore.

Required:

(i) State the reporting responsibility of Iyer & Associates for 2025-26 under SA 710.

(ii) Quantify the matter and draft the Basis for Qualified Opinion paragraph (ignore tax).

::: answer Model answer
**Relevant provision.** As per SA 710, when corresponding figures are presented, the auditor's opinion shall not refer to them except in specified circumstances. If the auditor's report on the prior period, as previously issued, included a qualified opinion and the matter which gave rise to the modification is unresolved, the auditor shall modify the opinion on the current period's financial statements. In the Basis for Modification paragraph the auditor shall refer to both the current period's figures and the corresponding figures when the effects of the matter on the current period's figures are material; in other cases, explain that the opinion has been modified because of the effects on comparability.

**Facts of the case.** The 2024-25 opinion was qualified for non-depreciation; the matter is unresolved; the 2025-26 effect is ₹1.80 crore against materiality of ₹1.20 crore.

**Analysis.** The prior modification is unresolved, so the current opinion must be modified. The effect on the current period's figures (₹1.80 crore) exceeds materiality, 1.5 times over, so it is material; it is confined to depreciation, property, plant and equipment, and profit, so it is not pervasive.

| Effect (before tax) | 2025-26 (₹ crore) | 2024-25 (₹ crore) |
|---|---|---|
| Profit overstated by | 1.80 | 1.65 |
| Property, plant and equipment overstated at year-end by | 3.45 | 1.65 |

*Working:* the year-end overstatement accumulates: 1.65 at 31 March 2025; 1.65 + 1.80 = 3.45 at 31 March 2026.

**Conclusion.** (i) Iyer & Associates should express a **qualified opinion** on the 2025-26 financial statements and, because the effect on the current period's figures is material, refer to **both** the current period's figures and the corresponding figures in the Basis for Qualified Opinion paragraph.

(ii) *Basis for Qualified Opinion:* As stated in Note 2.6, the Company has not provided depreciation on its refrigerated trucks since 1 April 2024, which is a departure from the applicable accounting standards. This matter caused us to qualify our opinion on the financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2025. Had depreciation been provided, the profit for the year would have been lower by ₹1.80 crore for 2025-26 and by ₹1.65 crore for 2024-25, and property, plant and equipment would have been lower by ₹3.45 crore as at 31 March 2026 and by ₹1.65 crore as at 31 March 2025, before tax effects. We believe that the audit evidence we have obtained is sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for our qualified opinion.
:::

:::: example Four second columns
Iyer & Associates audit four entities for 2025-26, all presenting corresponding figures. State, with reasons, the reporting in each.

(a) Indore Spice Exports Ltd: the 2024-25 report was qualified because a term loan of ₹40 crore, repayable in 2028, was shown as a current liability. The 2025-26 balance sheet classifies it correctly, but the corresponding figures have not been restated. The misclassification has no effect on any 2025-26 amount.

(b) Malwa Dairy Ltd: Iyer & Associates issued an unmodified opinion on 2024-25. This year they find that ₹2.10 crore of sales despatched in April 2025 was recorded in March 2025; 2024-25 materiality was ₹1.00 crore.

(c) Mehra Masala Traders, a partnership firm, has its first audit this year; its 2024-25 accounts were never audited. Bunty proposes to write an Other Matter paragraph saying so and, since users are then warned, to skip all work on the opening balances.

(d) Ujjain Kulhad Works Ltd: Iyer & Associates are newly appointed; the 2024-25 statements were audited by CA Meera Joshi, who expressed an unmodified opinion in a report dated 22 May 2025. Law permits a reference to her report, and Iyer & Associates decide to make it.

::: answer Model answer
**(a)** As per SA 710, if the prior period report was qualified and the matter is unresolved, the auditor shall modify the opinion on the current period's financial statements; where the effects on the current period's figures are not material, the Basis for Modification paragraph explains that the opinion has been modified because of the effects of the unresolved matter on the **comparability** of the current period's figures and the corresponding figures (SM 8.60). The matter is unresolved, since the corresponding figures still show the loan as current, and has no effect on 2025-26 amounts. Hence a **qualified opinion**, with the Basis paragraph explaining the effect on comparability.

**(b)** As per SA 710, when evidence shows a material misstatement in prior period financial statements on which an unmodified opinion was issued, the auditor shall verify whether it has been dealt with as required under the applicable financial reporting framework; if not, the auditor shall express a qualified or adverse opinion on the current period's financial statements, modified with respect to the corresponding figures (SM 8.60). As the auditor of the prior period, Iyer & Associates shall also follow the relevant requirements of SA 560, and shall obtain a specific written representation for any prior period item separately disclosed in the current year's statement of profit and loss (SM 8.59). The ₹2.10 crore is more than twice the prior period's materiality. If management deals with it as the framework requires, no modification arises on this account; if not, a **qualified opinion** (the misstatement is confined to revenue, receivables, inventories and profit, so not pervasive), modified with respect to the corresponding figures. The same sales belong in 2025-26 revenue, so the auditor also checks that the current period's figures are not misstated on this account; if they are, that misstatement is evaluated under SA 705 as well.

**(c)** As per SA 710, if the prior period financial statements were not audited, the auditor shall state in an **Other Matter paragraph** that the corresponding figures are unaudited; this does not relieve the auditor of obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence that the opening balances do not contain misstatements that materially affect the current period's financial statements (SM 8.60). The firm's report carries that paragraph, but Bunty's plan to skip the opening balances is wrong: the paragraph does not relieve the auditor of that evidence, which is obtained under [[aud.evid.sa510|SA 510]] (SM 4.87–4.91).

**(d)** As per SA 710, if the prior period was audited by a predecessor auditor and the auditor is permitted by law or regulation to refer to the predecessor's report on the corresponding figures and decides to do so, the auditor shall state in an **Other Matter paragraph** (a) that the prior period was audited by the predecessor auditor; (b) the type of opinion expressed and, if modified, the reasons; and (c) the date of that report (SM 8.60–8.61). The paragraph states that the 2024-25 financial statements were audited by CA Meera Joshi, who expressed an unmodified opinion in her report dated 22 May 2025. SA 510 also applies, as this is an initial audit engagement.
:::

:::: example Two years on one page
Indore Kulhad Exports Ltd's loan agreement with a foreign bank requires it to present comparative financial statements for 2024-25 and 2025-26, and the terms of the audit engagement require an opinion on each.

(i) Iyer & Associates audited 2024-25 and qualified that opinion because inventory was overvalued by ₹1.10 crore. Management has now amended the 2024-25 financial statements to correct the overvaluation, and the firm concludes that the amended 2024-25 statements give a true and fair view.

(ii) Alternatively, suppose 2024-25 had been audited by Bose & Co., who reported without modification, and Iyer & Associates find that the 2024-25 inventory was materially overvalued.

Advise Iyer & Associates on their reporting in each situation.

::: answer Model answer
**Relevant provision.** As per SA 710, when comparative financial statements are presented, the auditor's opinion shall refer to each period for which financial statements are presented and on which an audit opinion is expressed. If the auditor's opinion on the prior period financial statements differs from the opinion previously expressed, the auditor shall disclose the substantive reasons for the different opinion in an Other Matter paragraph in accordance with SA 706. If the prior period was audited by a predecessor auditor, the auditor shall state in an Other Matter paragraph that the predecessor audited it, the type of opinion expressed (with reasons if modified) and the date of that report, unless the predecessor's report is revised with the financial statements. If the auditor concludes that a material misstatement affects prior period financial statements on which the predecessor reported without modification, the auditor shall communicate it with the appropriate level of management and those charged with governance and request that the predecessor be informed; if the prior period statements are amended and the predecessor agrees to issue a new report on them, the auditor shall report only on the current period.

**Facts and analysis.**

(i) The approach is comparative financial statements, set by the engagement terms. The firm's opinion on the amended 2024-25 statements (unmodified) differs from the qualified opinion it issued earlier.

(ii) The predecessor reported without modification on statements that contain a material misstatement.

**Conclusion.**

(i) Iyer & Associates should express an opinion on **each** of 2024-25 and 2025-26, and include an **Other Matter paragraph** giving the substantive reasons for the different opinion on 2024-25: the earlier report was qualified for the ₹1.10 crore overvaluation of inventory, and the statements have since been amended to correct it.

(ii) They should communicate the misstatement to the appropriate level of management and to those charged with governance and request that Bose & Co. be informed. If the 2024-25 statements are amended and Bose & Co. agree to issue a new report on them, Iyer & Associates report **only on 2025-26**. If the statements are not amended, SA 710 in the Study Material gives no further rule; Iyer & Associates express their opinion on 2025-26 with the Other Matter paragraph stating that 2024-25 was audited by Bose & Co., that Bose & Co. expressed an unmodified opinion, and the date of that report. The overvalued closing inventory of 2024-25 is also the opening inventory of 2025-26, so SA 510 applies: if the misstatement materially affects the 2025-26 financial statements and its effect is not properly accounted for or adequately presented and disclosed, they express a qualified or an adverse opinion on 2025-26 (SM 4.91).
:::

## Under the hood

### SA 710 and its neighbours

- **SA 510.** When the prior period was audited by a predecessor or not audited, SA 510's requirements on opening balances also apply (SM 8.57); SA 510 in turn says SA 710 applies when the statements include comparative information (SM 4.87). An Other Matter paragraph saying "unaudited" never replaces the opening-balance evidence.
- **SA 560.** A possible material misstatement in comparative information that the auditor audited last year brings in the relevant requirements of [[aud.complete.sa560|SA 560]] as well (SM 8.59).
- **SA 580.** Representations cover every period the opinion refers to: under comparative financial statements, every period on which the auditor expresses an opinion (SM 8.59).
- **SA 706.** Every "Other Matter paragraph" in SA 710 follows SA 706: a separate section headed "Other Matter" or other appropriate heading, and communication with those charged with governance of the expectation and the wording (SM 8.51).

### A note on ICAI's own answers

In January 2025 Q5(b) and MTP September 2025 Series II Q4(a), the company presents **comparative financial statements** and the prior year's opinion was adverse. ICAI's suggested answers quote the corresponding-figures rule for an unresolved prior modification (refer to both periods, or to comparability) and then the comparative-statement rules (an opinion on each period; the predecessor Other Matter paragraph). If a question of that kind comes, write both parts, as ICAI did.

## Answer frame

### A case question: provision, facts, analysis, conclusion

1. **Relevant provision**: "As per SA 710, …" name the approach, then quote the circumstance the case turns on.
2. **Facts of the case**: which approach the entity uses; last year's opinion; who audited last year; what has been found.
3. **Analysis**: is the matter unresolved? Is the effect on the current period's figures material? Which paragraph?
4. **Conclusion**: the opinion, and the content of the Basis for Modification or Other Matter paragraph.

### "Distinguish between corresponding figures and comparative financial statements"

| Point | Corresponding figures | Comparative financial statements |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Integral part of the current period statements | Included for comparison with the current period statements |
| Read | Only in relation to the current period figures | As a set comparable with the current period |
| Level of detail | Dictated by relevance to the current period figures | Comparable with the current period statements |
| Opinion refers to | Current period only | Each period presented and audited |
| Prior period unaudited | Other Matter: corresponding figures unaudited | Other Matter: comparative statements unaudited |

## Common mistakes

::: mistake Hall of Shame: "last year's problem is last year's auditor's problem"
Bunty issues a clean 2025-26 opinion although last year's qualification is unresolved. **Fix:** an unresolved prior modification requires the current opinion to be modified, with the Basis paragraph referring to both periods or to comparability (SM 8.60).
:::

::: mistake The Emphasis of Matter for unaudited comparatives
**Fix:** that the prior period was not audited is not disclosed in the statements; it is about the audit. The paragraph is an **Other Matter** paragraph (SM 8.60, 8.62).
:::

::: mistake "Unaudited" as an excuse
Writing the Other Matter paragraph and skipping the opening balances. **Fix:** the statement does not relieve the auditor of obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence on opening balances (SM 8.60).
:::

::: mistake One opinion for two years
Under comparative financial statements, giving an opinion "on the financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2026" only. **Fix:** the opinion refers to each period presented and audited (SM 8.61).
:::

## Recap

- Comparative information: prior period amounts and disclosures included under the applicable framework. Approach: set by law or regulation, or by the engagement terms.
- Corresponding figures: opinion on the **current period only**. Comparative financial statements: opinion on **each period** presented.
- Procedures: required and classified; agrees with prior period; policies consistent or changes accounted for and disclosed; possible misstatement: extra procedures (and SA 560 if we audited it); representations for all periods, plus prior period items.
- Corresponding figures exceptions: prior modification unresolved (refer to both, or to comparability); prior misstatement not dealt with (qualified or adverse, on the corresponding figures); unaudited (Other Matter); predecessor referred to (Other Matter).
- Predecessor Other Matter contents: predecessor audited; type of opinion (reasons if modified); date of the report.
- Comparative statements: a different opinion on the prior period: Other Matter with the substantive reasons; predecessor's missed misstatement: tell management and TCWG, ask that the predecessor be told; amended and reissued: report on the current period only.
- "Unaudited" never relieves the opening-balance work. (Today's commentary still checks last match's score.)

## Flashcards

Q:: SA 710: define comparative information.
A:: The amounts and disclosures included in the financial statements in respect of one or more prior periods in accordance with the applicable financial reporting framework.

Q:: SA 710: what are the two broad approaches, and the essential reporting difference?
A:: Corresponding figures (the opinion refers to the current period only) and comparative financial statements (the opinion refers to each period presented).

Q:: SA 710: what are the objectives of the auditor?
A:: (a) To obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence about whether the comparative information is presented, in all material respects, in accordance with the framework's requirements for comparative information; (b) to report in accordance with the auditor's reporting responsibilities.

Q:: SA 710: what two things does the auditor evaluate about comparative information?
A:: (a) Whether it agrees with the amounts and other disclosures presented in the prior period; (b) whether its accounting policies are consistent with the current period's, or changes have been properly accounted for and adequately presented and disclosed.

Q:: SA 710: which written representations are required?
A:: Representations for all periods referred to in the auditor's opinion, and a specific representation on any prior period item separately disclosed in the current year's statement of profit and loss.

Q:: SA 710, corresponding figures: prior report modified and matter unresolved. What does the auditor do?
A:: Modify the current period's opinion; in the Basis paragraph, refer to both the current and corresponding figures if the effects on the current period's figures are material, otherwise explain the modification is due to effects on comparability.

Q:: SA 710, corresponding figures: material misstatement found in prior period statements that received an unmodified opinion?
A:: Verify whether it has been dealt with as the framework requires; if not, express a qualified or adverse opinion on the current period's statements, modified with respect to the corresponding figures.

Q:: SA 710: prior period financial statements not audited?
A:: State in an Other Matter paragraph that the corresponding figures (or comparative statements) are unaudited; this does not relieve the auditor of obtaining sufficient appropriate evidence on opening balances.

Q:: SA 710: what does the Other Matter paragraph state about a predecessor auditor?
A:: (a) That the prior period was audited by a predecessor auditor; (b) the type of opinion expressed and, if modified, the reasons; (c) the date of that report.

Q:: SA 710, comparative financial statements: the auditor's new opinion on the prior period differs from the earlier one?
A:: Disclose the substantive reasons for the different opinion in an Other Matter paragraph in accordance with SA 706.

Q:: SA 710, comparative financial statements: when does the auditor report only on the current period?
A:: When a material misstatement in prior period statements reported on without modification by the predecessor is communicated, the statements are amended, and the predecessor agrees to issue a new report on the amended prior period statements.
