here are saved in `~/CA_PERSONAL/ca_sources/sm/_applicability/pdf/`, and the HTML pages they came from are in `.../_applicability/_html/`.

[For comparison, the same notice gives Paper 3A and 3B as "July, 2025 + RTP for January 2027 + Statutory Update for January 2027".
For Papers 4, 5 and 6 it names no Statutory Update.]

**What I downloaded matches this notice.** Every module's initial pages (page ii) print `Edition : July, 2024  Reprint : August, 2025`.
The covers say "(Relevant for May, 2026 Examination[s])", and the BoS portal labels these sets "Applicable for May 2026 Examination
Onwards". That set is in `sm/<paper>/pdf/` and every `txt/` file and `toc.json` is built from it.

**A newer edition exists but does NOT apply to January 2027.** The BoS portal also lists a set labelled "Applicable for May 2027 Exam
Onwards". Its PDFs print `Edition : July, 2026` and the cover "(Relevant for May, 2027 and onward Examinations)". It exists for P4, P5
and P6B; there is no P6A set yet. I downloaded it for reference only, into `sm/<paper>/pdf/may2027-edition/`, and did not extract it to
text. A word-level comparison is in `sm/<paper>/_edition_diff_may2027.json`:
- P4: mostly the same text. Chapter 1 and Chapter 7 are unchanged. The other chapters gain 0-10 pages of extra material, with no heading changes.
- P5: substantial rework. Ch 3: the IT / automated-environment sections (old §8-15) move after the "Auditor's responses to assessed risks"
  section (old §16, now §8). Ch 4: SA 610 and SA 530 (old §2-3) move later in the chapter. Ch 11: the sub-headings 17A-F (SQC 1
  elements) and 18A-F (SA 220 elements) are restructured, and the chapter goes from 69 to 58 pages. Ch 1, 2, 6 and 7 grow by 8-20 pages.
  Both editions still teach **SQC 1 and SA 220**. Neither mentions SQM 1, SQM 2 or SA 220 (Revised).
- P6B: the same headings, with roughly 4-12 pages added per chapter.

Writers for January 2027 should use the July 2024 edition (reprint August 2025) in `txt/`.

## 2. "Applicability of Standards / Guidance Notes / Legislative Amendments etc." (study guidelines)

**I found no notice of this kind for January 2027 or September 2026.** As of 2026-10-20 the BoS announcements list
(https://boslive.icai.org/bos_announcement.php) has no such entry after the May 2026 one. Guessed file names on boslive.icai.org/assets/
(`List_of_Pronouncement_Sep2026_Exam.pdf`, `..._Jan2027_Exam.pdf` and variants) return 404.

**The newest such notice is for May 2026.** "Applicability of Standards/Guidance Notes/Legislative Amendments etc. for May 2026
Examination (Study Guidelines for May 2026 Examinations)", BoS announcement id 576, posted 06 Mar 2026:
https://boslive.icai.org/assets/List_of_Pronouncement_May2026_Exam.pdf (29 pages; local `pronouncements-may2026.pdf`).

**It says nothing about Intermediate Papers 4, 5, 6A or 6B.** Its "Intermediate Level" part (pp. 1-23) covers only Paper 2 and
Paper 3. The only Paper 2 cut-off it gives is:

> Paper 2: Corporate and Other Laws — The provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 along with
> significant Rules/ Notifications/ Circulars/ Clarification/ Orders issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, and the laws covered
> under Part II: Other Laws, as amended by concerned authority, including significant notifications and circulars issued up to 31st
> October 2025 are applicable for May 2026 examination.

The earlier Intermediate notices are the same in this respect: they also have no Paper 4, 5 or 6 section.
- May 2025: id 461, https://resource.cdn.icai.org/84926bos68375inter.pdf, covers only Paper 2 and Paper 3.
- May 2024: id 310, https://resource.cdn.icai.org/78723bos63001.pdf, covers only Paper 2 and Paper 3.

So ICAI has never published a separate Standards/amendments cut-off for new-scheme Intermediate Paper 5.

For contrast only, here is the Final-level auditing rule from the same May 2026 notice (p. 26). It is stated for **Final Paper 3 AAA**,
not for Intermediate Paper 5:

> Paper 3: Advanced Auditing, Assurance and Professional Ethics — Applicability of the Legislative Amendments for May, 2026 Examination:
> Students are expected to be updated with the notifications, circulars and other legislative amendments made up to 6 months prior to
> the examination. Accordingly, the relevant notified Sections and legislative amendments including relevant Notifications / Circulars /
> Rules / Guidelines issued by Regulating Authority up to October 31st, 2025 will be applicable for May, 2026 Examination.

**Study Guidelines: none have been issued for Papers 4, 5 or 6.** ICAI's Study Guidelines page
(https://www.icai.org/post/bos-study-guidelines-sep2025-exam, and the BoS portal page
https://boslive.icai.org/education_content.php?p=Study%20Guidelines&c=intermediate) lists Intermediate guidelines only for "Paper-3:
Taxation (Section B: Goods and Services Tax)" (Sep 2025, Jan 2026, May 2026). So no topic exclusions are published for Papers 4, 5, 6A
or 6B.

**Statutory Update or Amendments for Paper 5: none.** Checked these pages:
- BoS "Statutory Update" page: https://boslive.icai.org/education_content.php?p=Statutory%20Update&c=intermediate. Lists Paper 3A and
  3B only, including "Paper-3: Taxation (Section A: Income-tax Law) Statutory Update for January 2027 Examination".
- "Amendments/Developments" page: https://boslive.icai.org/education_content_AmendmentsDevelopments.php?p=Amendments/Developments&c=intermediate.
  Lists Paper 2 amendments (Sep 2026, Jan 2027 and earlier) and the Paper 3 statutory updates.

Neither page has anything for Paper 4, 5 or 6.

## 3. Paper 5 Auditing and Ethics: Standards on Auditing, and the cut-off for Companies Act / CARO / other amendments

**Standards in scope.** These are the SAs named in the Paper 5 syllabus printed in the study material (Module 1 initial pages, pp. 6-11,
`sm/p5/txt/00-initial-pages-m1.txt`). They match the ICAI syllabus PDF `~/CA_PERSONAL/ca-firm-notes/syllabus-pdfs/p5.pdf`:

SA 200, SA 210, SA 220, SA 230, SA 260, SA 265, SA 300, SA 315, SA 320, SA 330, SA 450, SA 500, SA 501, SA 505, SA 510, SA 520,
SA 530, SA 550, SA 560, SA 570, SA 580, SA 610, SA 700, SA 701, SA 705, SA 706, SA 710, and SQC 1.

The syllabus asks only for an "Overview and Introduction" of SA 260, SA 265, SA 330, SA 450, SQC 1 and SA 220. The syllabus text also
covers "Reporting requirements under the Companies Act, 2013 including CARO" (Unit 8), and "Branch audit; Joint audit".

The study material also mentions other SAs in passing: SA 240, SA 250, SA 299, SA 540, SA 600 and SA 720 (Revised) appear in Ch 4, 8
and 10, and Ch 8's learning outcomes include "the basics of SA 600" and "SA 299". It teaches **SQC 1** (Ch 1, 6, 11) and **SA 220**,
not SQM 1/SQM 2. CARO 2020 is covered in Ch 8 (pp. 8.73 ff.).

**Standing note in the syllabus.** This is printed in the study material (Module 1 initial pages p. 11) and also in the syllabus PDF:

> Note:
> (i) The specific inclusions/exclusions, in any topic covered in the syllabus, will be effected every year by way of Study Guidelines.
> (ii) If new legislations/ Standards on Auditing/Guidance Notes/Statements are enacted in place of the existing legislations/ Standards
> on Auditing/Guidance Notes/Statements, the syllabus would include the corresponding provisions of such new legislations/ Standards on
> Auditing/Guidance Notes/Statements with effect from a date notified by the Institute. The changes in this regard would also form part
> of Study Guidelines.

**Cut-off date for Companies Act / CARO / other amendments: ICAI has not published one for Intermediate Paper 5 for January 2027.**
There is no Paper 5 Study Guideline, Statutory Update or applicability notice, as section 2 shows. What applies, per the January 2027
notice, is "July, 2024 (Reprint August, 2025 and onwards) + RTP for January 2027".

The nearest official statement covering company-law changes for the same attempt is **Paper 2's "Amendments for January 2027
Examinations"**: https://resource.cdn.icai.org/94282bos-aps5940-p2-amendment-jan2027.pdf (local `p2-amendments-jan2027.pdf`). It is
written for Paper 2, not Paper 5:

> PAPER – 2: CORPORATE AND OTHER LAWS AMENDMENTS FOR JANUARY 2027 EXAMINATIONS
> The Study Material (July 2024 edition-Reprint August 2025) is applicable for January 2027 examinations. This study material is
> updated for all amendments till 31st July, 2025.
> All relevant amendments/ circulars/ notifications etc. in the Company law part for the period 1st May, 2025 to 30th June 2026 are
> mentioned below:

Two of its items touch Paper 5 topics:
- **Small company definition.** "Notification G.S.R 880(E) dated 1st December, 2025": "paid up capital and turnover of the small company
  shall not exceed rupees ten crores and rupees one hundred crores respectively" (previously ₹4 crore / ₹40 crore). Section 2(85)
  feeds the CARO 2020 exemption for small companies. The P5 material cites only "a small company as defined in clause (85) of
  section 2", with no figures.
- **Fraud reporting form.** "Chapter 10: Audit and Auditors — Notification G.S.R 359(E) dated 30th May, 2025", amending rule 13(2) of
  the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014: "(d) the report shall be filed electronically in form ADT-4"; clauses (e) and (f)
  omitted. "[Enforcement Date: Amendments effective from 14th July, 2025]". This concerns the auditor's fraud report under section
  143(12).

[It is my inference, not an ICAI statement, that a careful Paper 5 lesson might mention these. ICAI has not said Paper 5 examines
amendments beyond the study material.]

The September 2026 Paper 2 equivalent (https://resource.cdn.icai.org/91780bos-aps4085-sep2026.pdf) covers "the period 1st May, 2025 to
28th February 2026".

## 4. Papers 4, 6A and 6B

no study guidelines, exclusions, statutory updates or amendment notices for these papers (see section 2).
