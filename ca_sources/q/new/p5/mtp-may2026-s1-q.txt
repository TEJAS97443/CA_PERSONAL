While discussing the audit approach, the engagement partner first finalised the overall audit

The engagement partner explained that the first risk relates to financial statements as a whole, 
whereas the second relates to specific assertions for particular classes of transactions and 
account balances. Accordingly, the team decided to assess the risks at both levels so that 
appropriate further audit procedures could be designed.
