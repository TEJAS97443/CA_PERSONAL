## In plain words

### The report is clean. Lakshmi still wants to say something.

Iyer & Associates have finished the 2025-26 audit of Tapri Tea Co. Ltd, and the opinion is unmodified: the financial statements give a true and fair view. Then, on 18 May 2026, seven weeks after the year-end and twelve days before the report is due, a cyclone flattens Tapri's cold store at Paradeep in Odisha. Note 46 to the financial statements tells the whole story.

> **Kesar:** The opinion is clean, Lakshmi-ji. Why write anything extra? People will panic.
> **Lakshmi:** Not panic, Kesar-ji. Read. Anyone who skips Note 46 misunderstands your year.
> **Bunty:** So we qualify the report, Ma'am?
> **Lakshmi:** Nothing is wrong with the accounts, Bunty. We point at the note. That is all.

SA 706 gives the auditor two ways of pointing, both added to a report whose opinion has already been decided.

An **Emphasis of Matter paragraph** points at something that is **already inside** the financial statements, presented or disclosed properly, but so important that users cannot understand the statements without it. The cyclone in Note 46 is one.

An **Other Matter paragraph** talks about something that is **not in** the financial statements at all, but helps users understand the audit, the auditor's responsibilities or the report: for example, that some branches were audited by other auditors, or that last year's figures were never audited.

Neither paragraph changes the opinion. And neither may be used to dodge a harder job: if the accounts are wrong, the auditor modifies the opinion; a paragraph pointing at the problem is not enough.

::: analogy The fitness certificate
A doctor signs "Fit to play" for a fast bowler and adds two notes. "Please see page 3 of the file: a healed stress fracture." That points at something already in the file, so important that a selector must not miss it. "The blood tests were done by another laboratory; I have relied on its report." That is about the examination, not about the bowler. Neither note changes "Fit to play". The first is an Emphasis of Matter; the second an Other Matter.
:::

::: key
Emphasis of Matter: "look at this, it is already in the financial statements, and it is fundamental". Other Matter: "here is something outside the financial statements about the audit, our responsibilities or this report". Neither modifies the opinion, and an Emphasis of Matter is never a substitute for a modified opinion, a missing disclosure or the going concern section.
:::

## How it works

SA 706 (Revised), "Emphasis of Matter Paragraphs and Other Matter Paragraphs in the Independent Auditor's Report", deals with **additional communication** in the auditor's report when the auditor considers it necessary to (SM 8.47–8.48):

- (a) draw users' attention to a matter or matters **presented or disclosed** in the financial statements that are of such importance that they are **fundamental to users' understanding** of the financial statements; or
- (b) draw users' attention to any matter or matters **other than those presented or disclosed** in the financial statements that are relevant to users' understanding of the audit, the auditor's responsibilities or the auditor's report.

### 1. The objective

As per SA 706 (Revised), the objective of the auditor, ==having formed an opinion on the financial statements==, is to draw users' attention, when in the auditor's judgment it is necessary to do so, by way of clear additional communication in the auditor's report, to (SM 8.48):

- (a) a matter, although appropriately presented or disclosed in the financial statements, that is of such importance that it is fundamental to users' understanding of the financial statements; or
- (b) as appropriate, any other matter that is relevant to users' understanding of the audit, the auditor's responsibilities or the auditor's report.

The opinion comes first ([[aud.report.sa700|SA 700]], or [[aud.report.sa705|SA 705]] when it is modified); the paragraphs come after it and never decide it.

### 2. Two definitions

::: definition Emphasis of Matter paragraph
A paragraph included in the auditor's report that refers to a matter appropriately presented or disclosed in the financial statements that, in the auditor's judgment, is of such importance that it is fundamental to users' understanding of the financial statements (SM 8.48).
:::

::: definition Other Matter paragraph
A paragraph included in the auditor's report that refers to a matter other than those presented or disclosed in the financial statements that, in the auditor's judgment, is relevant to users' understanding of the audit, the auditor's responsibilities or the auditor's report (SM 8.48).
:::

### 3. Emphasis of Matter: when it is included

If the auditor considers it necessary to draw users' attention to a matter presented or disclosed in the financial statements that, in the auditor's judgment, is of such importance that it is fundamental to users' understanding of the financial statements, the auditor **shall** include an Emphasis of Matter paragraph in the auditor's report **provided** (SM 8.49):

- (a) the auditor would not be required to modify the opinion in accordance with SA 705 (Revised) as a result of the matter; and
- (b) when SA 701 applies, the matter has not been determined to be a key audit matter to be communicated in the auditor's report.

### 4. Emphasis of Matter: how it is written

When the auditor includes an Emphasis of Matter paragraph in the auditor's report, the auditor shall (SM 8.49):

- (a) include the paragraph within a **separate section** of the auditor's report with an appropriate heading that includes the term **"Emphasis of Matter"**;
- (b) include in the paragraph a **clear reference** to the matter being emphasized and to where relevant disclosures that fully describe the matter can be found in the financial statements. ==The paragraph shall refer only to information presented or disclosed in the financial statements==; and
- (c) indicate that the auditor's opinion is **not modified** in respect of the matter emphasized.

**Examples** of circumstances where the auditor may consider it necessary to include an Emphasis of Matter paragraph (SM 8.49):

1. An uncertainty relating to the future outcome of exceptional litigation or regulatory action.
2. A significant subsequent event that occurs between the date of the financial statements and the date of the auditor's report.
3. Early application (where permitted) of a new accounting standard that has a material effect on the financial statements.
4. A major catastrophe that has had, or continues to have, a significant effect on the entity's financial position.

### 5. What an Emphasis of Matter is not

The inclusion of an Emphasis of Matter paragraph **does not affect the auditor's opinion**. It is **not a substitute** for (SM 8.50):

- (a) a modified opinion in accordance with SA 705 (Revised) when required by the circumstances of a specific audit engagement;
- (b) disclosures in the financial statements that the applicable financial reporting framework requires management to make, or that are otherwise necessary to achieve fair presentation; or
- (c) reporting in accordance with SA 570 (Revised) when a material uncertainty exists relating to events or conditions that may cast significant doubt on an entity's ability to continue as a going concern.

### 6. Other Matter: when and how

If the auditor considers it necessary to communicate a matter other than those that are presented or disclosed in the financial statements that, in the auditor's judgment, is relevant to users' understanding of the audit, the auditor's responsibilities or the auditor's report, the auditor shall include an Other Matter paragraph, **provided** (SM 8.51):

- (a) this is not prohibited by law or regulation; and
- (b) when SA 701 applies, the matter has not been determined to be a key audit matter to be communicated in the auditor's report.

The auditor shall include the paragraph within a **separate section** with the heading **"Other Matter,"** or other appropriate heading (SM 8.51).

### 7. Tell those charged with governance first

If the auditor expects to include an Emphasis of Matter or an Other Matter paragraph in the auditor's report, the auditor shall communicate with **those charged with governance** (the board or its audit committee: the people who oversee the entity's financial reporting) regarding this expectation and the wording of this paragraph (SM 8.51).

## Formats and formulas

### The two drafts, word for word

*Reproduce these from memory: the heading, the reference to the note and the last sentence each carry marks.*

**Emphasis of Matter** (ICAI's draft in the Study Material, SM 8.50):

> **Emphasis of Matter – [what the matter is]**
>
> We draw attention to Note [X] of the financial statements, which describes [the matter]. Our opinion is not modified in respect of this matter.

**Other Matter** (ICAI's illustrative company report, for branches audited by branch auditors, SM 8.30):

> **Other Matter**
>
> We did not audit the financial statements/information of [number] branches included in the standalone financial statements of the Company whose financial statements/financial information reflect total assets of ₹[…] as at 31st March 20XX and the total revenue of ₹[…] for the year ended on that date, as considered in the standalone financial statements/information of these branches have been audited by the branch auditors whose reports have been furnished to us, and our opinion in so far as it relates to the amounts and disclosures included in respect of branches, is based solely on the report of such branch auditors.
>
> Our opinion is not modified in respect of these matters.

The Other Matter text is as printed in the Study Material. In ICAI's illustration it sits after the Auditor's Responsibilities section and before "Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements" (SM 8.30–8.31); the branch side of it is in [[aud.report.branch|branch audit and joint audit]].

### Emphasis of Matter or Other Matter: the side-by-side

| Point | Emphasis of Matter | Other Matter |
|---|---|---|
| Refers to | A matter presented or disclosed in the financial statements | A matter other than those presented or disclosed |
| Test | Fundamental to users' understanding of the financial statements | Relevant to users' understanding of the audit, the auditor's responsibilities or the report |
| Provided | No SA 705 modification is required for it; not a key audit matter | Not prohibited by law or regulation; not a key audit matter |
| Heading | Includes the term "Emphasis of Matter" | "Other Matter", or other appropriate heading |
| Content | Clear reference to the matter and to the note; only information in the financial statements; opinion not modified | The matter, in a separate section |

("Not a key audit matter" applies when SA 701 applies.)

## Worked examples

:::: example The cyclone, Bunty's creative draft and Kesar's two requests
Iyer & Associates are the statutory auditors of Tapri Tea Co. Ltd, a listed company, for the year ended 31 March 2026 and have concluded that an unmodified opinion is appropriate. Note 46 to the financial statements describes the Paradeep cyclone of 18 May 2026 as an event after the balance sheet date: building and equipment with a carrying amount of ₹4.35 crore and inventory of ₹2.45 crore were destroyed, an insurance claim has been lodged, and its outcome is uncertain. The report is to be signed on 30 May 2026. Lakshmi judges the matter fundamental to users' understanding of the financial statements. She has considered it under SA 701 and has not determined it to be a key audit matter, and she asks Bunty to draft an Emphasis of Matter paragraph.

Bunty's draft reads:

> **Qualified Opinion – Cyclone:** We draw attention to the cyclone. From news reports, we estimate that the loss will exceed ₹9 crore, and the insurer is likely to reject the claim.

Kesar makes two requests: (1) add the line "The cold store was fully insured"; and (2) "Tapri is listed, so the report has a Key Audit Matters section anyway. Describe the cyclone there and drop the separate paragraph."

Required:

(i) Examine Bunty's draft with reference to SA 706 (Revised) and redraft the paragraph.

(ii) Comment on each of Kesar's requests.

(iii) State what Iyer & Associates must do before issuing the report.

::: answer Model answer
**Relevant provision.** As per SA 706 (Revised), if the auditor considers it necessary to draw users' attention to a matter presented or disclosed in the financial statements that, in the auditor's judgment, is of such importance that it is fundamental to users' understanding of the financial statements, the auditor shall include an Emphasis of Matter paragraph, provided the auditor would not be required to modify the opinion in accordance with SA 705 (Revised) as a result of the matter and, when SA 701 applies, the matter has not been determined to be a key audit matter. A significant subsequent event between the date of the financial statements and the date of the auditor's report, and a major catastrophe with a significant effect on the entity's financial position, are examples of such matters. The auditor shall include the paragraph within a separate section with a heading that includes the term "Emphasis of Matter"; include a clear reference to the matter and to where the relevant disclosures can be found in the financial statements, referring only to information presented or disclosed in the financial statements; and indicate that the opinion is not modified in respect of the matter (SM 8.49). Under SA 701, the auditor determines the key audit matters, from the matters communicated with those charged with governance, as those that required significant auditor attention in performing the audit and were of most significance in the audit of the current period (SM 8.52–8.53). The auditor shall communicate with those charged with governance the expectation of including the paragraph and its wording (SM 8.51).

**Facts of the case.** The cyclone of 18 May 2026 falls between the balance sheet date (31 March 2026) and the report date (30 May 2026); Note 46 discloses it, with ₹6.80 crore of assets destroyed and an insurance claim of uncertain outcome; no modification is required; the auditor has not determined it to be a key audit matter.

**Analysis.**

(i) Bunty's draft fails on four counts: the heading says "Qualified Opinion", although the opinion is unmodified and the heading must include the term "Emphasis of Matter"; it does not refer to Note 46, where the disclosures are found; the ₹9 crore estimate and the view on the insurer are not in the financial statements, and the paragraph may refer only to information presented or disclosed there; and it does not state that the opinion is not modified in respect of the matter.

(ii) **Request 1.** Note 46 says that the outcome of the claim is uncertain, and "fully insured" is not information presented or disclosed in the financial statements, so the paragraph cannot carry it. Any further information is management's to disclose in the notes, not the auditor's to add. **Request 2.** Key audit matters are determined by the auditor, in the auditor's professional judgment, not chosen by management. Lakshmi has not determined the cyclone to be a key audit matter, so it does not belong in the Key Audit Matters section; and because it is not one, the second proviso for an Emphasis of Matter paragraph is met. Had she determined it to be a key audit matter, it would have been described in that section and not given an Emphasis of Matter paragraph as well. The separate Emphasis of Matter section therefore stays.

(iii) Before the report is issued, Iyer & Associates must communicate with those charged with governance (Tapri's audit committee) the expectation of including the paragraph and its wording.

**Conclusion.** Iyer & Associates should include the following, in a separate section, with the opinion unmodified:

> **Emphasis of Matter – Effects of the Cyclone at the Paradeep Cold Store**
>
> We draw attention to Note 46 of the financial statements, which describes the effects of a cyclone that destroyed the Company's cold store at Paradeep, Odisha, after the balance sheet date. Our opinion is not modified in respect of this matter.
:::

:::: example Lakshmi's review list
Bunty has drafted the reporting for four engagements. State, with reasons, the correct reporting in each.

(a) Morena Clay Products Ltd, an unlisted company that does not communicate key audit matters, has lost its largest customer and breached a covenant on its term loan. The auditor has concluded that the going concern basis of accounting is appropriate but that a material uncertainty exists, and Note 31 discloses it adequately. Bunty has drafted an unmodified opinion followed by a paragraph headed "Emphasis of Matter – Going Concern".

(b) For Tapri Tea Co. Ltd, a GST demand of ₹28 crore under appeal required significant auditor attention and has been determined to be a key audit matter. Bunty has described it under Key Audit Matters and again in an Emphasis of Matter paragraph, "to be safe".

(c) Masala Mills Ltd refuses to disclose a corporate guarantee of ₹15 crore given for a related company's bank loan, a disclosure its financial reporting framework requires. Bunty plans an unmodified opinion with an Emphasis of Matter paragraph describing the guarantee.

(d) Mehra Masala Traders, a partnership firm, is audited for the first time for 2025-26 at its bank's request. The 2024-25 figures shown as corresponding figures were never audited. Bunty has said nothing about them.

::: answer Model answer
**(a)** As per SA 570 (Revised), if the going concern basis is appropriate, a material uncertainty exists and adequate disclosure is made, the auditor shall express an unmodified opinion and include a separate section under the heading **"Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern"** to draw attention to the note that discloses the matter and to state that these events or conditions indicate that a material uncertainty exists that may cast significant doubt on the entity's ability to continue as a going concern and that the opinion is not modified in respect of the matter (SM 7.22). An Emphasis of Matter paragraph is not a substitute for reporting in accordance with SA 570 (Revised) (SM 8.50). Bunty's unmodified opinion is right, but the Emphasis of Matter paragraph should be replaced by that section, referring to Note 31.

**(b)** An Emphasis of Matter paragraph is included only if, when SA 701 applies, the matter has **not** been determined to be a key audit matter (SM 8.49). The GST demand is a key audit matter, so it is described **only** in the Key Audit Matters section; the Emphasis of Matter paragraph should be removed.

**(c)** An Emphasis of Matter paragraph is not a substitute for disclosures that the framework requires management to make, and it may refer only to information presented or disclosed in the financial statements (SM 8.49–8.50). The omission is a misstatement relating to the non-disclosure of required information, so the opinion is **modified** under [[aud.report.sa705|SA 705]] (qualified or adverse, depending on pervasiveness). The auditor shall discuss the non-disclosure with those charged with governance, describe the nature of the omitted information in the Basis for Opinion section and, unless prohibited by law or regulation, include the omitted disclosure where practicable and supported by sufficient appropriate audit evidence (SM 8.45).

**(d)** As per [[aud.report.sa710|SA 710]], if the prior period financial statements were not audited, the auditor shall state in an **Other Matter** paragraph that the corresponding figures are unaudited; this does not relieve the auditor of obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence that the opening balances do not contain misstatements that materially affect the current period's financial statements (SM 8.60). Bunty should add the Other Matter paragraph and complete the opening-balance procedures of [[aud.evid.sa510|SA 510]].

In each case where a paragraph is added, the expectation and its wording are communicated first to those charged with governance (SM 8.51).
:::

## Under the hood

### Where other Standards send you to SA 706

Several Standards prescribe an Emphasis of Matter or Other Matter paragraph, and one forbids it:

| Situation | Standard | Paragraph |
|---|---|---|
| Fact found after the report date but before issue; amendment restricted to the subsequent event; new or amended report | SA 560 | Emphasis of Matter or Other Matter: subsequent-events procedures restricted to that amendment (SM 7.8) |
| Statements amended after they were issued; new or amended report | SA 560 | Emphasis of Matter or Other Matter: refers to the note on the reason for amendment and to the earlier report (SM 7.10) |
| Corresponding figures unaudited | SA 710 | Other Matter (SM 8.60) |
| Predecessor's report on the prior period referred to | SA 710 | Other Matter: predecessor audited; type of opinion, with reasons if modified; date (SM 8.60–8.61) |
| Comparative statements: new opinion on the prior period differs from the earlier one | SA 710 | Other Matter: the substantive reasons (SM 8.61) |
| Branches audited by branch auditors | SA 700 illustration | Other Matter (SM 8.30) |
| Material uncertainty on going concern, adequately disclosed | SA 570 | **Not** an Emphasis of Matter: the "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" section (SM 7.22, 8.50) |

See [[aud.complete.sa560|SA 560]], [[aud.complete.sa570|SA 570]] and [[aud.report.sa701|SA 701]] for the neighbouring rules.

### Two smaller corners

- **Documentation.** Findings that could result in a modification to the audit opinion or in the inclusion of an Emphasis of Matter paragraph are among SA 230's examples of significant matters to be documented (SM 6.10).
- **Not for missing evidence.** When sufficient appropriate audit evidence on opening balances cannot be obtained, the answer is a qualified opinion or a disclaimer under SA 705, not an Emphasis of Matter paragraph (SM 4.91; ICAI's MTP July 2021 Series II marked the opposite statement incorrect).

## Answer frame

### A case question: provision, facts, analysis, conclusion

1. **Relevant provision**: "As per SA 706 (Revised), …" the definition or the "provided" conditions, whichever the case turns on.
2. **Facts of the case**: what is disclosed, where (which note), and whether any modification is needed.
3. **Analysis**: inside the statements (Emphasis of Matter) or outside (Other Matter)? A key audit matter? A modification, a missing disclosure or going concern in disguise?
4. **Conclusion**: name the paragraph; draft it in ICAI's format; add the communication with those charged with governance.

### "Distinguish between a key audit matter and an Emphasis of Matter"

Use the table in [[aud.report.sa701|SA 701]]; add one line that carries marks: a matter determined to be a key audit matter is not also given an Emphasis of Matter paragraph (SM 8.49).

### Three answers to "Define an Emphasis of Matter paragraph and state how it is presented"

::: cols
**Loses marks**

"A paragraph to highlight important things in the report."

No definition, no "fundamental", no requirements.
---
**Passes**

The definition in ICAI's words, and "a separate section, heading Emphasis of Matter".

Two of the three requirements missing.
---
**Full marks**

The definition; the three requirements in order (separate section and heading; clear reference, only information in the statements; opinion not modified); the two provisos if the question is a case.
:::

## Common mistakes

::: mistake Hall of Shame: the Emphasis of Matter that tried to be a qualification
Bunty finds an unprovided liability, keeps the opinion clean and "emphasises" it. **Fix:** an Emphasis of Matter is included only if no modification is required, and it is not a substitute for one (SM 8.49–8.50). Modify under SA 705.
:::

::: mistake The paragraph that told users something the notes do not
Adding the auditor's own estimates, newspaper reports or management's reassurances. **Fix:** the Emphasis of Matter paragraph shall refer only to information presented or disclosed in the financial statements (SM 8.49).
:::

::: mistake Going concern dressed up as Emphasis of Matter
**Fix:** an adequately disclosed material uncertainty goes in the separate "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" section; an Emphasis of Matter is not a substitute for SA 570 reporting (SM 7.22, 8.50).
:::

::: mistake Mixing up the two
Writing an Other Matter paragraph about a disclosed fire, or an Emphasis of Matter about unaudited corresponding figures. **Fix:** ask one question: is the matter presented or disclosed in the financial statements? Yes: Emphasis of Matter. No: Other Matter.
:::

- [ ] State that the opinion is not modified in respect of the matter
- [x] Add the auditor's own estimate of the matter's financial effect where the notes give none

Q: An Emphasis of Matter paragraph is not a substitute for which of the following? Select all that apply.
- [x] A modified opinion required by the circumstances under SA 705
- [x] Disclosures the applicable financial reporting framework requires management to make
- [x] Reporting under SA 570 when a material uncertainty on going concern exists
- [ ] An Other Matter paragraph about branches audited by branch auditors
> ICAI's list has exactly three items (SM 8.50). The last option is a different paragraph for a different kind of matter, not something an Emphasis of Matter is forbidden to replace.

Q: Case scenario. Nine of the eleven branches of Bhopal Snacks Ltd are audited by branch auditors appointed under the Companies Act, 2013, who have sent their reports to the company's auditor, CA Irfan Qureshi. He has dealt with those reports, will express an unmodified opinion, and wants users to know that his opinion relies on them for those branches. Which of the following is correct?
- [x] An Other Matter paragraph on the branches audited by the branch auditors
- [ ] An Emphasis of Matter paragraph, because the branches' figures are included in the financial statements
- [ ] A qualified opinion, because CA Irfan did not audit those branches himself
- [ ] A Key Audit Matters section listing the procedures the branch auditors performed
> Who audited the branches is not presented or disclosed in the financial statements; it is relevant to users' understanding of the audit, which is the Other Matter test. ICAI's illustrative report carries exactly this Other Matter paragraph (number of branches, their total assets and total revenue, the opinion on them based solely on the branch auditors' reports) with an unmodified opinion (SM 8.30), so using branch auditors' reports is not by itself a reason to qualify. Key audit matters are the matters of most significance in the audit, not a note on who audited what.

## Recap

- Emphasis of Matter: refers to a matter **appropriately presented or disclosed** in the financial statements that is **fundamental to users' understanding**.
- Included provided no SA 705 modification is required for it and, when SA 701 applies, it is not a key audit matter.
- Written in a separate section headed with "Emphasis of Matter"; clear reference to the matter and the note; only information in the statements; "our opinion is not modified".
- Examples: exceptional litigation or regulatory action; significant event after the date of the financial statements and before the report; early application of a new standard; major catastrophe.
- Not a substitute for a modified opinion, required disclosures, or SA 570's going concern section.
- Other Matter: a matter **not** presented or disclosed, relevant to understanding the audit, the auditor's responsibilities or the report; provided law does not prohibit it and it is not a key audit matter; heading "Other Matter".
- Tell those charged with governance the expectation and the wording first. (The bowler stays fit to play.)

## Flashcards

Q:: SA 706: what is an Emphasis of Matter paragraph?
A:: A paragraph in the auditor's report that refers to a matter appropriately presented or disclosed in the financial statements that, in the auditor's judgment, is of such importance that it is fundamental to users' understanding of the financial statements.

Q:: SA 706: what is an Other Matter paragraph?
A:: A paragraph in the auditor's report that refers to a matter other than those presented or disclosed in the financial statements that, in the auditor's judgment, is relevant to users' understanding of the audit, the auditor's responsibilities or the auditor's report.

Q:: SA 706: what is the objective of the auditor?
A:: Having formed an opinion, to draw users' attention, when necessary, by clear additional communication in the report, to (a) a matter appropriately presented or disclosed that is fundamental to users' understanding, or (b) any other matter relevant to users' understanding of the audit, the auditor's responsibilities or the report.

Q:: SA 706: an Emphasis of Matter paragraph is included provided what two conditions are met?
A:: (a) The auditor would not be required to modify the opinion under SA 705 as a result of the matter; and (b) when SA 701 applies, the matter has not been determined to be a key audit matter.

Q:: SA 706: what three things shall the auditor do when including an Emphasis of Matter paragraph?
A:: (a) Put it in a separate section with a heading that includes "Emphasis of Matter"; (b) refer clearly to the matter and to where the disclosures are in the financial statements, referring only to information in them; (c) state that the opinion is not modified in respect of the matter.

Q:: SA 706: four examples of circumstances for an Emphasis of Matter paragraph?
A:: Uncertainty about the outcome of exceptional litigation or regulatory action; a significant subsequent event between the date of the financial statements and the report date; early application of a new accounting standard with a material effect; a major catastrophe with a significant effect on the financial position.

Q:: SA 706: an Emphasis of Matter is not a substitute for what three things?
A:: A modified opinion required under SA 705; disclosures the framework requires or that are necessary for fair presentation; reporting under SA 570 when a material uncertainty on going concern exists.

Q:: SA 706: an Other Matter paragraph is included provided what?
A:: (a) It is not prohibited by law or regulation; and (b) when SA 701 applies, the matter has not been determined to be a key audit matter.

Q:: SA 706: what heading does an Other Matter paragraph carry?
A:: "Other Matter", or other appropriate heading, in a separate section of the report.

Q:: SA 706: what must be communicated to those charged with governance?
A:: If the auditor expects to include an Emphasis of Matter or Other Matter paragraph, the expectation and the wording of the paragraph.

Q:: SA 706: draft the Emphasis of Matter paragraph for a fire described in Note Y.
A:: "Emphasis of Matter – Effects of Fire in the Company's Factory. We draw attention to Note Y of the financial statements, which describes the effects of a fire in the Company's factory. Our opinion is not modified in respect of this matter."
