# CAST.md: the PassBook world, one name per entity

The shared cast is defined in `docs/CONTENT_GUIDE.md` §2a. This file is the canonical list of every entity that recurs across
lessons (or that is easily confused with one that does), with the facts lessons must not contradict. It was compiled by the
consistency pass of 2026-10-20 from all 181 lessons. **Rule: one name, one entity.** A lesson that needs a different kind of
entity (unlisted, a partnership, loss-making, liquidated) invents a new name rather than changing the facts of a name below.
Figures (sales, profits, share counts) may differ between self-contained examples; identity facts (what it is, who owns or runs
it, legal form, listed or not) may not, and nor may a figure a lesson says it takes from another lesson.

## 1. Tapri Tea Co. Ltd (the company)

| Fact | Canon |
|---|---|
| Legal form, listing | Public company, listed on NSE and BSE since 2024 (so SA 701 KAMs, CARO 2020 and IFC reporting apply) |
| Financial year | Ends 31 March |
| Home | Indore (the original roadside tapri) |
| Footprint | Kulhad cafés in **40 cities**, about **300 cafés** in all (sm.impl.digital, sm.intro.meaning); Audit lessons sample its **40 flagship cafés** (aud.evid.sa520, aud.evid.sa530). Bigger city clusters: Indore and Bhopal 40 cafés, Delhi NCR 60, Pune 30, Bengaluru 30. Cafés in 25 cities are franchised |
| Businesses | Packaged chai premix (plant at Dewas), kulhad cafés, a samosa factory (frozen snacks), a cold-chain fleet, the TapriNow delivery app, Tapri Express railway-station kiosks, highway cafés at petrol pumps |
| Statutory auditors | Iyer & Associates (engagement partner Ms. Lakshmi Iyer) |
| Main bank | Narmada Bank of India, Indore Main Branch (current account, working capital limits, term loans) |
| 2025-26 audit | Overall materiality **₹1.50 crore** (aud.risk.sa320 derives it: 5% of normalised profit before tax of ₹30.00 crore, the average of 2022-23 to 2024-25: ₹30.40, ₹31.60 and ₹28.00 crore). Every Audit lesson that states Tapri's 2025-26 materiality uses ₹1.50 crore; aud.complete.sa450 uses ₹1.60 crore (planning) and ₹1.40 crore (actual) for **2024-25** |
| Revenue 2025-26 | ₹900 crore (fm.ratio.liquidity / activity / profit / prepare; aud.risk.sa320 draft; aud.items.revenue) |
| Delivery app orders 2025-26 | About 31 lakh orders, app revenue ₹84 crore (aud.risk.model, aud.nature.sa200, aud.report.sa701) |

Café revenue 2025-26: ₹260 crore (sm.choice.portfolio, sm.intro.intent); the highway cafés at petrol pumps earned ₹48 crore from 160 cafés (aud.nature.assurance).

Known scale difference, not reconciled: the FM ratio lessons give Tapri's 2025-26 profit after tax as ₹90 crore, while the Audit
lessons work with a profit before tax of about ₹30 crore (the materiality base); and FM's café subsidiary (Tapri Kulhad Cafés Ltd, fm.lev.leverage) sells ₹50 crore a year against SM's ₹260 crore of café revenue. Each set is self-contained; do not quote one in the other.
