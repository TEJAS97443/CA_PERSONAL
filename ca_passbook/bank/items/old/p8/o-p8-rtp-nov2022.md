#### Question
Bounce Ltd. evaluates all its capital projects using discounting rate of 15%. Its capital structure consists of equity share capital, retained earnings, bank term loan and debentures redeemable at par.

Rate of interest on bank term loan is 1.5 times that of debenture. Remaining tenure of debenture and bank loan is 3 years and 5 years respectively. Book value of equity share capital, retained earnings and bank loan is ₹ 10,00,000, ₹ 15,00,000 and ₹ 10,00,000 respectively. Debentures which are having book value of ₹ 15,00,000 are currently trading at ₹ 97 per debenture. The ongoing P/E multiple for the shares of the company stands at 5. You are required to CALCULATE the rate of interest on bank loan and debentures if tax rate applicable is 25%.
#### Answer
Let the rate of Interest on debenture be x

∴ Rate of Interest on loan = 1.5x

∴ $K_d$ on debentures = $\dfrac{\text{Int (1-t)} + \dfrac{\text{RV-NP}}{\text{n}}}{\dfrac{\text{RV+ NP}}{2}}$

= $\dfrac{100x(1-0.25) + \dfrac{100-97}{3}}{\dfrac{100+97}{2}}$

= $\dfrac{75x+1}{98.5}$

∴ $K_d$ on bank loan = 1.5x (1 – 0.25) = 1.125x

$K_e = \dfrac{EPS}{MPS} = \dfrac{1}{MPS/EPS} = \dfrac{1}{P/E} = \dfrac{1}{5} = 0.2$

$K_Y = K_e = 0.2$

**Computation of WACC**

| Capital | Amount (₹) | Weights | Cost | Product |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Equity | 10,00,000 | 0.2 | 0.2 | 0.04 |
| Reserves | 15,00,000 | 0.3 | 0.2 | 0.06 |
| Debentures | 15,00,000 | 0.3 | (75x+1)/98.5 | (22.5x + 0.3)/98.5 |
| Bank Loan | 10,00,000 | 0.2 | 1.125x | 0.225x |
| | 50,00,000 | 1 | | 0.1 + 0.225x + $\dfrac{22.5x+0.3}{98.5}$ |

WACC = 15%

∴ 0.1 + 0.225x + $\dfrac{22.5x}{98.5}$ + $\dfrac{0.3}{98.5}$ = 0.15

∴ 9.85+22.1625x+22.5x+0.3 = (0.15) (98.5)

∴ 44.6625x = 14.775 – 9.85 – 0.3

∴ 44.6625x = 4.625

∴ x = $\dfrac{4.625}{44.6625}$

∴ x = 10.36 %

∴ Rate of interest on debenture = x = 10.36%

Rate of interest on Bank loan = 1.5x = (1.5) (10.36%) = 15.54%.
#### Approach
Let the debenture rate be x: the bank loan's cost and the debentures' cost (from their market price and remaining life) are both in terms of x, the equity cost comes from the P/E multiple, and the book-value WACC is set equal to the 15% discount rate.

#### Answer
**Calculation of Expected Cash Flow, Standard Deviation & Co-efficient of variation**

(a) Project X

| Probability (P) | Cash Flows (x) | P.x | P.x² |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.20 | 5,00,000 | 1,00,000 | 50,00,00,00,000 |
| 0.30 | 3,00,000 | 90,000 | 27,00,00,00,000 |
| 0.15 | 1,50,000 | 22,500 | 3,37,50,00,000 |

| 0.15 | 10,000 | 1,500 | 1,50,00,000 |
| | | 2,24,000 | 80,89,00,00,000 |

Expected Cash flow = ∑ P.x = 2,24,000 = $\overline{X}$

Standard Deviation = $\sqrt{\sum P.x^2 - \left(\sum P.x\right)^2}$

= $\sqrt{80{,}89{,}00{,}00{,}000 - \left(2{,}24{,}000\right)^2}$

= $\sqrt{30{,}71{,}40{,}00{,}000}$
