# PGBP — Profits and Gains of Business or Profession · visual contract

1920×1080, dark cinematic 2D on skia (`~/edu2d/eng.py`), the same house look as `~/LLD_PERSONAL/parking_lld_video` and
`~/LLD_PERSONAL/chess_lld_video` (read `~/LLD_PERSONAL/parking_lld_video/scenes/ch_l.py` and `ch_p.py` for style and helper idioms).
Audience: CA Intermediate students (Paper 3A, Income-tax Act 1961, A.Y. 2026-27). The narration is final
(`script.py`, `cues.json`, `beats.json`). Every chapter is drawn by hand in `scenes/ch_<k>.py`; there are **no
templates** in this film, so your scene owns the whole frame for its whole span (the spine's `tpl_alpha` is always 0).

The film's idea: **the tax law runs every rupee of the profit and loss account through a machine.** Show money and
paper moving: bills riding a conveyor into scanner gates, amounts rolling up in a computation sheet, stamps slamming
ALLOWED / DISALLOWED, a block-of-assets tank filling and draining, calendars and year bars with due dates. A chapter
that is only labelled boxes appearing is a failure. Make the *problem* visible first (the expense that looks
deductible, the sale that breaks the block), then the rule fixing it.

## How the frame is built (read this first)
The spine `film.py` draws, in order: backdrop + grid → **your chapter scene** → vignette → beat cards → kicker.
- **Never redraw a beat card or a `show=` string.** The spine already shows: the hook banner (top, y < ~250, while a
  hook plays), the NAME IT term card (top-right, x > ~1370, y 100…~400, during `name` beats — keep that corner clear
  then), COMMON MISTAKE / WRITE THIS IN YOUR EXAM / PAUSE AND PREDICT cards along the bottom (y > ~830), the formula
  card (bottom centre), TEST YOURSELF (centre card with a dark wash, during `check` beats — your scene keeps playing
  dimmed behind it; put a small illustration **below** it at y > 780 or leave the scene calm), and the small
  EXAMPLE / WHAT IF? / ANALOGY / THOUGHT EXPERIMENT tag at the top-left (x < 400, y 100–145).
- Keep important content in x ∈ [80, 1840], y ∈ [170, 820] (y ≥ 270 while a hook banner is up).
- Chapter spans: `B.span('k')` → (start, end). The spine cross-fades chapters over ±0.45 s.

## Hard rules
1. **Show, don't list.** First half of each chapter: the situation and the problem animated (Meera's bill, the oven,
   the van sale, the cash payment). Second half: the rule acting on it. Numbers appear exactly when spoken.
2. **One consistent world** (numbers must match the narration everywhere — see "World" below).
3. Timing only from beat ids: `T('i4')`, `TE('i4')`, `W('i4', 'mixer')`, `TA('i10')` (a check's answer), never
   hard-coded seconds. Spoken words are what `W()` matches: amounts are spoken the Indian way, so `₹2,25,000` is the
   words "two lakh twenty five thousand rupees" — match on a distinctive word (`W('i6', 'twenty')`, `W('i6','total')`).
   Section numbers are spoken like "forty ay three" (40A(3)), "one fifteen B ay C" (115BAC), "forty three B" —
   `W()` tries the lexicon respelling, so `W('t4', '40A(3)')` works; still print your important `W()` calls once
   while developing to check they resolve (a miss silently falls back to the beat start).
4. Palette (from scenes.common): `ACC '#7dd3fc'` structure / neutral · `GOLD '#fbbf24'` money amounts, the thing
   being named, totals · `GREEN '#86efac'` allowed / deductible / income added correctly · `RED '#f87171'`
   disallowed / problem / penalty · `VIO '#a78bfa'` law references (section chips) · `DIM '#c9d3ea'` secondary text ·
   `BLUE`, `PINK`, `ORANGE` extra series. Paper is cream `CREAM '#f5efe0'` with dark ink `INK '#1f2430'` (props.py).
5. Legibility: body labels ≥ 26 px, small tags ≥ 22 px, amounts on the computation sheet ≥ 30 px. No collisions.
   Money strings with `rupee(n)` (Indian grouping: `₹2,25,000`) or `inr(n)` (`₹12 lakh`, `₹1.2 crore`).
6. Motion everywhere: no frame static for > ~6 s. Ease everything (`seg`, `win`, `eout`, `back`). Amounts roll up
   (`roll()`), stamps slam (`stamp()` with u), bills slide along conveyors, tank levels slosh.
7. SFX: append `[time, kind]` to your module's `EVENTS` list. Kinds: `whoosh pop blip chime chime_lo ok error warn
   tick tap thunk snip flip key spark`. ≤ 25 per chapter. `thunk` on a stamp, `error` on a disallowance, `ok`/`chime`
   on an allowance or a final total, `tick` on a calendar day / digit roll, `flip` on paper, `key` on a lock.
8. Only `eng.py`, `props.py`, `scenes/common.py`, numpy, skia. Chapter-specific helpers go in your own file with a
   leading underscore. **Don't edit shared files** (props.py, common.py, film.py, script.py, beats.json, cues.json,
   SHOTS.md). If a prop is missing something, write a local variant in your file.
9. Tasteful, professional: this is exam prep. Characters are simple flat busts (`person()`), never cartoonish gags.

## World (exact numbers — keep them identical everywhere)
- **Meera's Bakehouse, Pune** — a manufacturer (cakes, bread). Proprietor **Meera** (individual, default regime
  u/s 115BAC). P.Y. 2025-26 = 1 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026, A.Y. 2026-27. Turnover ₹2.4 crore, 12% received in cash at
  the counter → tax audit; audit report by 30 Sep 2026; return due 31 Oct 2026.
- Books: **net profit ₹12,00,000 → PGBP income ₹18,99,000** (optional regime: ₹17,99,000).
- P&M block (15%): opening WDV ₹10,00,000 · **oven ₹4,00,000** put to use 1 Jun 2025 (price ₹3,70,000 + freight
  ₹10,000 + installation ₹20,000) · **mixer ₹2,00,000** put to use 20 Dec 2025 (102 days) · 15% × ₹14,00,000 =
  ₹2,10,000 · 7.5% × ₹2,00,000 = ₹15,000 · total ₹2,25,000 · closing WDV ₹13,75,000.
- Other blocks (ch z): shop building ₹2,00,000 @ 10% = ₹20,000 · furniture ₹2,00,000 @ 10% = ₹20,000 · laptop
  ₹1,00,000 put to use 1 Jan 2026 @ 20% (half of 40%) = ₹20,000 → depreciation as per the Act ₹2,85,000.
- Van block (ch j, a thought experiment): WDV ₹3,00,000 · new van ₹5,00,000 · sales ₹3,60,000 / ₹2,40,000 / ₹9,00,000.
- **Rohan** — Meera's brother: intraday share trader (ch d, loss ₹50,000), later partner in **Meera & Rohan Bakers**
  (ch s): profit ₹17,00,000 before depreciation ₹1,50,000, salary and interest · interest 15% on ₹5,00,000 = ₹75,000,
  allowed 12% = ₹60,000 · book profit ₹14,90,000 · limit 90% × ₹6,00,000 = ₹5,40,000 + 60% × ₹8,90,000 = ₹5,34,000
  = ₹10,74,000 · salary paid ₹40,000 × 2 × 12 = ₹9,60,000 → fully allowed · deed signed 1 July.
- **Kabir** — dentist (ch b, y): gross receipts ₹60 lakh, 3% cash → 44ADA income ₹30 lakh.
- **Ravi** — trucks (ch y): 2 light trucks all year (2 × 12 × ₹7,500 = ₹1,80,000) + 1 heavy 15-ton truck bought
  29 Aug 2025 (Aug–Mar = 8 months × 15 × ₹1,000 = ₹1,20,000) → ₹3,00,000.
- **Anil** — Meera's cousin, cold storage (ch n): ₹1.2 crore spent = land ₹40 lakh + ₹80 lakh deductible u/s 35AD;
  profit ₹30 lakh → loss ₹50 lakh carried forward.
- **Sunil** — grocery (ch x): turnover ₹2,98,50,000, cash ₹14,00,000 (4.69%) → 8% × ₹14,00,000 = ₹1,12,000 +
  6% × ₹2,84,50,000 = ₹17,07,000 → ₹18,19,000.
- The **Assessing Officer** (`person(who='officer')`) appears whenever "the officer" or "the law takes it back".
