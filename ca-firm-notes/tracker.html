Revision tracker

Reading a topic is not knowing it. Evidence is.

Every examinable topic in Group II, in ICAI syllabus order. Click a dot to record how far you have actually got. Status is stored on this machine only. Downgrade a row the moment a past-paper question exposes a gap — that is the whole point of tracking it.

How to use it

The four columns are a ladder, not a checklist. Clicking a column fills everything to its left; clicking a lit column switches it and everything right of it back off.

LevelMeans
ReadYou have read the topic once. Worth almost nothing on its own.
Worked a sumYou did a problem or wrote the format out yourself, with the book shut. For theory topics: you wrote the answer, not read it.
Can write it coldBlank paper, no prompt, correct format and correct terminology. For audit, that means the Standard’s own vocabulary.
Past-paper provenYou got it right in an actual ICAI past paper, RTP or MTP question under time, weeks after first reading it.
