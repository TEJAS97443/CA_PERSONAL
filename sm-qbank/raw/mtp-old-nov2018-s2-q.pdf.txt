    (b) Discuss Master Data and Non-Master Data with examples.             (4 Marks)

    (b) Explain the term “Mortgage Plan”. Also, briefly discuss its different types. (4 Marks)
5.  (a) Recognize all the technical exposures that include unauthorized implementation or modification of
        data and software.                                                 (6 Marks)
    (b) List the Risks and Control objectives of Procure to Pay (P2P) business process at Masters level.

        between both.                                                      (6 Marks)
    (b) Explain the term “Audit Trail”?                                    (4 Marks)
